# Alpha disclaimer

ZIGoals Alpha is experimental, unaudited software for local simulation and testnet compatibility testing. It is not investment advice, a savings account or a promise of returns. Simulated and testnet assets have no monetary value. Never use mainnet or real funds during this Alpha.

The public undeployed Alpha lets you create fictional plans and simulate deposits and withdrawals. Keplr connection is optional and does not place funds under management. Goal Manager is not deployed; financial blockchain actions are unavailable. A future deployed-testnet build requires separately reviewed source, configuration and owner authorization.

Habits and Health are manual self-tracking tools. Health information shown or calculated by the Alpha is not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment and should not be used as a substitute for professional medical care. Use fictional or synthetic Habit and Health information when testing the Alpha.

Goal plans, Habits and Health records are stored locally in the browser in the supported Alpha flows; Habit and Health data is not cloud-synced or placed onchain by those flows. Browser-local storage is not encrypted against someone with access to the device or browser profile, hostile extensions or malicious same-origin code. Exported Goal, Habits and Health backups are readable files and can contain sensitive personal information.

Keep private backups secure. Check the actual environment and build identity before testing. Never enter a seed phrase, private key or wallet password into ZIGoals. Report suspected security issues privately to **hello@zigoals.app** and do not include secrets, personal financial information or real health information in a report.
