# Run #10 repository privacy addendum — not a hosted activation claim

The existing deployed Alpha description below is historical. Draft PR20 now contains opt-in email accounts and encrypted sync, but no hosted auth/email/backend or physical-device rollout was performed in this run. Local-only operation and Showcase remain separate; signing in does not silently upload their records.

Account selection is tab-local; the server derives tenant identity from verified authentication. The browser encrypts private domain snapshots before transmission using a random vault root and an independent recovery secret. The server receives ciphertext plus account, vault, record identifiers, sizes, timing and domain metadata; this is not metadata anonymity. Health upload/decryption requires a separate opt-in. Turning Health sync off does not delete prior encrypted copies.

Unlocked keys remain in memory and clear on lock/sign-out/account change/reload, unless the person chooses **Remember on this device** (Session M, [ADR-008](architecture/ADR-008-remember-this-device.md)). Then this browser also stores, in its own IndexedDB database (`zigoals-device-unlock-v1`), the vault root itself as a non-extractable key that can only derive other keys (Session U; builds before it stored a device key and the root sealed with it, and the next open replaces such a record), bound to the verified account, the server session and the live vault manifest, plus whether Health sync was on. Nothing new is sent to the server. Anyone who can use this browser profile can then open the account records here without the recovery secret. "Non-extractable" means no browser call returns the root's bytes: script running in this site (a hostile extension, injected code) can still use the key while ZIGoals runs, including to decrypt records, but cannot copy the root out for later; with the earlier sealed record it could ([ADR-008](architecture/ADR-008-remember-this-device.md), T2). Someone holding the profile's files may still recover it. After losing a remembered device, or after a suspected compromise: revoke its session, then rotate the vault key. Lock (it locks every tab of this browser), Forget this device, sign-out, another account, a new sign-in, key rotation and section or account deletion remove it. A session revoked on another device, or a deleted account, removes it the next time this browser checks with the server: when ZIGoals starts, when its page is shown again or focused, and every 15 minutes while the page stays open and visible (Session AD; the check is the usual sign-in status request, sends nothing new and never runs in the background). A device that stays closed, hidden or offline keeps the record until it next checks (FINDINGS Q-SYNC-05), which is why a lost device also needs a key rotation. The check also needs this browser's sign-in to still be current: once it has lapsed, the record goes only when the vault is next opened or a sync is refused. Local account records, sync bases, pending local operations and recovery copies remain **plaintext at rest** in this browser profile; app locking does not protect against extensions, same-origin compromise or profile access. Losing the separate recovery secret can make cloud records unrecoverable. Email access does not recover it.

Alongside the account records, this browser keeps a queue of the changes made since the last sync, readable like the records themselves; sync removes each entry once its section is uploaded, and the Local Demo, which never syncs, keeps no queue. Small receipts that make a repeated save safe (a section's name, a fingerprint and a revision number, never a record's content) are kept for about the last 1,000 changes of each section. When this device's sync record is set aside (re-linking, starting over, a forward recovery), its last synced copy stays on this device for recovery: at most the 20 most recent per account, plus any that hold changes never uploaded. A session revoked on another device, or a deleted account, also deletes this browser's remembered unlock record the next time the app checks; the records on this device stay. Lock and sign-out hide all of this in the app; anyone with this browser profile can still read it, as they can the records.

The encrypted extras sections keep no queue: each sync uploads a section's whole current state. To combine changes from your other devices, this browser keeps the last synced copy of each extras section (one copy per section, replaced at every sync, readable like the records themselves); the account's erase removes them, and “Remove from the cloud” deletes the encrypted copies for every device.

The isolated backend retains hashed-token session records and user-supplied device labels for access revocation; raw provider tokens remain in protected HttpOnly cookies and are forwarded only server-side. Revocation blocks future backend access at once, not data already downloaded or knowledge of the root secret. Since Session U it also ends the email provider's session: "Revoke other sessions" signs the others out there at once, and a single revoked session is signed out there the next time it reaches ZIGoals. A revoked session's record (its device label, token hash and dates) is deleted 90 days after the revocation; a dateless marker that its sign-in may never come back stays, within the account's lifetime limit of 5,000 sign-ins. Key rotation and account deletion exist (Settings); other operation records stay bounded by fixed capacities. A code request is answered the same way for every address, so the answer does not reveal whether an address is invited.

Encrypted backup downloads are now available separately from legacy plaintext JSON exports, with explicit recovery-secret acknowledgement and optional Health inclusion. Private restore preserves prior local bytes. Since Session AD, a restore's Undo keeps, in this browser only (never synced or exported), a plaintext copy of the device records the restore replaced, until Undo or “Keep the restore” is used, those records change, or Settings → Your data is next opened after its 7 days. Food lookup sends only a confirmed barcode to the same-origin server/provider; scanner frames stay on device. For abuse limits only, the food service counts lookups per client the same way as the market coordinator: a keyed hash of the network address group under a key replaced every UTC day, never the address itself, with day records deleted after about 48 hours; a barcode the provider does not know is remembered for one hour so a repeat costs no provider request. Since Session AJ, when a barcode is not found or has no nutrition values, you can search the Swiss Food Composition Database (Federal Food Safety and Veterinary Office FSVO, `api.webapp.prod.blv.foodcase-services.com`) instead. It receives only the search words you type and the language you choose, sent by ZIGoals' food Worker, never your IP address, cookies or identity. The Worker keeps the search and each food's values for 24 hours (a search with no results for 1 hour), keyed only by the words, the language and the food number, and counts searches per client the same way as barcode lookups. Camera permission is self-only on the Health document, with explicit activation; microphone and location remain denied.

# Alpha privacy notes

This describes the public Alpha at https://alpha.zigoals.app/app and the current repository. See [STATUS.md](STATUS.md) for the current deployed-versus-repository state. ZIGoals has no app account, goal-sync server, advertising tracker or application telemetry. Hosting providers still operate infrastructure logs and aggregate analytics; this document does not promise a particular retention policy or anonymity from the host.

Current tracked Goals, Positions, allocations, contribution plans and snapshots use the browser-origin-wide platform store (`zigoals:platform:v1`). They are not isolated by the active wallet; switching wallets does not hide, move or erase them. Legacy Goal metadata uses network/wallet-specific keys; changing wallets selects different legacy metadata, but does not encrypt or remove the previous records. The local simulation ledger is also browser-origin-wide. This is local storage, **not encryption**: someone with access to the browser profile, a malicious extension or compromised same-origin code may read or change it.

Habits and Health use separate versioned browser-local stores. Habits can contain habit definitions, schedules and targets, descriptions or notes, check-in history and optional links to Goals. Health can contain foods and recipes, meal diary entries, user-entered nutrition or activity targets, weight readings and manually entered activity. These stores belong to this browser origin independently of the active wallet; switching wallets does not hide, move or erase them.

Supported Alpha flows do not cloud-sync Habits or Health and do not place Habit or Health records onchain. Browser-local storage is not a medical or privacy-grade encrypted vault. Device access, browser-profile access, extensions, screenshots, clipboard tools, backups or compromised same-origin code may expose locally stored information.

Testnet transaction history uses a separate IndexedDB journal. It retains public wallet/contract identifiers, the intended action and amounts, local operation IDs, timestamps, signed transaction hashes and observed outcomes. The journal does not store a mnemonic/private key or raw signature bytes. Records can remain uncertain until a matching chain receipt is available. Damaged or unsupported rows are retained with a warning rather than silently erased.

Connecting Keplr requires the owner's approval and exposes the selected public account to the app. Requests to public ZIGChain RPC/REST services may reveal the visitor's IP and queried public account/contract/hash to those operators. Checking diagnostics also contacts those endpoints. Explorer/Hub/provider links open external services with their own practices. ZIGoals does not transfer private plan text, Habit records or Health records to those services through the supported Alpha flows.

The web host receives ordinary page and asset requests, including IP/browser information and request paths. A goal detail path such as `/app/goals/<id>` exposes that identifier to the host even for a simulated goal; the path does not include its name, target, date or notes. Browser history, screenshots, clipboard tools, device backups and extensions may expose additional information independently of ZIGoals. HTTPS and a restrictive CSP do not protect data from compromised permitted origin code or a hostile extension.

Supported market features send selected public market identity information, such as provider asset identity, quote currency and requested history range where applicable (Wealth asks only about the holdings it prices from the market, and the assets you open or search; a manually valued holding is never named in these requests), to the ZIGoals web host/server and from there to CoinGecko as required for the operation. These market requests do not include quantities, allocations, Goal names or IDs, wallet addresses, Habits or Health records. Public asset selections are not necessarily privacy-neutral: their combinations and timing can reveal interests to hosting/provider infrastructure. Excluding holdings and quantities does not make these requests anonymous. For abuse limits only, the market coordinator counts these requests per network address group (an IPv4 address or an IPv6 /48) and day under a pseudonym: it stores a keyed-hash (HMAC) bucket under a key it replaces every UTC day, never the address or group, and deletes the counts after about 48 hours (`workers/market-coordinator/README.md`).

The deployed public web Alpha, whose Goal Manager remains undeployed, supports local simulation and optional connection/read-only checks. A future explicitly deployed-testnet build would send intended messages, public accounts, contract identifiers and amounts to RPC for fee simulation and transaction submission. Those public transaction inputs are separate from private planning fields, Habit data and Health data. No claim about this future path substitutes for the deployment and owner-approval gates.

Positions and Goals, Habits and Health backups are separate readable JSON exports and can contain personal information. The Positions and Goals backup contains private planning, positions, allocations, contribution plans and snapshots. Legacy Goal metadata exports are separate and retain their network/wallet identity. Habits backups can include definitions, schedules, notes and check-in history. Health backups can include foods, recipes, meal history, targets, weight readings and manually entered activity. Store all backups privately and remove personal data before sharing a reproduction. They are not wallet backups and do not include wallet credentials, seed phrases or private keys.

Legacy Goal metadata import applies only to the matching network/wallet and matching IDs replace saved plans after validation. Current Positions and Goals restore validates the platform schema and replaces the browser-origin-wide platform store; it is not restricted to the active wallet. Habits and Health restores validate their own schemas and replace only the selected module, keeping other modules. A restore can preserve previous stored bytes in a local recovery record. Exported backups are not encrypted.

Showcase uses a separate generation-prefixed, tab-scoped sessionStorage dataset selected by a session marker. Supported Showcase flows operate on that demo dataset, not the normal localStorage records; exiting Showcase returns to the normal data. This separation is not encryption or an access-control boundary against same-origin code, extensions or someone with browser-profile access.

Storage is separate for each origin. The Workers fallback hostname and the official Alpha domain do not share plans, simulated balances, Habits, Health data or history. Supported private backups can transfer their corresponding data manually; changing a hostname or rolling back the frontend does not migrate or erase browser data.

Clearing this site's browser data removes private plans, simulated funds, Habits, Health records and local transaction history. It does **not** erase public chain data, revoke Keplr permission or remove funds already held by a deployed contract. Revocation is performed in Keplr. Confirmed onchain messages, amounts, addresses and optional metadata commitments are public and generally cannot be erased by deleting browser data. Withdrawals remain available without private goal metadata once a reviewed deployment exists.

Diagnostics show a shortened account, network status, public configuration and build identity. A screenshot of other screens or an exported file can still reveal private information: crop/redact it before posting. Report privacy or security problems privately to **hello@zigoals.app**; never include secrets or personal financial or health data.

Historical M5 verification used fictional data on the real public origin; [evidence](verification/m5/README.md) records those tested flows and their limits. Cloudflare `cf-nel` network error reporting headers are infrastructure reporting, distinct from ZIGoals application analytics. The app does not add a tracker; this is not a claim that Cloudflare collects no infrastructure data.

The current Alpha returns to Local Demo after reload. A versioned boolean in tab-scoped sessionStorage may remember a previous successful connection only to offer **Reconnect Keplr**. It contains no account, plan, Habit or Health data, grants no capability and causes no automatic wallet call. Explicit Local demo, and Disconnect (Session AE), clear that hint. Keplr's own origin permissions remain separately controlled in the extension.

**Linked wallets** (Session AE, [ADR-027](architecture/ADR-027-session-ae.md)). Public wallet addresses you link read-only, with their labels, the wallet each came from and the wallet card's display choice, are kept in one record on this device only (`zigoals:linked-wallets:v1`, at most 12 addresses). It is never synced, it is part of "Export everything" (a restore never brings it back), and it is removed by **Remove** beside each address, by the account's erase and by clearing this site's data. The Showcase never writes it. A public address can identify you and its history on its chain: like the other device records, anyone with this browser profile can read it. Linking asks a wallet only for its address: a Cosmos wallet gets the same reviewed ZIGChain Testnet details as Keplr and is asked for its address; an EVM wallet is asked for its accounts (`eth_requestAccounts`) and the first is kept; a Bitcoin address you type is checked on this device. Linking itself signs nothing, sends nothing and makes no request of its own; since Session AF the app looks up a linked address's balances, as **Earn, staking and wallets** below describes; the wallet may keep its own permission for this site until you revoke it there. Keplr stays the app's wallet. Today's wallet card has a currency choice (ZIG, USD, EUR, BTC), kept in the same record; the app's own balance is practice money, so in USD, EUR or BTC it reads "unavailable" with the reason, and choosing a currency asks no service for anything. **Disconnect** (beside Connect Keplr) returns this tab to the local demo, clears the reconnect hint and asks Keplr to forget this site's ZIGChain Testnet permission when the extension supports it.

**Earn, staking and wallets** (Session AF, [ADR-028](architecture/ADR-028-session-af.md) H16). Which service sees what:
- **ZIGChain addresses** (yours and the ones you link): balances, stakes, rewards, unbonding, stZIG and your votes are read by ZIGoals' read-only relay (`/api/positions` and `/api/chain/*`) from the official ZIGChain service (`api.zigchain.com` or `testnet-api.zigchain.com`), at one block height. The relay receives the public address and the network you asked about, keeps the answer for about a minute so repeated views do not repeat the request, limits how often one visitor can ask, and writes no address to any log; the ZIGChain service sees ZIGoals' server, not your IP address. One exception (Gate C security review, ADR-028 H133): for a ZIGChain wallet you link, your browser also reads the same key's Cosmos Hub address (`cosmos1…`, the same account written with another prefix) from PublicNode (below), to show what that address holds on Cosmos Hub (ATOM bought through Add money arrives there). PublicNode then sees your IP address with an address it could rewrite into your ZIGChain one.
- **Signing a staking step or a vote:** Keplr holds your keys and signs on your device; your browser then talks to the chain's own RPC service (`rpc.zigchain.com` or `testnet-rpc.zigchain.com`) to check which network it is (its `/status` and `/abci_info` answers), read your account number and sequence, estimate the fee, send the signed transaction and check its result. That service sees your IP address, your public address and the transaction, which is public on the chain anyway. ZIGoals' server never receives the transaction. The review screen says what the transaction will publish before you sign it; nothing personal (goal names, habits, health, notes) is ever part of it.
- **Other chains you link** (Cosmos Hub, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, OP Mainnet, Polygon, BNB Chain, Solana, Bitcoin): when you open Wealth or Portfolio with a wallet linked, your browser asks each address's own chain service directly for its balances: PublicNode (`*.publicnode.com`, run by Allnodes, which says it keeps nothing longer than 24 hours and does not link addresses to IP addresses) and, for Bitcoin, `mempool.space` (which keeps web-server logs for 10 days). Those operators see your IP address and the address asked about; ZIGoals' server never sees those addresses. Solana token balances are read from the token accounts your device works out from the address, so no indexer is asked. The balances last read stay on this device.
- **A Bitcoin wallet watched by its public key (xpub, ypub or zpub; Session AJ):** the key is kept only on this device, in its own browser database (`zigoals-xpub-v1`), never synced, exported, backed up or logged, and erased by Remove, the account erase or "Erase everything on this device". To read the balance, your device works out the wallet's addresses and asks `mempool.space` about each one (when Wealth opens and when you tap Refresh, at most every 5 minutes, stopping after 20 unused addresses in a row). mempool.space therefore sees those addresses, which together show it that they belong to one wallet, and your IP address. ZIGoals' server sees neither the key nor the addresses. A private key is refused before it is read.
- **Prices** come from ZIGoals' market coordinator as before and carry no address or quantity.
- **Governance and permissions:** your own vote on a proposal and the permissions your address has given (authz grants, fee allowances) are read through the same relay with your address, nothing logged. **Practice badges** (practice network only, off unless you switch them on): checking them asks ZIGoals' server to search the practice network's public record for your practice address's badge transactions at Polkachu's public testnet service (`zigchain-testnet-api.polkachu.com`), only when you press "Check them on the chain"; the badge's note and salt stay on this device.
- **The Earn page** reads your figures through the same relay: the wallets you linked and, in this tab, the wallet you connected on the Staking page (the relay already reads that address for Staking); it stores nothing new. Its measured staking rate reads one validator's own public account twice, 10,000 blocks apart. Since Session AH it also shows your OroSwap pool shares and USDC vault shares (`/api/earn/shares`, [ADR-033](architecture/ADR-033-session-ah.md) M37): the relay asks the same official ZIGChain service for one public address's balance of eight fixed tokens, on the real network only, with the same limits, and stores or logs nothing. Your share is worked out on this device.
- **The Earn hub's own numbers** (staking rates, validators, pools, vaults, proposals) and **Ramp Network's public asset list** (assets, minimums and fees for Add money) are fetched by ZIGoals' server from the chain, from DefiLlama and from Ramp's public API, with no personal data; they are cached and shared by every visitor.
- **Add money and ecosystem apps** are ordinary links that open the provider's or the app's own site in a new tab. For a purchase, the link carries the address you chose to receive into and the asset and network you picked, nothing else; ZIGoals sends no referrer, never sees the payment or the identity check, and the provider's own privacy policy applies from there.
- **On this device only** (in "Export everything", never synced in this release, never restored from a backup): linked addresses and their last balances, Goal Vault links, your staking and vote journal, the practice/real choice, the Checklist and its days, Earn path and Academy progress, ZIGi's plans and briefing choices, My Universe and Stories marks, Day Timeline times. Two hand-offs live only in the open tab (sessionStorage, never exported, gone when the tab closes): the connection to the wallet you connected for staking, and a staking form ZIGi filled in for you to review (read once, at most ten minutes old). The staking wallet's public address and the positions read for it are kept in your finance record (`zigoals:platform:v1`), as a watch-only wallet's are, so Today, Wealth and your Goals show them; with an account that record syncs end-to-end encrypted, like the rest of your finances (ADR-028 H63).

**Checklist** (Session AF, [ADR-028](architecture/ADR-028-session-af.md) H10). The Checklist keeps two records on this device only: what is on your path (the habits, goals, steps and health inputs you starred, your own items, their order, sections and last values) in `zigoals:checklist:v1`, and the day history (how many dots were done each day, today's ticks and what each tick wrote, so Undo can put it back) in `zigoals:checklist-days:v1`. Both are part of "Export everything", removed by the account's erase and by clearing this site's data, and never synced or restored from a backup in this release; another device starts with an empty path. The display choices (the launcher, the view, the accent colour, sounds, vibration and nudges) live in this device's display record (`zigoals:ui-prefs:v1`). Ticking a dot writes to the item's own record, through that page's own change (a habit check-in, a health entry, a goal step), and that record keeps its own rules: a habit check-in syncs like any other when you use an account, health entries only with this device's Health consent. The Checklist sends nothing anywhere itself; its sounds are made on the device and nothing is downloaded for them.

**ZIGi's new skills** (Session AF, [ADR-028](architecture/ADR-028-session-af.md) H69). The morning brief and the evening recap are made on this device from your records, without an AI; a brief preference (morning, evening, read aloud) is kept in ZIGi's own device record. Plans you accept are kept in `zigoals:zigi-plans:v1` on this device only (in Export everything, erased with the account, never synced or restored). When you ask ZIGi about "this page", the AI you chose receives the page's name and its cards' titles only, never a value, nothing from Settings, and Health cards only while Health is shared. The Earn coach quotes the figures the Earn page already read, from memory; nothing new is fetched or stored. A Remember card keeps a note only after you agree once (the consent is kept with the notes).

**My Universe, Day Timeline and Stories** (Session AF, ADR-028 H76–H78). Three device-only records: the milestones already celebrated (`zigoals:universe:v1`), the times you moved on the timeline (`zigoals:day-timeline:v1`) and the stories you opened (`zigoals:stories:v1`). Everything else is worked out from your records each time. Nothing is sent anywhere; share images are drawn on this device and carry counts only. In Export everything, erased with the account, never synced or restored.

**Reminders while ZIGoals is closed** (Session P, [ADR-010](architecture/ADR-010-push-reminders.md); off in every build until the owner activates it, [run11/PUSH_ACTIVATION.md](run11/PUSH_ACTIVATION.md)). Push reminders are opt-in per device, in Settings → Reminders when closed, and need a signed-in account; on an iPhone only the app saved to the Home Screen can receive them. When on, the push Worker (`workers/push-reminders/`, one SQLite Durable Object per account) stores: the address the browser hands out for push messages and the two keys that encrypt them; the device's time zone and quiet hours; up to 20 reminder times as times of day with weekdays; a sent mark per reminder and day; a daily send counter and an hourly request counter. Never a habit's name, a count, a kind or anything recorded: every message is the same encrypted `{"v":1}`, and the text, "ZIGoals" / "A reminder from ZIGoals", is fixed in the service worker on the device (`apps/web/public/push-sw.js`, which has no fetch handler and no cache). The platform's push service (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on the browser) delivers the message and sees that one arrived for this device at these times, not what it says. Retention: a device's data stays while the app refreshes it (once a day while used, and after a reminder time changes); a device not refreshed for 30 days is deleted at the Worker's next alarm; marks and counters are pruned after two days. Deletion: "Turn off and delete from the server" and sign-out delete this device; "Revoke other sessions", cloud deletion and account deletion delete the whole account's push data. Until the two app values are set, `/api/push` answers 503 and Settings shows "Not available in this build."

**The Guide** ([ADR-011](architecture/ADR-011-coach.md), opt-in in Settings → Guide on this device) reads your records on this device only, through the same storage as Today, and calls no service: its one device key, `zigoals:guide:v1`, holds the switch and the nudges you dismissed, and nothing is sent anywhere.

**ZIGi · your AI** ([ADR-012](architecture/ADR-012-your-ai.md), Settings → ZIGi · your AI, off until you connect it) is a chat with an AI *you* bring: an API key you hold with OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, xAI or OpenRouter, a model running on your own computer (Ollama, LM Studio), OpenRouter's own sign-in, or, with only a consumer subscription, a prompt you copy into that app yourself. Your message, the current page's data (if that page's switch is on; Health only with its own switch and consent) and your own instructions go from your browser straight to that provider, under its terms, and never through ZIGoals; ZIGoals runs nothing for this on its servers and logs nothing. Keys stay on your device (in the page's memory, or sealed in an encrypted store you can turn off) and are never synced, exported or shown back; chats stay on your device per account and are part of "Export everything". ZIGi reads nothing on Settings or while a sign-in, unlock, recovery-secret or deletion screen is showing, never writes a record by itself (every change is a card you add, with Undo), and can never move money. Voice is optional: your browser's speech recognition (Chrome may send audio to Google unless on-device recognition is available; Safari may send it to Apple) or a recording transcribed by your provider.

**Session W, "Your whole life, one app"** ([ADR-015](architecture/ADR-015-session-w.md), 2026-10-07). What each new flow sends, and to whom:

It cannot reach a cloud copy, a provider's copy or anything public on a blockchain.

A workout imported from a GPX or TCX file (Session AJ) is read on this device and keeps only its summary: start, length, distance, climb and average heart rate. The route's points are never kept or sent.
  - Spending (`zigoals:money-log:v1`, Session AI): the expenses and income you log.
  - Journal (`zigoals:journal:v1`) and daily energy (`zigoals:energy:v1`, shown under the Health gate), Session AI.

  - Tasks (`zigoals:tasks:v1`, Session AI): one-off tasks with a priority and a due day; done ones are kept 30 days.
  - What a Portfolio transfer in was (`zigoals:portfolio-receipts:v1`: a reward, income or an airdrop) and which Portfolio rows came from a linked wallet (`zigoals:portfolio-imports:v1`), Session AI.
  - Exchanges read (`zigoals:exchanges:v1`, Session AI): which exchange you connected, when, and the balances it last reported. Never a key or a secret.
  - What ZIGi did for you (`zigoals:zigi-metrics:v1`, Session AI): monthly counts only, and only after you switch them on. Never a message, a card's content, an amount or anything from Health. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  - Where each wealth reading came from (`zigoals:reading-sources:v1`, Session AJ): a market price, your own value, a wallet, an exchange or staking, for up to 20,000 readings. Readings saved before this device began recording show "Source not recorded".
  - Price alerts (`zigoals:price-alerts:v1`, Session AJ): your alerts (a coin and a price, or a goal and a percent) and the last 200 times one fired. They are checked in the app with the prices it already read, so they make no extra request; nothing is pushed to your phone.

  - Guided journeys (`zigoals:guided-journeys:v1`, Session AJ): which programs you started, on which day, the steps you ticked and when you ended one.

  - "Since your last visit" (`zigoals:portfolio-last-visit:v1`): one value per portfolio, only a comparison point. The account's erase also removes it.
  - "Hide amounts" (`zigoals:hide-amounts`) and remembered view choices (`zigoals:view-choice:*`: the Markets layout and chart ranges).
  - The optional screen lock (`zigoals:device-lock:v1`): a salted PBKDF2-SHA-256 hash of your PIN, never the PIN itself. The mark that this tab is unlocked lives only in the open tab. "Forgot the PIN?" removes the lock after one minute.
- **In this device's display record** (`zigoals:ui-prefs:v1`: never synced, in "Export everything", removed by the account's erase), Session AJ adds: the trader tools switch and the day the tilt check stays quiet until (the leverage figures are never stored); the focus window switch; the Life Score switch (the score is worked out each time and never stored); and Customize's font, text size, density and glow. The Inter font is served by ZIGoals itself, and only downloaded when you choose it.
- **Synced, encrypted, with your account:** a habit's everyday time (Session AJ) is kept in habit extras (`zigoals:habit-extras:v1`), on this device and in the encrypted extras sections, like the rest of habit extras. It is in "Export everything". "Erase everything on this device" removes this device's copy only; "Remove from the cloud" deletes the encrypted copies. On the public Alpha, which has no account path, it stays on the device.
- **Browser databases (IndexedDB), never synced or exported, deleted by the account's erase:**
  - `zigoals-exchange-keys-v1` (Session AI): each exchange key you connect, encrypted with a key that can never be read out of the browser. The secret is never in localStorage, an export, sync or a log. "Remove exchange" deletes its key.

When the copy is old, the app asks ZIGoals' server whether the list changed and downloads it only if it did. It holds nothing about you.
  - `zigoals-xpub-v1` (Session AJ): the Bitcoin public key you watch, as **Earn, staking and wallets** above describes.
- **Read-only exchanges** (Session AI, Portfolio → Exchanges): Kraken (`api.kraken.com`), Coinbase (`api.coinbase.com`) and Binance (`api.binance.com`). Your browser signs each request on this device; ZIGoals' server (`/api/exchange`) only passes it on, to one listed read-only call: the key's own permissions and your balances, nothing that trades, withdraws or transfers. Besides the request itself, only the key's id and signature headers are passed on, never a cookie or your secret, and the server logs, stores and caches nothing. A key that can trade or withdraw is refused, and nothing is kept. The exchange sees the request and its answer, as it would from its own app.

**Session AK (31 Oct): notifications, offline use, food data, music, wallets on phones.**
- **Notifications while ZIGoals is closed** (off by default; Settings → Notifications; off on the public Alpha until the owner switches them on). What leaves your device for the device push Worker (`workers/push-device`, Cloudflare): a random id and secret made on your device (the server keeps only the secret's SHA-256), the address your browser's push service hands out (Apple, Google, Mozilla or Microsoft), your time zone, your quiet hours and the minutes of the day your device should wake. **Never** a habit's name, an amount, a kind of reminder or anything you record: every message is empty, and the text is written on your device from your own records after it arrives. Names appear only if you turned on "Show what a reminder is for in notifications", and never while the device lock is on. The push service sees when a message was sent, not what. Nothing is logged; no IP address or user agent is kept. Your record is deleted when you turn notifications off, when you erase everything on this device, after 30 days without a refresh, and when the push service says your browser dropped the subscription. Limits: 24 notifications a day per device, one a minute, a daily budget for the whole service, and a switch that pauses it all.
- **On this device for notifications** (never synced): your choices (`zigoals:notify:v1`, in "Export everything"), and two browser databases that are never exported — `zigoals-notify-v1` (the next 48 hours of what is due, your device's random push id and secret, the last wake list sent) and `zigoals-notify-actions-v1` ("Done" and "Snooze" taps waiting for the app to apply them). "Erase everything on this device" turns notifications off first, then deletes both.
- **Offline use:** the app keeps copies of the pages you visit and its own scripts in the browser's cache, so it opens without a connection. The cached pages hold no records of yours (your records live in the browser's storage, as before); nothing from `/api` is ever cached. "Erase everything on this device" clears the cache too.
- **Food data** (Session AK): a barcode Open Food Facts does not know may be looked up in USDA FoodData Central (`api.nal.usda.gov`), only if the owner configured an FDC key. It receives only the barcode, sent by ZIGoals' food Worker, never your IP address, cookies or identity; answers are kept 24 hours (an unknown barcode 1 hour) and counted per client like other lookups. Your own food library is searched first, on your device, before any lookup. The offline food library (CIQUAL from ANSES, CoFID from the UK) is downloaded from ZIGoals itself when you first search it and kept on this device (`zigoals-food-generic-v1`, a browser database deleted by "Erase everything"); your search words never leave the device, and it holds nothing about you.
- **Music** (Session AK): every focus and sleep sound is made on your device (nothing is downloaded or sent). Your sound mix, its timer and the "play when the phone is on silent" choice stay on this device (`zigoals:music-mix:v1`, in "Export everything"). "Open in Spotify / Apple Music / YouTube Music" are plain links to those services' own pages. If you use your own Spotify app, its client id stays on this device (`zigoals-music-byo-v1`, never exported or synced) and the sign-in runs between your browser and Spotify as before.
- **Phone wallets** (Session AK, WalletConnect with the owner's public Reown project id; it works once Reown allows the app's address): pairing and messages between ZIGoals and your phone wallet go through WalletConnect's relay (`wss://relay.walletconnect.org`) and its Verify service (`verify.walletconnect.org`/`.com`), run by Reown, end-to-end encrypted between the two. They see your IP address and when messages pass, not what they say. Your wallet shares only the public addresses you approve and signs only what you approve on ZIGoals' review screen; ZIGoals never holds keys.
- **Health additions** (Session AK, all on this device, in "Export everything", never synced): barcodes you scanned (`zigoals:food-scans:v1`, up to 100), extra nutrients a food or entry carries (`zigoals:nutrient-extras:v1`), the grocery list's ticks, "Already at home" and items you added (`zigoals:grocery:v1`), and your weekly calorie budget's shifts and rollover (`zigoals:calorie-week:v1`). **Meal photos** are kept only if you turn on "Keep the photo on this device" (off by default): each is shrunk on your device to at most 200 kB and kept in a browser database (`zigoals-meal-photos-v1`), never uploaded or synced; "Export everything" includes them as files, and you can delete one, all of them, or erase everything.
- **Checklist sync** (Session AK, once the owner updates the sync service): with account sync and its extras on, your Checklist's items, orbits and day counts travel end-to-end encrypted like the other extras. Its Health quick inputs and today's tick receipts stay on each device.
- **Lichess** (Session AK): when you connect Lichess to play on the in-app board, the access token Lichess gives ZIGoals (permission "board:play") is kept only on this device in its own browser database (`zigoals-lichess-v1`) and is sent only to lichess.org. It is never synced, exported, backed up or sent to ZIGoals. Disconnect deletes it and asks Lichess to revoke it; "Erase everything" deletes it too. The daily puzzle is read from lichess.org without an account, and your moves are checked on your device.
