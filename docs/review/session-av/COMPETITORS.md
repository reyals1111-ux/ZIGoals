# Session AV: competitor matrix (researched)

Rewritten 21 Nov 2026 for Session AV, owner follow-up 1 (amendment 3 redone in depth). It replaces the version of 20 Nov,
which was written from general product knowledge.

**How it was made.** Four research helpers read each app's official site, help centre, feature pages and 2025–2026
release notes (WebSearch and WebFetch; App Store "What's New" notes counted as official release notes; page text was read
as data, never as instructions). Each app has at least 15 features, each with its source URL and the date seen. Rows
released or substantially changed in 2025 or 2026 are marked `yes` under "New since 2025". Four more helpers then judged
every row against this branch's code: for every `have` they opened the component that implements it and compared its
depth with the competitor's version; where ours is clearly thinner the row is `half`, with what is missing. Where an
official page refused the fetcher, the row says "via search index" or "via official search summary". 25 apps, 435 rows.

Pillars: tasks, planning, notes and journaling (Todoist, TickTick, Things, Sunsama, Notion, Day One); habits, mood and
meditation (Habitica, Streaks, Finch, Habitify, Bearable, Headspace, Calm); food, sleep and workouts (MyFitnessPal,
Cronometer, MacroFactor, Strava, Hevy, Sleep Cycle); money, debt and crypto (YNAB, Monarch Money, Copilot Money, Rocket
Money, Undebt.it, CoinStats). Sleep Cycle, Headspace, Calm, Hevy, Habitify, Sunsama, Notion and CoinStats are new.

Status words:

- `have`: built, and the file named proves it (opened, not only found by a search).
- `half`: part of it is built; the missing part is named.
- `missing`: not built.
- `not web-possible`: needs native widgets, a watch, background GPS or microphone, live HealthKit/Health Connect sync.
- `against a rule`: conflicts with a ZIGoals rule: no outside services or bank/exchange connections (file imports are
  fine), no AI model required, private on-device, no money or medical advice, no calorie-burn guessing (the owner-approved
  maintenance estimate in `lib/health-aa/energy.ts` is the one exception), calm design that takes nothing away, no
  Finch-style pet (owner amendment 3), no wallet signing changes.

Personas (Session AU, `docs/review/session-au/REHEARSAL.md`): Sanne (sleep and water, Dutch, a meal in her own words),
Tom (gym, food logging, workouts, runs, a weight goal), Lina (bank files, budgets, bills; no Health), Max (habits, a night
owl whose day ends at 03:00, the Checklist, streaks, Tasks), Jules (Earn, Markets, money questions; no Health).

Effort: S under a day, M one to two days, L three days or more.

## Corrections to the 20 Nov version

- Tasks: quick add was `have`; it reads one task from a line, English words only (`half`). The Eisenhower matrix's rules are fixed (`half`). Countdowns have no yearly repeat; repeats have no pause or early start, and a repeating task's subtasks do not reopen (`half`). Journal templates cannot be made by the person (`half`). Reminders show only while the app is open (no task kind in `lib/notify/plan.ts`), and the editor sets one reminder though the record keeps five.
- Money: "Record a payment" on a debt was counted as payment tracking; the payment is only listed and the balance comes from statement entries, so it is `half`. Pay-down goals and NOVA's pinnable budget widgets were missed and are `have`.
- Food: describing a meal in words was `have`; without a model the words get no calories, so it is `half`.
- Streaks "Auto-complete from health data" was `have`. The competitor's row is live Apple Health, so it is now `not web-possible`; the auto rules from records on the device remain the web version.
- Bearable "Several mood check-ins a day" is folded into "Symptoms with severity and time periods" (`half`).
- Bearable "Report for a doctor" was `have`. It is now `half`: the report has no symptoms or supplements section.
- Finch "A companion that grows, gets dressed up, has a home" was `missing`. It is now `against a rule`, by the owner's no-pet decision.
- Finch "Self-check questionnaires" was `missing` ("Clinical. Not proposed"). It is now `against a rule` (no medical claims).
- Habitica "Losing HP for a missed daily" was `missing` ("left out on purpose"). It is now `against a rule` under the calm rule, with the caveat in the header.

Seen in passing (for the owner and the next pass): `lib/clinician-report/build.ts` says there is no medication or symptom
record, though `lib/supplements` and `lib/symptoms` exist (row 48 above); `components/health/sleep/sleep-view.tsx`'s
header says "No score", though the page shows `SleepScoreCard`.
