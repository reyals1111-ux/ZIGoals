# Lane 2 registry: PK-10, chain Help answers wait for the chain choice (`lane2/pk10`)

Owner decision PK-10 (follow-up 3, decided 21 Nov; ADR-045 "Owner follow-up 3"): "the chain Help topics are hidden while
no chain is chosen, and they still turn up in Help search with a note on how to choose one." LEDGER.md line "PK-10 chain
Help topics hidden while no chain is chosen, still found in Help search with a note" (follow-up 3's lane-2 items).
UNFINISHED.md PK-10 (KL-help-chain, P14-A's "owner list 8"). The rule for "chosen" is lib/chain-choice.ts, read with the
shell's one hook `useChainChoice()` (components/chain/use-chain-choice.ts): the Showcase, a wallet in use or linked, a
crypto page switched on (Markets, Staking, Portfolio, Ecosystem), or Earn or Governance switched on.

## Verdict

| row | verdict |
|---|---|
| PK-10 | built; it takes effect once the lead applies the help-page.tsx patch below (the one shared file it needs) |

What a person sees on a device that has not chosen the chain:

- **Help**: the 25 chain answers below and the plain question "Are my Goals on the blockchain?" are left out of the page.
  The headings that named the chain say plain words: the topic "Earn, Staking, Ecosystem and Chess" reads "More pages and
  Chess" (same place, same `#more-pages` link), its section title too; section `ae`'s eyebrow "TODAY'S SCENE AND THE
  SIDEBAR"; section `af`'s eyebrow "ZIGI AND THE CHECKLIST" and title "ZIGi, and your day as one path". No section
  empties (each keeps consumer answers), so no section heading hides.
- **A link that names a chain answer** (`/app/help#help-<id>`: from Search, Today's What's new, a page's own link, ZIGi's
  Help reply or a Known limitations line) still shows it, opened and focused as every linked answer, with the note at the
  top of its answer: "This answer is about the money and chain pages. Switch one on in Settings → Your pages & buttons
  to see these topics in Help." ("Settings → Your pages & buttons" is an in-sentence link to `/app/settings#your-pages`.)
  Once shown it stays for the visit (following another link never pulls it away), and the note goes once the chain is
  chosen, without the answer closing.
- **The search palette** still finds every chain answer by its question; its line carries the same note
  ("Help · This answer is about …"); the note is added after the search, so its own words never make a match. The chain
  topic is found as "More pages and Chess".
- **Help's own search box** ("Search Help") lists the hidden chain answers whose question holds every word under its
  count ("N answers about the money and chain pages match, listed under the search."), with the note and one 44 px link
  per answer that opens it in place; an answer already on the page (opened from a link) is found the usual way, not
  listed twice.
- Once the chain is chosen everything is exactly as before (every answer, heading and search line).

Left visible on purpose (not chain topics): the Known limitations lines that name staking, Earn or WalletConnect (they
are the Alpha's honest limits; their links now deep-link with the note), the "Every published version" titles (a record of
releases), "Is any of this real money?" and "Where do prices come from?" (Goals, Wealth and prices concern everyone).
Today's What's new card already filters chain lines (lib/whats-new-chain/filter.ts, Session AK RB).

## Help text and What's new

- Help Q+A (EN), for the more-pages section, after "What does the Chess page do?":
  **Why don’t I see answers about staking, wallets or Earn?** — Until you switch on a money and chain page (Earn,
  Markets, Staking, Portfolio, Ecosystem or Governance) in Settings → Your pages & buttons, Help leaves those answers out,
  so a first look stays simple. Search and Help’s own search still find them, with a note on how to switch one on, and a
  link to one still opens it. Once you switch one on, every answer shows.
  (Id suggestion `av2-chain-help`; it is a plain answer, so it is not itself wrapped. Adding it means one HELP_QUESTIONS
  line in static.ts in page order.)
- What's new label: "Help shows the staking and wallet answers once you switch on a money and chain page".
