## Production-build spec results (first pass: 4 failed, 9 skipped) — what changed

- hud-look (d) Dark border parity: the old sheet let `.nova-widget` override the Holo rim; the regenerated sheets (5e54e15a) keep `.nova-widget[data-holo]` (1,2,0) above it. Sheets must be regenerated again after this pass's CSS.
- hud-look (e): app cause — the View controls panel stayed open when the focus moved to the toast; it now closes on focus leave (nova-command.tsx); the spec asserts that.
- hud-perf: app cause — turning ornaments were groups inside the data SVGs (main-thread repaint every frame) and the orb breathed inside its SVG; both are compositor layers now. The integrator measures.
- hud-two-way: Health links are full loads by design (camera permission), so that step proves the record, not "no reload"; the live no-reload claims stay.
- Skips: the 9 were the mobile project skipping computer-only specs (de-duplication). Stillness now also runs on a 390 px phone in both projects; the perf skip is a true capability skip (CDP throttling, laptop target).
