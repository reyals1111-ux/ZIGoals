  1. Read this page, including the key insights below.
  2. In MARKET_LANDSCAPE, read the four-app stack table and the complaint themes.
  3. In FEATURE_GAP_MATRIX, read the section on gaps that matter for paying users.
  4. In BETA_IDEAS_BACKLOG, read the top 20 and the three 90-day options.
- **Bring:**
  - the friends-Alpha feedback themes from contact@zigoals.app, with no personal details in the notes;
  - [COST_MODEL.md](../business/COST_MODEL.md);
  - [LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md](../business/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md).
- **During:** fill in the decision log at the end of this page. Topics that are not ready stay "parked", with what is missing.
- **After:**
  - turn the chosen ideas into session briefs;
  - add the new lawyer questions to a dated file next to LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md, as that file asks;
  - keep this pack unchanged as the record of what was known on 2026-10-03.
- **Re-check before relying on a number.** Prices, ratings, fees and store rules change often. Each one carries its date.

## Rules this pack follows
- **Sources:**
  - official or reputable only: company sites and pricing pages, official App Store and Google Play listings, official developer and policy pages, public filings, regulators, and first-party public reports;
  - blogs and affiliate review sites are never cited as fact;
  - each document ends with every source and its access date.
- **Unknown stays unknown.** No number, price, rating, user count or market size is invented or estimated. A missing fact is marked **UNVERIFIED** or "unknown".
- **Prices** always carry an amount, currency, storefront or region, and the date read. When a store lists several prices for one item, all are shown and none is interpreted.
- **Copyright:**
  - everything is in our own words;
  - at most one short quote, under 15 words, per source;
  - reviews appear only as aggregated themes.
- **Access:**
  - no logins and no paywalled data;
  - no bot wall was bypassed; blocked sources are listed by URL;
  - no personal data about individuals.

## Labels
| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **UNVERIFIED** | No official or reputable source could confirm it on the read date |
| **company claim** | The company says it about itself; not independently checked |
| **small visible sample** | Complaint themes from the few public reviews a store page shows; not representative |
| **assumption** | A modelling input chosen for a scenario (PATH_TO_500K only), with its reason. Never a forecast |
| ✓ / ⚠ / ✗ | Principle fit: fits / bends a principle (the principle and the reason are named) / conflicts (listed for completeness, never proposed) |
| **LC§1–LC§5** | Legal flag pointing to [LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md](../business/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md): §1 Open Food Facts and the ODbL · §2 GDPR and health data · §3 MiCA, staking and stablecoins · §4 advice-like features, AI and medical-device rules · §5 app stores, payments and consumer law |
| **LC§6** | Earn and staking questions that Session N proposes on its branch (`earn/session-n-2026-10-02`), not yet on `main` |
| **LC-new** | A question not yet in the checklist (for example children, or a non-EU regional rule), to add after the brainstorm |

A legal flag means "ask a lawyer before building or saying this". It never means a conclusion either way.

## The ZIGoals principles used as the fit check
| Short name | Principle | An idea bends it when it… |
|---|---|---|
| **chain-invisible** | Consumer first, chain invisible | asks a person to understand chains, tokens, gas or wallets before getting value |
| **private** | Private by design: on the device, plus end-to-end encrypted sync | sends readable personal data to a server or third party, or adds tracking |
| **no-chore** | Never a chore: no manual backups or transfers | makes a person export, import, copy or back up to stay safe or in sync |
| **non-custodial** | Non-custodial money: the person signs; no AI moves funds | holds keys or funds, or moves value without a signature per step |
| **honest** | Honest numbers: no fake data, no projections as promises | shows estimates as facts, invents data, or implies a return |
| **testnet-first** | Testnet first | needs mainnet value movement before testnet proof |
