# ZIGoals brand assets — 2026-10-01

Owner-supplied artwork (Robin), optimized in Claude chat on 2026-10-01 for use in apps/web.
Originals: six 3000x3000 transparent PNGs and ZIGoals-FINAL-1080p.mp4 (16.5 s, 24 fps, H.264 + AAC music).

**File names (Session P, 2026-10-03):** the high-density files are `<name>-2x.webp` (they were `<name>@2x.webp` until
then). Workers Assets answers a path with `@` in it with a 307 redirect to its `%40` spelling, one extra round trip on
every high-density screen (seen on the live Alpha, 2026-10-03). The bytes are unchanged; `tests/brand-assets.spec.ts`
and `lib/brand-assets.test.ts` keep every file at 1x and 2x reachable and free of `@`.

## marks/  (figure + its words, transparent WebP, 720 px wide; -2x = 1440 px)
today-swan ("Shape & Fold / Your Own Future"), goals-lotus ("Goals"), habits-butterfly ("Habits"),
health-heart ("Health"), wealth-bull ("= Wealth"), zigoals-lockup (Z + "ZIGoals" + equation).
Use: the desktop sidebar figure, on every page since Session K (the six pages without a mark of their own show the
today-swan). There its baked-in words are clipped off and shown from words/ instead, larger on the planet.

## words/  (Session K, 2026-10-02: each mark's words on their own, transparent lossless WebP; -2x = twice the size)
today-swan ("Shape & Fold / Your Own Future"), goals-lotus ("Goals"), habits-butterfly ("Habits"), health-heart ("Health"),
wealth-bull ("= Wealth").

**How they were made:**
- Cut from `marks/<name>-2x.webp`: the words region after the mark's fully transparent gap, plus an 8 px transparent margin.
- Lanczos-resized with ImageMagick 6.9.12 (the cloud sandbox's own copy; nothing added to the repository).
- The 1x file is exactly the size the words are drawn in the sidebar: 1.6 times their size inside the 160 px mark.

| Mark | 1x (CSS px) | -2x |
|---|---|---|
| today-swan | 188 × 49 | 376 × 98 |
| goals-lotus | 89 × 31 | 178 × 62 |
| habits-butterfly | 99 × 31 | 198 × 62 |
| health-heart | 100 × 31 | 200 × 62 |
| wealth-bull | 152 × 33 | 304 × 66 |

**Use:** the desktop sidebar shows each page's figure (the mark file, with its baked-in words clipped off in that transparent gap), and these words larger on the planet below it. See `components/page-mark.tsx` and `components/navigation.css`.

## figures/  (figure only, words cropped off, transparent WebP, 640 px wide; -2x = 1280 px)
today-swan, goals-lotus, habits-butterfly, health-heart, wealth-bull, zigoals-z (the Z alone).
Use: compact placements (e.g. phone headers) where the words would be too small.
