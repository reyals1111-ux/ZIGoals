# Run #9.1 — Beta productization

Local implementation on `codex/run9-goal-intelligence-live-wealth`, based on `f5d04a01fc7a6b97a574b5ca900f3e2b251dcd9b`. No deployment, merge, push, PR creation, wallet signature or financial execution. The production app remains unchanged.

1. **Implementation.** Wealth is the canonical home for assets, with a shared picker, asset detail pages, favourites, market history and atomic Goal funding. Existing wallet reads, local simulation, Habits and Health retain their boundaries.
2. **Visual redesign.** Readable labels, quieter asset summaries, purposeful owned-asset cards, larger financial hierarchy, smooth nebula rings and consistent source/freshness badges replace the dense, sparse Run #9 panels. The supplied 13 screenshots were reviewed in order as acceptance references.
3. **Today.** The pinned/open Goal pulse shows current funding, remaining amount, actual versus planned contributions, next contribution and latest record. It links to Goal intelligence. A real-data Goals/Habits/Health/Wealth snapshot and compact market watch connect daily life with wealth.
4. **Wealth.** The retained horizon hero now leads to Add asset, Manage assets and Refresh prices. Favourites, a freshness/attention pulse, compact asset-class summaries, owned cards, allocation/available values, recorded wealth history and restorable archives form the workspace. Currency totals remain separate.
5. **Canonical management.** Add cash, crypto, stablecoins, stocks, ETF references, precious metals, property or custom assets. Edit quantity, label, notes and explicit valuations from asset detail. Automatic market identity stays fixed. Archive confirmation names linked allocations before release, retains history and allows restoration. Locked linked Goals prevent release.
6. **Stake / Positions.** Manual Crypto and Stablecoins remain here alongside native wallet/staking reads. Cash, stocks, metals, property and custom wealth live in Wealth. Edit links reuse the canonical editor; there is no second asset store.
7. **Favourites.** Up to eight canonical CoinGecko identities, including Bitcoin, Nvidia and Gold, can be followed without ownership. Prices, sources and freshness are visible. Move earlier, remove, reload and Today representation are supported. Favourites never enter wealth totals or Goal allocations.
8. **Charts.** Asset and favourite details contain a responsive price chart, pointer/keyboard inspection, exact UTC table and only ranges with adequate observations. A single point remains a point; absent history explains when tracking starts. Local observations never invent earlier performance.
9. **CoinGecko.** Search uses the cached canonical catalog, exact IDs, coin/RWA distinctions, manual fallback and public-only requests. A real local-adapter Bitcoin/USD/90-day request returned **200 and 2,161 observations**, from 2026-06-22T21:00:00Z through 2026-09-20T20:30:20Z. See [machine-recorded evidence](verification/run9-1/live-history-check.json). No paid endpoint or new provider was introduced.
10. **Goal creation/editing.** One AssetPicker selects existing wealth or creates an additional canonical asset. Value Goals retain separate allocation quantities per asset. A successful new-asset save returns to existing assets and disables duplicate submission while saving. Existing allocation editing uses the same picker; adding an asset alone never fabricates a contribution.
11. **Contributions.** Fund Goal is the default: it previews the added quantity/value, current and resulting funding, allocated quantity, remaining amount and surplus. Confirmation saves asset balance, allocation and contribution evidence under one private-store lock. It caps allocation at the remaining target and rejects an indivisible asset when the cap cannot be represented accurately. **20,000 USD into an empty 2,000 USD Goal produces 100% completion, one contribution and 18,000 USD available.** Funding an already partially funded Goal preserves its existing wealth. Record history only explicitly leaves wealth and allocation unchanged.
12. **Funding Wealth.** This replaces the visible term Funding Health throughout the app. Goal status, contribution pace, quality, next action, zero-return forecast and required recurring amount are distinct. No plan is explicit. Optional scheduled-date links consume only the matched amount, including partial installments; reversing the history restores that schedule credit. Unmatched records and market changes never guess schedule matches.
13. **Activity.** Dated cards distinguish Goal/contribution, asset, Habit and Health events with category labels, icons, amounts, provenance and destination links. Wealth has its own filter. Gold and stocks receive asset-aware treatments. History reversals remain visible; they do not silently alter a holding.
14. **Branding.** The app hero and consumer subtitle use exactly **Goals, Habits & Health = Wealth**, with continuous nebula colour and a stronger financial-orbit eyebrow. The standalone landing subtitle is aligned. Ecosystem positioning remains The Goal Layer for ZIGChain.
15. **Design system.** Shared AssetIcon, ProgressRing, FreshnessBadge, MetricCard and modal Sheet support the new surfaces. Rings use a continuous conic gradient with two-decimal text and an accessible progress label. Visual QA corrected a flex rule that stretched the Today ring, added asset-detail spacing and improved hero contrast.
16. **Health.** The working nutrition/diary experience remains first. Barcode scanning, wearables and food-photo concepts appear below it as clearly planned, unavailable or not connected. No camera capture, wearable connection, inferred meal or fake metric is added.
17. **Roadmap extras delivered.** Global Quick add routes to the existing Goal, Wealth, contribution, Habit and Health workflows; Portfolio pulse exposes real attention needs; the Life + Wealth snapshot uses existing private stores. None creates a parallel mutation path.
18. **Persistence.** Schema v3 adds bounded favourites, durable Position tracking/archive periods, asset Activity events and funding metadata to the existing `zigoals:platform:v1` namespace. Valid v1/v2 migrate in memory; passive viewing preserves exact bytes. The first explicit write retains recovery bytes. v3 backups round-trip; unknown/invalid versions fail closed. Independent Health/Habit stores are unchanged.
19. **Unit tests.** Final results are in the [verification index](verification/run9-1/README.md). New regressions cover quantity valuation, original quote timestamps, immutable retry identity, surplus, matched/partial/reversed schedules, archive/restoration history, watchlist boundaries, migrations, backup and atomic storage failure. Existing exact-unit and provider/privacy suites remain intact.
20. **Browser tests.** The ten owner acceptance flows are implemented in `apps/web/tests/run9-1.spec.ts`, with deterministic public market adapters and disposable private data. Existing tests were migrated to the actual picker/editor controls without removing accounting assertions. Both desktop and mobile are verified; final counts are in the verification index.
21. **Screenshots.** [Verification index](verification/run9-1/README.md) links the four-width evidence. All application screenshot portfolios, histories, Habits and Health entries are **controlled fixtures**, not the owner's portfolio or live price evidence. The separate live-history JSON is the real provider check.
22. **Mobile.** 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 widths cover landing/Today, Wealth, asset detail, Goal detail, Funding Wealth, Activity, Health, Goals, Positions and asset addition. Checks assert no document overflow; manual visual inspection reviews hierarchy, clipping, rings, cards and modal controls.
23. **Security/privacy.** Only public market identity, quote currency and history range reach the new endpoint. Strict bounded request schemas, response parsing, fixed provider paths, retry/freshness admission and retained last-good values remain in force. Local compilation and Alpha outputs receive credential scans; the existing Alpha environment sanitizer strips the server credential from deployable artifacts. Exact results are in the verification index.
24. **Limits.** See the [Beta backlog](RUN_9_1_BETA_BACKLOG.md). RWA prices are tokenized reference units, not authoritative physical spot or broker execution prices. RWA provider history is not exposed; saved local unit observations remain available. Ordinary 24-hour change and watchlist sparklines are omitted when evidence is absent. Holdings are private user records and do not connect accounts.
25. **Remaining Beta work.** Transaction-based return attribution, distributed provider quotas, broader history retention, richer watchlist movement, full physical-metal/FX evidence, camera/wearable integrations and explicit cloud sync remain scoped backlog items, not claims of this release.

27. **Git status.** The final delivery records repository status after the local commit. Browser profiles, traces, scratch reports and original archives remain ignored; user private state and environment files are not tracked.
