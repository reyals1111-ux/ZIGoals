# Run #9.2 — final local productization and Showcase

This release connects Goals, Habits, Health and Wealth in a populated, explicitly fictional Showcase. It adds Markets, public asset identities and movement, portfolio composition, a funding agenda, actionable attention items, Habit consistency and a nutrition dashboard. Financial ownership, allocations and contributions retain the existing accounting model.

The owner’s final slogan correction supersedes the earlier lowercase/free-wrapping brief. Work stays on `codex/run9-goal-intelligence-live-wealth`, from `21b5f6d9edcea948b7a31db65cbc95d6ef056210`. Nothing is deployed, merged, pushed or submitted as a PR.

## Required 35-point record

1. **Product audit.** All 17 required destinations received a KEEP/IMPROVE/REDESIGN decision before implementation. The [deliverables manifest](RUN_9_2_DELIVERABLES.md) retains each requirement separately, with source, tests and visual evidence.
2. **Exact slogan.** Line 1 is `Today's Goals, Habits & Health`; line 2 is `= Tomorrow's Wealth`. `Today's Goals,` and `= Tomorrow's` are bright white. `Habits & Health` and `Wealth` use the same continuous cyan–blue–violet–magenta–coral gradient. Explicit line wrappers and container-based type sizing preserve those two lines at 1440, 1024, 390 and 320 pixels. Both the static landing and Today hero share this composition; YOUR FINANCIAL ORBIT has a separate, stronger supporting treatment.
3. **Showcase architecture.** Settings activates a versioned session namespace. A new generation is fully staged and validated before its selector is committed. Reset stages a new generation; failure preserves the previous one. Exit restores the normal storage adapter. Queued Goal saves and delayed public cache responses capture their original scope; they cannot write into another mode. No wallet connection is initiated in Showcase. Visible notices distinguish fictional private records from actual public market evidence.
4. **Dataset inventory.** Thirteen holdings: BTC, ETH, ZIG, USDC, Apple, Nvidia, Gold, Silver, VOO, USD, EUR, property and a custom asset. Seven favourites include six owned identities and favourite-only Solana. Six Goals cover three Value Goals, a completed BTC Quantity Goal, a Project and a Reward Goal, with Ahead, On Track, Behind, Completed and Review states. Records include matched/unmatched funding, history-only entries, a correction/reversal and funded surplus; 195 valuation observations and 180 Goal valuation points plus plan and milestone events cover a fictional 90-day history. Six Habits and 30 days of Health records populate consistency, meals, macros, movement and weight. All private quantities/history are deterministic examples, never claimed as verified balances or real returns.
5. **Every page audited.** Landing, Today, Goals, Goal creation, Goal detail, Funding Wealth, Wealth, Markets, Asset Add, Asset Detail, Stake / Positions, Habits, Habit history, Health, Activity, Ecosystem and Settings. The manifest records each decision and final screenshot links.
6. **New surfaces.** `/app/markets` is navigable from the main rail and Wealth. Settings has Load, Reset and Return to my data controls. Global Quick Add opens existing creation flows.
7. **New modules.** Portfolio Composition, Funding Agenda, Needs Attention, Habit Consistency, Nutrition Summary/Meal Timeline and the Market Center are visible populated modules, beyond cosmetic card changes.
8. **Wealth.** Holdings now distinguish unit reference price from total holding value, show canonical identity/logos, public movement where available, allocation/available amounts, provenance and actions. Composition reconciles asset classes, top holdings and allocated/available amounts within each currency. EUR and USD remain separate; there is no invented FX conversion. Empty categories remain compact.
9. **Markets and favourites.** Owned, Favourite and category views share canonical identity, bounded local search and public data caches. Following Solana does not create a holding or affect Goal wealth. Favourites preserve ordering/removal and show a saving state until the locked write completes. The compact Today strip remains available.
10. **Asset logos.** CoinGecko server metadata supplies narrowly validated raster URLs through a bounded local proxy. Missing/broken images retain readable names and distinct asset-class glyphs. Metadata, quotes and insights are batched, cached and deduplicated. No API credential enters a browser URL, request body or public response.
11. **Today.** The dashboard combines the primary Goal, actual/plan pace, next/latest contribution, funding agenda, attention links, favourites and Life + Wealth summary. Negative net funding and Reward Goal units retain their correct meanings.
12. **Funding Wealth.** The interface explains current funding, actual versus planned contributions, the next scheduled date, required pace and zero-return projection. Liquidity and missing/stale evidence stay separate. A source scan found no obsolete user-facing Funding Health terminology; internal accounting identifiers and dated reports remain unchanged where appropriate.
13. **Goals.** Cards show status, current/target/remaining, progress, target date and next contribution cadence. Smooth nebula progress rings and readable metadata carry into detail, source allocation, history and contribution views. Project milestones remain distinct from financial Goals.
14. **Contribution regression.** The preserved 20,000 USD contribution into a 2,000 USD Goal records one contribution, allocates 2,000 USD, leaves 18,000 USD available and completes the Goal once. Timeline and Activity update. History-only recording leaves balances and allocations unchanged. Browser and unit regressions also cover retries, surplus, reversals, schedule credits and stale previews.
15. **Activity.** Larger category glyphs, readable titles/metadata, date groups, filters and destination links cover finance, crypto, stock/ETF, metals, Goals, Habits and Health. Current favourites appear as present context; follow dates are not invented because the schema does not record them.
16. **Health.** Actual saved diary records drive calories consumed/target/remaining, protein/carbs/fat progress, meal count, meal timeline, movement and 30-day nutrition patterns. The sample day contains four meals, 1,970 kcal against a 2,200 kcal target, 131 g protein, 217 g carbohydrate and 65 g fat. The working diary remains usable. Barcode, photo recognition and Apple Health/Health Connect/Fitbit/Garmin stay clearly planned or not connected.
17. **Habits.** Completion, streaks, weekly momentum and a 30-day heatmap use saved check-ins. Exact history remains available; completing a contribution Habit does not create financial funding.
18. **Ecosystem.** Readable status cards retain The Goal Layer for ZIGChain positioning and explicit integration states. No fake connection or financial execution was added.
19. **Settings.** Showcase controls sit beside backup/export, recovery and data safety explanations. Normal local, wallet/testnet and unrelated storage bytes survive Showcase load/edit/reset/exit unchanged.
20. **Quick Add.** A native accessible dialog provides Goal, Asset, Contribution, Habit and Health actions. Contribution first selects a Goal; the other actions reuse existing forms. Keyboard focus, Escape, return focus and rapid repeated same-page entry are covered. Matching URL intents are consumed synchronously before opening, with Next-compatible history state; unrelated parameters/hash remain intact.
21. **Needs Attention.** Deterministic missing/stale values, allocation deficits, overdue funding and missing plans link to the affected asset or Goal action. These are data/planning tasks, not investment recommendations.
22. **Typography.** Functional text, labels, metadata, timestamps and chart alternatives use a 14 px minimum. Body and supporting copy generally use 15–16 px. Narrow layouts recompose rather than shrinking controls. The exact long public unit-price case wraps without losing precision or overflowing 320 px.
23. **Gradient/progress system.** Slogan spans and shared progress visuals use a continuous nebula spectrum. White remains the main readable text color. Reduced-motion behavior is preserved.
24. **Responsive results.** Automated four-width Showcase checks cover layout bounds and readable functional text. Final screenshot inspection covers all required destinations, modal scrolling, chart containment and both corrected slogan lines. The screenshot index lists exact evidence by width.
25. **Accessibility.** Tests cover AssetPicker and Quick Add keyboard interactions, focus restoration, labels, reduced motion, failed-image identity fallbacks and exact chart tables. Chart numbers/dates use readable HTML alternatives alongside their SVG visuals. This is focused product QA, not a claim of formal accessibility certification.
26. **Persistence/migration.** Schema v3 is preserved. Existing v1/v2 migration, backups, future-version rejection, corrupt-byte recovery and cross-tab locks remain covered. Showcase adds a scoped storage adapter, not another financial schema. Staging failure and queued-operation scope transitions have dedicated regressions.
27. **Unit tests.** Final source passes **1006 tests in 76 files**. No baseline test was removed.
28. **Browser tests.** Final production desktop/mobile result is recorded in the [verification index](verification/run9-2/README.md). Baseline slogan/Funding Wealth assertions were updated to the requested copy; the privacy test now permits only explicitly validated public market identities. The watchlist test awaits visible persisted removal before navigating, while preserving the final two-favourite assertion.
29. **Alpha/security.** The packaged local Workers build, dry run, full browser suite and dedicated Alpha/security result are recorded in the verification index. Actual provider capture exposed Workers rejecting redirect:error; server requests now use manual mode and reject non-2xx/redirected responses without following them. Both the CoinGecko adapter and logo proxy have regression coverage. Concurrent capture also exposed shared server waiters crossing ended request contexts; bounded request-owned waiting and cancellation recovery now have independent actual Workers coverage. Final public history/screenshots and all quality gates were verified after these fixes. Dependency audit, isolated deployment configuration checks, credential patterns and exact configured-key scans are included. Intermittent OpenNext response-stream cancellation diagnostics during rapid navigation are recorded as a remaining framework investigation; seven separate quiet full-body checks passed without errors, and no final market API 5xx or cross-request warning remained. No deployment command without `--dry-run` is part of this run.
30. **Screenshots.** Final count and the four-width surface matrix are in the verification index. Showcase portfolio/Goal/Habit/Health content is fictional. Public Bitcoin price/history evidence in the final capture comes from the actual configured CoinGecko provider, without mock market responses.
31. **Independent review.** The fresh read-only compliance pass and closure of valid gaps are saved in [independent-review.txt](verification/run9-2/independent-review.txt). Earlier focused storage/product reviews are supporting evidence, not substitutes for the final review.
32. **Genuine Beta backlog.** Remaining work is performance attribution, longer history capacity/aggregation, full contractual plan revisions, distributed provider quota admission, unsupported RWA/physical/FX coverage, larger portfolio sorting/capacity controls, real capture/wearable integrations, explicit multi-device sync and a separately authorized hosted rollout. Delivered movement, sparklines, Markets and Showcase are removed from the backlog.

The build identity records the starting source HEAD plus the dirty flag because packaging precedes the local commit. A tracked report cannot embed its own future Git content hash; this post-commit receipt avoids a stale or invented hash.

35. **Release boundary.** Nothing deployed, merged, pushed or opened as a PR. No trading, swaps, staking execution, custody, automated investing, financial signing or Goal Manager deployment was added or performed.
