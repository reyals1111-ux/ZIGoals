# Long-term vision — not implemented in Phase1

Goal → plan → policy → approved strategy → ZIGChain infrastructure. The goal persists as underlying strategies change. Future adapters may include verified Valdora stZIG/vaults, WME, Nawa or other approved infrastructure; no partnership or available integration is implied.

Policies may bound risk, minimum liquidity, maximum redemption delay, liquid reserve, categories, allocation caps, ZIG-native/Shariah-aligned preference and RWA eligibility. Glide paths could reduce risk approaching a deadline only through explicit permission and supported mechanics.

Family/group structures need owner, co-owner, contributor, beneficiary, viewer, guardian roles and explicit withdrawal governance. No shared custody is assumed in the single-owner contract. Reputation should use verified age, capital, realized returns, drawdown/volatility, redemption records, incidents/audits and goal outcomes, not star ratings.

Simple and advanced views share transparent accounting. Possible later business models: free basic goals, Plus planning/alerts/AI, Family features, disclosed ecosystem arrangements, B2B/API. No Alpha performance-fee stack or billing. Mainnet requires proven testnet, external security review, explicit authorization, multisig/timelock where appropriate, published upgrade policy and bug bounty.
