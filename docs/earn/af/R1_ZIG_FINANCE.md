## Summary

The rebrand is real and dated. The official announcement is the X post https://x.com/ZIGFinance/status/2102659605541073167 (account @ZIGFinance, user id 968212085854408708, business-verified) created 2026-09-23T07:21:30Z, text beginning "ZIGChain is now ZIG Finance."; KuCoin completed the exchange-side rename on 2026-09-25; zig.finance/about dates the merger of ZIG Markets, ZIG Chain, ZIG Labs and $ZIG into ZIG Finance to September 2026, and the site's Terms (effective 22 Sep 2026, operator STARBUY (PTY) LTD, South African law) name those four plus Zignaly as distinct brands. The L1 keeps the name ZIG Chain; the token ticker and contracts are unchanged. zig.finance is a 12-page static Astro site (sitemap read); there is no docs section on it, docs.zig.finance does not resolve, and the chain docs stay at docs.zigchain.com.

Everything the build depends on was re-read from the chain today (2026-10-18, mainnet height 13042400–13042693, testnet 8290499–8290516). Both networks report bond_denom "azig", display "zig" at exponent 18; "uzig" survives only as the legacy 6-decimal denom (mainnet supply 136,936,694,446 uzig) with no exponent-6 unit in its on-chain metadata. The decoy is public and named: `coin.zig153u6vhv4ds6x95chdnqmsduwxzpt0teh43alsk.zig`, symbol "ZIG", display "ZIG" at exponent 6, description "ZIG same-name Factory decoy for authorized deposit testing", supply 1,000,000 base units. Mainnet: supply 2,337,754,283.86 ZIG, bonded 425,559,497.76 ZIG (18.20% bonded, derived), inflation 1.0454% (mint params min 1%/max 2%/goal 15%, blocks_per_year 10,512,000), community tax 2%, annual provisions 24,439,912.81 ZIG, min gas 2500000000azig, zigchaind v5.1.2. Governance: 4-day voting, 10,000 ZIG deposit, quorum 0.334, threshold 0.51, veto 0.334, expedited 1 day/5,000,000 ZIG; 42 proposals (ids 1–43, #33 absent), none in voting, latest #43 passed 2026-10-06. Validators: 19 total, 14 bonded (max 14), 0 jailed, top-10 hold 96.52% of bonded stake, four "Foundation" validators 63.2%, commissions 5–10%. CosmWasm upload is AnyOfAddresses (15 addresses mainnet, 34 testnet), instantiate Everybody; mainnet whitelisting is by governance proposal, testnet by support request. IBC mainnet: channel-1 Axelar (axelar-dojo-1/channel-182; also channel-0, channel-2 to Axelar), channel-3 Noble (noble-1/channel-175), channel-4 Cosmos Hub (cosmoshub-4/channel-1555), channel-12 Injective (injective-1/channel-456); seven icahost channels from Cosmos Hub; no Osmosis channel exists. ICA host/controller enabled, transfer send/receive enabled, Stride-style rate limits of 5% out / 10% in per 24h on azig for every transfer channel and on ZIG.axl on channel-1.

All registry REST and RPC endpoints for both networks answered 200 with `access-control-allow-origin: *` (official api.zigchain.com is behind Cloudflare with X-Frame-Options SAMEORIGIN); the old `testnet-zigchain-lcd.numia.xyz` host used in the repo's older docs now returns 401. No provider publishes a numeric rate limit; the docs say the public endpoints are "not recommended for production use". The docs site, Hub, faucet, zigchain.com, Medium and LinkedIn block automated reads (403/429) and are recorded as unreadable; their content was taken from the ZIGChain/docs GitHub source (last commit 2026-03-30, so stale) and the repo's own 2026-10-07 reads. Tokenomics come from the official paper "$ZIG Tokenomics 2.0" (whitepaper.zigchain.com/zig-tokenomics.pdf, modified 2026-07-01): genesis 2,500,000,000 ZIG, circulating at mainnet 1,726,440,444 (69.06%), founders 445M vesting linearly over 30 months from January 2026, Foundation 100M on the same schedule, ZIG 2.0 monthly revenue-funded buybacks from 1 July 2026 with a 500M cumulative target, half to the Ecosystem Pool and half subject to a validator retirement vote, retirement wallet zig14har2kjzze5adwprvwjtruf2lzvfta53wflpml (balance 46,578,505.76 ZIG today). CoinGecko's supply figures (max 2.0B, total 1.954B, circulating 1.409B) conflict with both the paper and the chain and are flagged. Open items: the ZIGChain/zigchain-registry native file still says uzig/6; the testnet chain-registry entry says a 7-day lock while the chain says 21 days; proposal-detail URLs on zigscan and the Hub could not be verified; the "World Liberty / USD1 PayFi vault" and "new tokenomics" headlines on CoinGecko have no primary source found.

## 2. ZIG Finance in plain words (reusable, 212 words)

ZIG Finance is the umbrella name, since September 2026, for a group of related products that used to be called ZIGChain. It comes from the team behind Zignaly, a social-investing platform. The idea is to take investment products that normally only institutions can reach, such as private credit, invoice financing or tokenised stocks and ETFs, and make them available on a blockchain.

There are four parts. ZIG Chain is the blockchain itself: a Cosmos-style network with its own validators, staking and voting, where the token is called ZIG. ZIG Markets is the business arm: it packages and distributes regulated financial products through licensed partners and earns fees for doing so. ZIG Labs invests in and supports teams building apps on ZIG Chain, such as a decentralised exchange and liquid-staking and yield products. $ZIG is the token used to pay transaction fees, to stake with validators (which secures the chain and pays rewards) and to vote on proposals.

Under the "ZIG 2.0" plan that started in July 2026, part of ZIG Markets' revenue is used each month to buy ZIG on the open market; half goes to an ecosystem fund and validators vote on whether the other half is permanently retired. ZIG Finance says it is an ecosystem brand, not itself a regulated firm, and that $ZIG is a utility token, not a share.
