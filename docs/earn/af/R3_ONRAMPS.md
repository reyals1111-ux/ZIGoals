## Summary

Re-verified live on 2026-10-18 (18:43–18:54 UTC). Nothing has changed on the core blocker: no fiat on-ramp sells native ZIG on ZIGChain or any stablecoin on ZIGChain. MoonPay (155 currencies), Ramp (221 assets), Banxa, Mercuryo (56) and Transak (143) list ATOM on Cosmos Hub, ETH and USDC on Ethereum, but no ZIG and no ZIGChain network; Coinbase's public list (413) has no ZIG. Only Guardarian sells ZIG and only as the Ethereum ERC-20 (not re-read today; unsuitable per the prior research).

Recommended pair: Ramp Network and Banxa. Both are MiCA-authorised with Belgium in their passport in ESMA's interim register (CASPS.csv, Last-Modified 16 Oct 2026): Ramp Swaps (Ireland) Ltd, CBI, 02/12/2025, service c only; EU Internet Ventures B.V. (Banxa), AFM, 08/10/2025, services c, d, j. Both hand off with public identifiers only. Ramp's hosted mode is a redirect to https://app.rampnetwork.com/ with hostApiKey, hostAppName, hostLogoUrl (all required, all placed client-side by the docs) plus optional userAddress and finalUrl; no signing is described anywhere, and a URL with parameters returns 200 with no frame-ancestors restriction. Banxa's referral integration is https://{partnerRef}.banxa.com/?walletAddress=…&coinType=ATOM&blockchain=…&fiatType=EUR&returnUrl=… with "No server-to-server calls required", every parameter optional, and no webhooks at all in referral mode. Both are redirects, so the app's CSP needs no change; an iframe would need frame-src plus camera/microphone permissions, which the app's Permissions-Policy (camera=()) forbids today.

MoonPay is excluded on evidence: walletAddress requires a signed URL (HMAC-SHA256 with the secret key), "New on-ramp integrations sign every widget URL", and IP matching (allowedIpAddress) is "mandatory for going live" — a backend is unavoidable. Transak builds the widget URL server-side (single-use session, 5 min) and serves 26 countries with no EEA member. Mercuryo requires a v2:sha512(address+secret+ip+merchant_transaction_id) signature on every on-ramp URL. Coinbase Onramp needs a backend session token (single-use, 5 min) and a Coinbase account for EU users.

The honest route to native ZIG from a card is three signed steps on two chains: buy ATOM to a cosmos1 address (Ramp or Banxa; card within minutes after first-time KYC, SEPA 1–3 business days), IBC-transfer it from Cosmos Hub channel-1555 to ZIGChain channel-4 (both channel ends verified live: counterparties match, client chain ids cosmoshub-4 and zigchain-1), then swap on OroSwap's ATOM/azig pool (zig1wvqk…fe45: 81,145.66 ZIG and 2,366.21 ATOM at block 13042429; a 10-ATOM router simulation returned 341.457 ZIG at block 13042528). Skip Go still has no swap venue on ZIGChain (39 venues). The exchange route is weaker for EU users than the prior doc assumed: KuCoin global withdraws ZIG on chainId "zigchain" (contract "azig", min 20, fee 10 ZIG), but KuCoin EU answers "ZIG currency does not exist" and Bybit EU has no ZIGUSDT; Bitvavo (AFM, Belgium) lists ZIG on ETH only; Bitget and HTX are ERC-20 only; Binance and OKX do not list ZIG. Noble is confirmed winding down (Circle, 10 Sep 2026: minting off 13 Oct 2026, CCTP pause 12 Jan 2027) — do not build on it; MoonPay's usdc_noble is suspended.

Preconditions stand: a legal entity for KYB, the lawyer's answers (MiCA perimeter, FSMA advertising rules, data in the URL), and the Alpha leaving practice-money mode. docs.zigchain.com returned 403 to every reader today, so its exchange table and bridge pages were not re-read.

## 3. Recommended two: Ramp Network and Banxa

**Why these two.** Both are MiCA-authorised with Belgium passported; both hand off through a plain URL whose every parameter is public (wallet address, asset, fiat, amount, return URL); both deliver ATOM on Cosmos Hub, which is the only non-custodial path to native ZIG today (route B); both publish fees, KYC tiers, timing and sandbox test data; neither needs any CSP or Permissions-Policy change because the hand-off is a navigation, not a frame or a fetch. MoonPay, the best-known option and the one behind Keplr's Buy button (help page, 22 Sep 2025: "MoonPay, Transak, and Swapped"), is excluded on its own documentation: signing is mandatory with a wallet address and IP matching is mandatory to go live.

**Which first.** Trade-offs, for the owner and lawyer rather than for us:
- *Ramp:* one fixed hosted host, three required public parameters, fee caps published per method, public assets API that the app could read for a live availability check (CORS allows `https://zigoals.app`). Authorisation covers **service c only** (exchange of crypto-assets for funds) — no "transfer services (j)". Keys come by email after a form, due-diligence documents, proof of incorporation, end-user ToS and a DocuSign contract (blog: initial review 1–2 business days, compliance review 7–10 business days; article dated 2022, so treat as indicative).
- *Banxa:* broader authorisation (c, d, j) and an explicit statement that Banxa "is the regulated entity responsible for KYC and AML compliance"; referral mode has **no webhooks at all**, which matches "ZIGoals receives no order data"; self-serve sandbox and dashboard. Pricing is a spread (typ. 2–4%, up to 15%) on top of a 1.99% card processing fee, so the all-in cost is less predictable; partner subdomain is assigned by Banxa; production is enabled after Banxa approval with no published timeline.

Both should be registered; the one to show first is a legal and commercial decision. Technically the integration is identical: one outbound link per provider, built from public parameters.
