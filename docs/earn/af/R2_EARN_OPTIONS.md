## Summary

Re-verified live on 2026-10-18 (18:43–19:00Z, mainnet height ~13,042,413–13,042,735, zigchaind v5.1.2). Five earn venues exist on ZIGChain today; two fit ZIGoals' non-custodial, user-signs rule now, one is a DEX, and the rest are vault/RWA products needing legal review.

(a) Native validator staking is live and fully readable without keys: every input of the APR is on the LCD (annual_provisions 24,440,008 ZIG/yr, bonded 425,559,498 ZIG of 2,337,754,316 supply = 18.20% bonded, community_tax 2%, commissions 5–10%). Computed from those inputs the gross rate is 5.743%/yr before tax and commission (5.63% after tax; 5.35% at a 5% commission) — a derivation, not a published figure; docs.zigchain.com's own APR page is now behind a Cloudflare challenge (403 to curl and WebFetch), as are hub.zigchain.com and staking.zigchain.com. 14 bonded validators, none jailed, 4 "Foundation" validators hold 63.2% of stake; self-bonds range from 1 ZIG to 100,000 ZIG; signing_infos show 0 missed blocks except Numia (2). All six listed public LCDs return access-control-allow-origin: *. Keplr publishes a chain-suggestion JSON with a staking deep link.

(b) Valdora stZIG is live: staker zig18nnde5…k7ue55 (code 179, v1.1.0), 194,892,764 ZIG delegated across 4 ledgers to the 4 Foundation validators, stZIG supply 181,660,424 (6 dec), redemption quote 1 stZIG = 1.072841 ZIG (reverse_st_zig_price, DefiLlama's documented interpretation), DEX rate 1.0666 on OroSwap's deep stZIG/ZIG pool. On-chain the wasm admin, contract admin and treasury are the same single secp256k1 key zig1gy5zk…02kx, while docs still say "multi-signature". Fee 10% of rewards (fees_percentage 1000); docs contradict themselves on an unstake fee. Unbonding 21 days + "2 days for Valdora distribution"; minimum 50 ZIG; the staker's query set has no state/config/exchange_rate/params variants. Valdora's own site now says "ZIG Finance" (a 2026-09-23 rebrand per secondary coverage) and runs a referral/points program.

(c) OroSwap DEX is live (factory 2-of-3 multisig owner, 9 pair configs, fee tiers 0.01–2%, 20% of fees to the maker). ZIG/USDC xyk_25 holds 846,649 ZIG + 42,372 USDC; ZIG/ATOM xyk 81,146 ZIG + 2,366 ATOM; no active farms (incentives active_pools = []). Its REST API (api-mainnet.oroswap.org) is Cloudflare-gated (403), so pool reads must use CosmWasm smart queries; DefiLlama gives TVL/APY with CORS *.

(d) Zignaly is shutting down: new investments stopped 2026-10-15, all trading services/Z-Indexes closed 2026-10-16 10:00 UTC, Private Credit withdrawal requests by 2026-11-01, app closes 2026-12-01 23:59 UTC. Treat as information-only/ended.

(e) Other live dApps on DefiLlama: Valdora Vaults (USDC, 3 vaults, $67.8M AUM, curated by ZIG Markets, up to 60-day redemption), Nawa Protocol (stZIG vault and a USDC vault whose funds are "forwarded off-chain to Zignaly" per its DefiLlama adapter, $3.46M), DeFa by InvoiceMate (private mainnet, no retail app, $2.1M), Permapod lending (Mars-style red bank, TVL ≈ 0, an exploit noted on its X account, main market "migrated to a residual-sweeper after the hack" per its adapter). ZIG Markets is a distribution layer with a contact form only. No launchpad, NFT, perps or prediction market was verified live (Permapod has a deployed "perps" contract; MemesDotFun's site shows no ZIGChain reference).

Framing: valdora.finance, app.oroswap.org and zigscan.org send no X-Frame-Options/CSP; Nawa, Permapod app, Keplr and NodeStake explorer send DENY/frame-ancestors 'none'; Cloudflare-challenged hosts send SAMEORIGIN on the challenge page. Wallet flows must never be framed regardless.
