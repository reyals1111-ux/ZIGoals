# Earn & staking: what the repository already knows (Session N, 2026-10-02)

This survey collects what the committed repository already says about earning and staking, so Session N can see which records still hold. It covers Valdora and stZIG, native ZIGChain staking, vaults, stablecoins (Noble USDC, USDT, Axelar, Ondo), OroSwap, on-ramps, Zignaly, ZIG Markets / ZIG Finance and the related legal questions. It reflects `main` at `57275a6` (the Alpha deploy #23 source), read on 2026-10-02 without running, building or fetching anything; the rest of this PR (the evidence, the design, ADR-009, the registry update, the Valdora testnet reader and section 6 of `docs/business/LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md`) is left out. Line numbers are those of `57275a6`, except in `LEGAL_CHECKLIST.md`, where lines after section 5 are given as they are after this PR adds section 6. Dates come from the document itself, or from its last commit when marked "(git)". Citations are `file:line`; a bare `:N` means line N of that row's file. The last column compares each source with facts verified on official sources today, cited by evidence id in brackets (each links to its entry in [EVIDENCE_2026-10.md](EVIDENCE_2026-10.md)'s registers). **Outdated** means the fact has changed since the source was written. **Contradicted** means today's evidence disagrees with what the source records. **Superseded** means newer evidence replaces it, in part when marked "partly". **Missing** means a relevant fact the source lacks. **Still right** means it matches. **Check** means today's facts do not settle it.
