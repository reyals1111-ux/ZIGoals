## Help answers (`components/help/help-page.tsx`, a new section `aa` "New: habits, health and the game layer", one `<Question>` each)
- **How do the time-of-day sections work?** Each habit has a time of day in its editor (morning, afternoon, evening, any time). The Habits page and Today's habits group them; the section the clock is in opens first and a fold you choose is remembered on this device. "All" and the other filters work as before.
- **What does the timer's Finish do?** Finish stops the timer and logs today's check-in with the minutes it measured, in one tap. It survives a reload and a page change. An optional end bell plays outside your quiet hours; turn it on per habit.
- **What is "days since"?** On a quit habit the card counts the days since the last slip, keeps your longest run, shows the slips on a calendar and, if you enter what the habit cost per day, an estimate of the money kept. It is your own figure times days, nothing else.
- **Can I skip a day with a reason, or add a note and mood?** Yes: "Skip today" takes a reason; a check-in can carry a note and a mood. Both show in the habit's history. A skip does not break a streak the way a miss does.
- **What is a routine?** A chain of habits played one after the other, with the timer where a habit has minutes. Four templates (morning, evening, workout, focus) create the habits for you; you can also start from any stack. A routine played to its end counts once in the game layer.
- **What is the game layer, and is it on?** Off for everyone until you turn it on under Settings → Your pages & buttons → Game layer (the Showcase has it on). XP comes only from real actions in your journals and is never stored, so Undo takes it back. Levels have names, three daily quests come from the pages you show, weekly challenges compare you with your own last week, badges keep only the day you first earned them, and a streak freeze is earned, never bought. Nothing is ever taken away and nothing nags.
- **Where is the Journey page?** /app/journey, in the sidebar while the game layer is on: the level ring, XP this week, quests, the badge cabinet and the path ahead.
- **What are the weight trend and the energy estimate?** The trend smooths your readings (an exponential moving average) and shows the weekly rate and your goal line. The energy estimate compares a month of logged intake with the trend's change; it needs at least fourteen logged days and six readings, shows a band, and is an estimate, not medical advice.
- **What does the weekly coach ask?** Once a week it sums up the week in one sentence and asks: keep the calorie target or adjust it. "Adjust" writes the target through the same form you already use. Your answer stays on this device.
- **Micronutrients and macro views?** Targets for the seven nutrients the journal has (fiber, sugar, saturated fat, sodium, potassium, calcium, iron); unknown stays unknown, never zero. The macro view can show net carbs, a keto split or your own split; it changes nothing in your records.
- **How does the planner work?** Plan a recipe or a food into a day and meal; the grocery list groups what you need by aisle (you can move an item to another aisle), ticks off what you bought, and one tap logs the planned meal when you eat it.
- **Can ZIGoals import a recipe?** Paste the text of a recipe and confirm the lines it found; or paste a web address and the app reads the recipe the page publishes for search engines (name, ingredients, yield only; https pages only, never private addresses). Imported ingredients become foods with unknown nutrition until you fill them in.
- **What is the fasting timeline?** The hours of your current or last fast with neutral marks at 12, 16, 18 and 24 hours. It makes no health claims.
- **Measurements and progress photos?** Charts per kind, your own kinds, and progress photos that stay on this device (they never sync; they are in Export everything and erased with the account). The sleep score shows its formula; the water ring uses your own sizes.
- **Why does a swipe not delete?** On a phone a swipe right completes, a swipe left only reveals Edit, Skip and Archive; delete always opens the confirmation and offers Undo. A fast flick never deletes anything.
- **What does the new welcome set up, and can I undo it?** You pick what matters and two or three focuses; the welcome creates matching habits, goals and Today widgets. For a day "Undo setup" on the welcome card removes exactly what it created, and only that.
- **Where are my reminders?** Settings → Reminders: habit and water reminders, bedtime, the weekly review, a fast's end, a goal check-in, and quiet hours. Reminders show on Today; push delivery stays off until the owner activates it.
- **What can ZIGi do now?** Every new action above as a card: move a habit to the morning, start or finish a timer, start a routine, set a habit's area or a quit habit's cost, use a streak freeze, set a micronutrient target or the macro view, answer the coach, import a pasted recipe, turn the game layer on or off, and open the Journey, the year view, routines, the planner, insights, import and reminders, in English and Dutch, typed or spoken. A recipe web address, money, weight, fasting and deletions never auto-accept.
