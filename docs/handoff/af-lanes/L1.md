## Findings and open items
- **APR correction vs the brief's measured figure.** As specified (H9, H51), APR = provisions ÷ bonded × (1 − tax) ×
  (real blocks/yr ÷ `blocks_per_year`) × (1 − commission). On the recorded read (block 13,044,950) that is 5.743 % gross →
  5.628 % after tax → × 0.9468 (3.171 s blocks over 10,000 blocks, 365.25-day year) = **5.33 % before commission, 5.06 % at
  5 %**. The brief's "measured net 5.19–5.21 % at 5 %" is ~3 % higher than this: the block-time correction alone does not
  explain H51's measured accrual (3.0 ÷ 3.17 = 0.946, while 5.207 ÷ 5.347 = 0.974). The page shows the formula's 5.06 %
  with every input on tap; the accrual check belongs to the later live-check script. **A decision for the lead/owner**,
  not guessed here.
- The Phase R verdict tables look shuffled across tracks (R2's LB1–LB10 verdicts discuss authz, REStake, wasm upload
  params, wallet-standard…; R1's LB11–LB14 verdicts match R2's claims). Facts used here were re-read live today.
- yields.llama.fi/pools is about 11 MB / 16,368 rows: the route parses it once per 5 minutes per isolate (cap 24 MB), well
  inside the Worker's 2,000 ms CPU limit, but it is the heaviest read; pinned per-pool `/chart/<id>` reads would be lighter
  if the owner prefers (the brief asked for /pools).
