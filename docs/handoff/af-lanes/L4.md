## What is built, against the brief
1. **Daily Briefing v2 + Evening Recap (P8.2).** `lib/ai/proactive/{brief,brief-sources}.ts`: the morning brief gains
   sections (Checklist dots, meals planned, timers running, goals needing a step, earning, one note "You said: …"); the
   evening recap (from 18:00 by default: habits done/open, streaks kept, Health counts only with the gate, Checklist,
   earning, "One for tomorrow"). On Today the For-you slot shows both in one `div.zigi-brief-stack` (recap above the
   morning brief, each with Open ZIGi and Not today); `/brief` and `/recap` (and "what's my plan today", "how did today
   go", "hoe ging vandaag", "wat heb ik vandaag gedaan") answer in the chat on the device; "Read aloud" speaks once on a
   tap, never by itself, rests in ZIGi's quiet hours and when muted. Settings → ZIGi → "Your day" (`details#zigi-your-day`):
   morning brief on Today, evening recap on Today, its time, the earning line, read aloud a brief I ask for; kept as
   `brief` on `zigoals:zigi:v1`; `set-brief` is the card kind.
2. **Plans (P8.4).** `plan-bundle` (≤ 10 steps; goal, ≤ 4 habits with reminders, milestones, ≤ 6 Checklist items, ≤ 4
   favourites, ≤ 2 Remember cards, one Earn step) expands into ordinary cards under one head ("Your 10k plan · 6 steps",
   "Add the steps you want; nothing is written until you add one."), per-step Add/Edit/Dismiss, "Add all" for the writing
   steps (never a form, a device record or a staking review), "Skip the rest"; a cut says so; one plan per reply; a bad
   step is refused alone. Outcomes per step in `zigoals:zigi-plans:v1` (≤ 20 plans, loose, versioned; written only when
   the person acts); Settings → "ZIGi's plans" (`details#zigi-plans`) lists them with Forget and Forget all.
3. **Checklist by voice and text (P8.6).** `lib/ai/quick-commands/commands.ts`: EN and NL templates ("mark push-ups
   done", "vink push-ups af", "kun je push-ups afvinken", "zet 5 bladzijden lezen op mijn lijst", "favourite walk",
   "start my sleep timer", "start mijn slaaptimer", timers, fasts, "what's on my checklist"), decided on the device with
   a stop rule (whole message, no question mark or question word, no second clause, no other day, no amount, exactly one
   item). Kinds `checklist-tick`, `checklist-add-favourite`, `checklist-add-custom` through L3's command surface and
   `registerDeviceWrite` (`lib/ai/actions/checklist-writes.ts`); a habit dot is the habit's own check-in with Undo;
   Health dots only with the gate. `OPEN_PAGES += checklist, earn`.
4. **Earn coach (P8.5; H47, H69, D7).** `lib/earn/snapshot.ts` (memory only) is written by `use-earn-live.ts`'s one edit;
   `earn_rates` and the glossary quote `{value, source, asOf}` from it and nothing else; without a snapshot the answer
   says where the figure is and carries no digit. Glossary EN/NL (APR, APY, unbonding, staking, validator, commission,
   slashing, liquid staking, impermanent loss, pools, slippage, gas, the decoy ZIG, custody); "the ways to earn" in the
   Earn page's order with a risk each, never ranked; Start here. `prefill-staking` (validator, amount, network, action;
   `AUTO_ACCEPT_NEVER`) stashes L2a's `zigoals:ai:staking-prefill:v1` and opens `/app/staking#staking-actions-title`; the
   staking form reads it once. Signing, sending and broadcasting are refused in plain words with no card.
5. **Page awareness (P8.7).** `lib/ai/context/page-view.ts`: the route and the cards' ids and titles (figures stripped),
   Today's named regions as cards (once; never a card's own parts or the page head), a preset widget judged by its title,
   Health cards only with the gate, nothing on Settings or a sensitive screen; "what am I looking at" and "explain this
   card" on the device from `card-help.ts`; other page questions take one removable "This page" chip (ids and titles,
   1,500 characters). `AskZigiItem` gains a `card` prop (`ExplainCardItem`) for a card menu's "Ask ZIGi about this".
6. **Memory v2 (P8.3, H30).** `consent` on `zigoals:ai-memory:v1`: the first Remember card says how notes are kept
   (MEMORY_FIRST) and the person's Remember is their agreement; What ZIGi knows → "Notes from ZIGi's cards" switch;
   declined, a Remember card is refused in plain words (MEMORY_DECLINED); "Used in today's brief" names the note the brief
   quotes; plans may carry Remember cards; Export unchanged; never synced.
7. **Stretch (Dutch on-device answers):** Checklist commands and the Earn coach answer in Dutch; the brief and recap
   answer Dutch questions in English (not built).

Also: "Add all" and auto-accept now apply each write on what the step before it left (`use-proposals.ts` `apply(plan,
auto, prior)`), so a plan's new habit and its reminder go in one tap; before, the reminder failed with "Add the habit …
first" until a re-render. The runner's own comment always said "in order on the latest stores".

Also: a timer stop said with "… and log it" ("stop the Exercise timer and log it", "stop de timer van X en log het") is the
device's stop card (stopping logs the time). With the timer commands on the device, `tests/zigi-accept-correct.spec.ts`'s
stop-timer setup ("Start the timer on Exercise") no longer reaches its MOCK server; its stop ask is now answered on the
device too, so that spec passes unchanged (it checks the records, not the requests). In the app, the AA corpus's timer
asks ("Start the timer on my reading", "Stop the exercise timer and log it") are answered on the device; the harness
still scores them against the model.

And, for L5a's theme parity: `components/zigi/zigi-figure.tsx` imports its own `zigi.css` (the figure's shadow in Nebula
on a new device's Today and Goals); the lead removes the matching entries from `tests/parity/known.ts`.
