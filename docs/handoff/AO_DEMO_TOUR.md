# Session AO — demo tour (15 minutes)

Run it locally: `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ENVIRONMENT=LOCAL_DEMO pnpm dev`, then open http://127.0.0.1:3100/app. Settings → **Load
Showcase Demo** fills the app with fictional example data (everything stays in that tab).

1. **Settings, first group: Appearance.** The theme switch is on the first screen at 800 px. Switch to **Dark**: flat
   near-black, no stars or mist, the same colours as Nebula. Switch back to **Nebula**: the cosmic sky returns.
2. **Settings → Your app → My day ends at.** Choose 3:00. The line under it says what a change does: past days never move,
   and the day in progress keeps the later end. Habits, the Checklist and streaks now count until 03:00.
3. **Wealth at 1440.** The long sections are folded rows (about half the old length). Open "Your wealth over time": it stays
   open next time on this device. Inside it, **Net worth by source** shows the Showcase's labelled example history with gaps
   for days not opened and "No value" where a price was unknown.
4. **Today.** Each goal is named once (the focus tile names the top goal with its progress). In "Your week", a habit cell
   opens that day's habits with **Check in**. "Skip to a part of Today" jumps to Goals, the Checklist, widgets or the week.
5. **Habits → Open perfect days.** Today's "n of m", the current run, and a month you can browse; a day lists its habits.
6. **Goals → New goal.** Three steps (Your goal · What counts · Review, the rhythm optional inside the review); "Search all
   130 goal templates". On a goal, **Fund Goal → Cash** is two screens: amount, then Preview → Confirm.
7. **Activity.** "Search activity" filters the rows; **Export CSV** saves exactly what is shown, made on the device.
8. **A card's ⋯** is a real menu: arrow keys move, Escape returns to ⋯. A Wealth holding's ⋯ ends with **Edit**.
9. **On a phone (390 px):** the notice reads "PUBLIC ALPHA · On this device", whole on one line; Earn and Journey fold their
   long sections; Portfolio's holdings are cards up to 900 px.
