## Method and evidence
- **Build:** `NEXT_PUBLIC_APP_ENVIRONMENT=PUBLIC_ALPHA_UNDEPLOYED pnpm build`, then `next start` on 127.0.0.1. The swept build is `436536e` (#57 plus Part 1's sync fix, which changes no layout); Parts 2–3 change no app code.
- **Tooling:**
  - Node 24.19.0, pnpm 11.19.0, Playwright 1.63 with the `chrome` channel. Locally that channel is the sandbox's Chromium 141.
  - One browser at a time. The drivers are scratch scripts and are not committed; the one bug became a failing-first browser test.
- **Sizes:** desktop 1440×900 and 1024×768; tablet 768×1024 (touch); phones 390×844 and 360×800 (mobile, touch).
- **Pages:** Today, Goals, a Goal detail, Habits, Health, Wealth, an asset detail, Markets, Staking, Portfolio, Ecosystem, Activity and Settings.
- **Data:** the Showcase demo; an empty profile; and the power-user dataset (45 habits / 12,915 check-ins, 3 years of Health, 200 positions, restored through Settings).
- **Also checked:**
  - keyboard: 30 Tab stops from the top of each of 11 pages at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390, each with a visible focus ring and on screen;
  - screen-reader names: every visible control has an accessible name, one `h1` per page, no image without `alt`, no duplicate `id`;
  - reduced motion and Motion Off (nothing animating), and motion on (what still runs 1.8 s after load must be one-time);
  - display locales en-US, nl-BE and ja-JP;
  - phones: touch targets of at least 44 px, inputs of at least 16 px, labels and body text of at least 14 px;
  - timing on the same machine: `fc906e8` (#54, Alpha deploy #20) against this branch.
- **No provider:** every `/api/**` request was answered with a 503 fixture, so "no prices" is the tested state.
- **Evidence labels:**
  - **browser:** reproduced in the production build;
  - **unit:** a failing-first or regression test in this PR;
  - **code:** found by reading the code, not reproduced.

## For the owner (visual, product or wording; nothing changed)
1. **QA2-02, wording:** give Health and the reminder cards the same coded storage messages as Habits, Staking and Portfolio (a small UI change; the codes already exist).
2. **QA2-03, money:** should a loss be cut away from zero (−$12.35), so that "never reads better than it is" holds for losses too? Today it is cut toward zero, by at most one minor unit.
3. **QA2-04, reminders:** keep "device clock for the time, journal zone for the day" (as documented), or move reminders to the journal's zone with the timezone project.
4. **QA2-05, landing:** the FAQ line "the Goal Layer for ZIGChain" (`landing/index.html:116`) is live copy; replace it with the slogan, or keep it.
5. **QA2-06, Staking:** drop the Goal workspace tabs on Staking, or mark "Positions" as the current tab there.
6. **QA2-07, phone targets:** a padding pass on list-row links and the workspace tabs (layout change on phones only); all of them predate #57.
7. **QA2-08, Wealth timing:** worth a profile with 200 positions before the next performance pass; nothing else got slower.

## Checks that passed
- **Logo fold intro:** covered by `logo-fold.spec.ts` and `lib/logo-intro.test.ts` (CI green on `436536e`); not driven again here beyond the page audits, which found no running animation under reduced motion and Motion Off.
- **Page marks and navigation groups:** the right mark on every life page, the wordmark elsewhere (Part 6 changes this by owner decision); groups by space, not lines; tab order follows the layout (keyboard probe).
- **Glass bars and rings, quick counters:** rendered at every size (screenshots); the Health quick counters already queue taps one after another, each on the latest saved data, so no tap is lost there (code).
- **Money minor digits:** Wealth's total reads "US$ 501.800,00" and "+ € 8.000,00 held in EUR · not converted" in nl-BE, and "$501,800.00" and "€8,000.00" in ja-JP and en-US; the two currencies are never added. Prices below one unit keep four significant digits (code).
- **Storage errors:** Habits, Staking and Portfolio end refusals with their code (browser specs `storage-errors`, `habit-paint-first`); see QA2-02 for Health.
- **Ecosystem cards, Staking redirect and Valdora:** named regions and headings; `/app/goals/positions` answers 307 to `/app/staking` (spec); the Valdora card reads, connects and estimates nothing.
- **The film:** nothing downloads before it opens; a named modal dialog with a paused video and its controls; it fits the window at every size; Escape closes it and focus returns to "See how it works".
- **Reminders:** cards appear only after the device-clock time and only while the habit is due (unit tests and `reminders.spec.ts`); see QA2-04.
- **Phone sheets and folds:** the habit editor opens as a bottom sheet holding focus, and Escape gives focus back to "+ New habit" (390 and 360); Today's "How it works" fold opens in place.
- **QA-23, the offline notice:** appears when the browser goes offline (`role="alert"`), stays in view while scrolling, and leaves when back online, at all five sizes; in-app links stay on the page while offline (`qa-decisions.spec.ts`).
- **QA-35 to QA-38:** covered by `qa-decisions.spec.ts` and the unit tests from #57, green in CI.
- **Portfolio:** on this device only; prices from the Showcase fixture or the public quotes, otherwise "Unknown"; nothing it holds reaches Wealth, Goals or Today (`portfolio.spec.ts`).
- **Keyboard:** 30 Tab stops on each of 11 pages at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390: focus ring on every stop and never off screen. The calendar button inside Health's date field shows Chromium's own focus ring rather than the app's.
- **Settings file pickers** use 14 px text on phones; they open the system picker and never zoom the page, unlike text fields (all text fields are 16 px).
