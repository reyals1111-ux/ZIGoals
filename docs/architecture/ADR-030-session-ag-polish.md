## Decisions taken without asking
- **J1 — The branch.** The owner named `feature/session-ag-polish`; the session's harness branch (`claude/clever-pasteur-1gmmiv`) is left untouched, as the brief says.
- **J2 — The `frontend-design` skill is not available in this sandbox.** Its rules (a committed aesthetic direction, typographic hierarchy, purposeful motion, no generic defaults) are applied by hand to Parts 1–8, as Session AF did (ADR-028 H3).
- **J3 — The progress arc's root cause.** `path.css` sizes `.path-dot-ring` at `calc(100% + 14px)` with `inset: -7px`; the orbit sheet moved the inset to `-2px` but kept the size, so the arc's box was 10 px too large and anchored at its top-left corner: its centre sat 5 px right of and 5 px below the ring at every width, most visible on a phone's 62 px node (reference 16). The orbit sheet now gives the arc exactly the ring's border box, and the circle runs on the ring's own 2 px track.
- **J4 — Gold displays as pink.** The Checklist's palette leaves gold out. A stored or default gold (custom items, fasts, Wealth and Earn items) is shown as pink, the nebula hue nearest to gold (about 74° away on the hue wheel, against magenta's 109° and teal's 125°), before the no-equal-neighbours pass, so the stored colour is never rewritten. The shared `PATH_COLOURS` table is unchanged, so gold stays for Wealth and Earn elsewhere.
- **J5 — Why the celebration did not always fire, and the rule that replaces it.** It played only when the day went from incomplete to complete while the Checklist was open and its first summary of the day had been incomplete. So it missed a day completed elsewhere (Today's card, a habit's page, ZIGi with the Checklist closed), a first summary that already read complete (a reload right after the last tick, a store loading late), and any tab where `sessionStorage` threw (the guard then read "already celebrated"). The new rule is driven by state, not by a transition: once the Checklist, its stores and the UI preferences have loaded, today is complete and `checklist.celebratedOn` is not today, it plays and stores today's date (once per day per device). The tab-only `zigoals-checklist-celebrated` key is no longer read or written.
- **J6 — The glyph table and its order.** 29 new line glyphs (24-grid, the table's 1.9 stroke): glass, pages, bike, swim, yoga, stretch, bed, pill-bottle, salad, no-coffee, no-sugar, heart-rate, tooth, shower, phone-off, journal, gratitude, language, piano, guitar, brush, code, chess, savings-jar, chart, wallet, stake, bill, budget; the concepts the table already had (book, run, dumbbell, lotus, moon, hourglass, clock, target, pill, apple, scale, heart, sun, note, coin, star, footsteps, drop) are reused. `orbitGlyph` reads the name's words first (specific phrases before the broad words inside them — "brush teeth" before brush, "heart rate" before heart — with word edges so breakfast, brunch, start, embedded and Sunday never match), then the health input, then the kind, then the page's own mark, then a star. The page-mark step no longer runs the older loose word list (`itemGlyph`), which had turned "Get ready early" into a book and "No drinking" into a drop; the new word table covers its words. "Move €200 to savings" now shows the savings jar (it shares "save" with the owner's "Save €10"); vitamins and supplements show the pill, as the owner asked ("Vitamins & Creatine" → pill), so `pill-bottle` is drawn but unused for now.
- **J7 — Matte includes the next node.** Request 1c makes every node not yet done matte, no glow. Session AG had kept the next node's ring "half awake" with a soft glow; it is now matte too, its ring only a touch brighter than the others (26 % of its colour into the matte tint, no glow). The NEXT tag, its slightly larger size and ZIGi beside it still say which node is next. The matte ring is the node's hue at 78 % lightness and 28 % chroma (oklch), at least 3:1 against the night sky for every hue in the palette (tested). The lit glyph's glow is the nebula's own (blue-violet and a white core), not the node's colour.
- **J8 — Today's card wears the node.** Today's Checklist card showed the next item as a filled disc in its colour with a dark mark; a nebula glyph on a bright fill would not read, so the card now shows the path's own node there (dark glass, its ring in the item's colour, the nebula glyph; the same in every theme).
- **J9 — The phone's lanes.** Below 641 px each node stands in a lane chosen by `lib/path/phone-lanes.ts` (pure, unit-tested) from the stage's width and each name tab's measured one-line width (the check's room always counted, so a tick moves nothing): short names keep a rhythm across centre, right, centre and left lanes; a long name moves its node toward the left so its tab fits on one line; a tab wraps to two lines (check and words left-aligned) only where even the leftmost lane is too narrow, which happens at 320 px for the longest names. The lanes reserve ZIGi's room on the side away from the tab. Where a long name leaves no such room, ZIGi stands above the name on its side (`data-zigi-slot="above"`), clear of node and name, instead of pointing across the name. Nodes stand about 170 px apart (AG: 96) and each orbit's banner gets its own band. The computer keeps AG's wave and sides exactly. Two AF-era assertions move with this, at equal strength: a phone's opening screen holds three or more nodes, not six (the owner asked for a longer, airier journey); and the "ZIGi points at the next planet" check applies when ZIGi has its side slot, while the above-the-name slot is checked for 12 px clearance from node and name and nearness (60 px or less).
- **J10 — The phone's dock.** One row of 48 px chips under 420 px (icon over the word, as before but slimmer) and 44 px chips with the icon beside the word from 420 to 640 px; the bar is 64 or 58 px tall (AG: 72). The path's end keeps 136 px below it, so the finale and its name scroll clear of the dock and the toast's row.
- **J11 — The Habits banner, shared without touching the Habits page.** The Habits page's banner parts (symbol, nebula title, count, chevron) are one component, `HabitSectionBannerParts`, which the Habits page's `<summary>` now renders: the same markup, classes and order as before (amendment 2; the Habits specs pass unchanged). The Checklist's orbits render the same parts in a plain block with the Habits page's own classes, and the Checklist's path view loads the Habits page's own stylesheet (`habits-aa.css`; all its selectors are `habit-*` and `routine-*`), so the banner is the Habits banner, not a lookalike, and the Habits page's rules stay where they were. On the Checklist only: no box of its own, AG's fade, the count right after the title ("Complete ✓" once done, with one light sweep), titles "Morning Orbit" and so on, and, for the no-gold rule, the morning's gold glow and the afternoon's pale-yellow sun take their nebula neighbours (pink and ice cyan). The Habits page keeps its own colours.
- **J12 — The orbit planets.** The owner's seven planets were cut by a committed script (`scripts/checklist/prepare-planets.py`, Python with Pillow and numpy, no new dependency): each sphere is fitted to an exact disc with a feathered 1.25 px edge (no starfield, nebula, rectangle or dark fringe; checked on white), and the front-ring strip across it is retouched away, because every ring and orbit is redrawn in SVG from measured ellipses (`lib/path/planet-designs.ts`) for correct front and back occlusion; the moons are their own small images. Each planet weighs 8–16 kB in all (budget 60 kB, unit-tested), as a 256 px sphere and a 128 px one for phones (`srcset`), lazily. A planet stands on the line at its banner, before its orbit's first node (the line ends at its top rim and starts again at its bottom rim, unit- and browser-tested); its design comes from the first node's colour (`lib/path/planet-pick.ts`). On a computer it stands beside the banner; where the banner leaves too little room (a phone) it wraps under it, right-aligned, still on the line, in its own band. Mist and glow are CSS gradients in the planet's hues, so Light's white sky shows no muddy halo. Moons and bodies travel by a short `requestAnimationFrame` run (about 3 s, once) along their tilted ellipses, each drawn as a far copy under the sphere and a near copy over it, so the sphere hides the far side.
- **J13 — The phone's Checklist pill (owner addition, 22 Oct).** The pill sits first among the header's actions (between the logo and "+"), in the footer link-pill style, as a theme island. It fits by measuring: where the full pill would push the header's icons off a narrow phone (360 px with the wallet shortcut, or with the offline pill), it shows the check alone (44 px, its name "Open the Checklist" unchanged) and brings the word back as soon as there is room (375 px and wider with the wallet shortcut). It follows the launcher's "off" setting and is absent on the Checklist itself. The launcher's mark, and with it the phone pill's and Today's card's, is now one bold nebula check; the launcher's circle is unchanged.
- **J14 — The List view.** Done rows move, about a second after their bloom, into a "Done · n" fold, closed by default as Todoist does; Undo stays in the toast. "+" logs the item's usual amount (the stepper's own suggested value) through the same tick as the stepper. The ⋯ opens the existing details or stepper panel. Swipe right uses the app's own `SwipeRow`.
- **J15 — The celebration's ZIGi.** The owner asked for the animated `F009-celebrate` clip. That clip is 96 × 126 px, so drawn at 160–200 px it would be soft for the whole moment; it plays during the 4.5 s sequence and then crossfades to the same pose's 480 px still for the final composition (and under reduced motion and Motion Off that still alone). The choice lives in one file (`components/checklist/celebration-zigi.ts`), so the arms-up art from Studio-4 is a two-line swap.
- **J16 — The celebration on an already finished day.** By the owner's rule ("otherwise the next time the Checklist opens that day"), opening a day finished elsewhere plays the celebration and stores the day in `checklist.celebratedOn`. That is the one write a view of the Checklist can make, and only once per day per device; Continue and Escape close it (amendment 3). The days a test fixture or the Showcase opens unfinished never trigger it.
- **J17 — The phone's destination planet.** Phones and tablets now show the computer's destination art (pointer 9) from a 640 px copy (16 kB, `public/art/checklist/destination-640.webp`); the image's `sizes` names the phone's file even on a high-density screen (the art is a soft backdrop, low on the right behind the path), so a phone never downloads the 47 kB original.
- **J18 — Words by day, and the banner's band.** Owner amendment 4 keeps the celebration's hues, planets and bodies the same in every theme on each theme's own sky; its words are not hues, so in Light the title and line are ink (#10183a, #3b4566) and the gradient "lit!" keeps its stops with a faint ink shadow (the parity test compares the paint of the orbs, planets, ring, "lit!" and Continue, which stay identical). In the same way the orbit banner's band keeps a night ground by day, like the name tabs, so the nebula title reads on Light's white sky; the banner is a theme island and has no card of its own in any theme (the Light self-check, captures 6–11).
- **J19 — The layout audit's fixes (Part 9).** The audit of `/app/checklist` (path, List, Edit path, empty; 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px; three themes; touch at 768 and 1024) found five things, all fixed: the List's checkbox was a 34 px target and its "+" 36 px tall (now a 44 px button around the path's own 34 px node, and a 44 px "+"; the List spec asserts both); a row's second line ended in an ellipsis at 320 px (it wraps now); on phones the List's rows of words ran over the destination art, unreadable by day (the art is the path's backdrop and is left out of the List view in every theme); the phone's art showed its picture edges on Light's white sky (it now melts away round on every side, all themes); and the banners' gradient titles fell just under 3:1 where each symbol's glow (the Habits page's 70 px) reached under the title, and in Light over a lighter band (the glows stay round their symbols at 48 px; by day the band is the night ground with its tint at 65 %). Dropping the Habits section card's class had also dropped the evening and anytime tints; they are restored on the orbit banner. After the fixes the audit is clean in all three themes (lowest gradient word 3.58:1).
- **J20 — A smaller "+" in the phone header (owner request, 22 Oct).** The owner asked for the header's quick-add "+" to be slightly smaller, to fit the logo and the new Checklist pill. The 44 px touch rule stays (non-negotiable 6), so what is drawn shrinks and the target does not: the "+" circle is drawn at 36 px (a 1 px nebula ring, its glow following the circle) inside its 44 px button, and the pill's glass likewise at 36 px inside its 44 px link, the height of the logo (34 px). This is the app shell's phone header, outside the Checklist's lane; the owner's own request makes it the second exception to non-negotiable 10 after the pill. Behaviour, size of target and theme parity unchanged (the pill spec and the parity rows pass).
- **J21 — The Checklist specs seed on a page with no app code.** `webkit-checklist:11` failed twice in WebKit (CI on `961f32de` and `4322f55c`; it passed on `9e98adc0`), each time with the same three page errors: the app shell's music, push and account-status reads on the seeding Settings page, cancelled "due to access control checks" when the test navigated on. A navigation that cancels requests in flight is WebKit's known fault (ADR-025 E28, ADR-027 G23). This PR's header pill shifts the timing on that page slightly, though it adds no request delay (measured in Chromium). The second failure ruled out a one-off, and waiting longer would be a guess, so the cause is removed instead: `seedChecklist` writes the storage on `/robots.txt`, a same-origin page with no app code, so nothing is in flight when the test moves on. Every page error is still counted, and the test's assertion is unchanged. All specs that use the fixture pass locally (64 + 56, with the two WebKit specs run in Chromium); WebKit itself is judged by CI.
- **J22 — The pill as served, and no prefetch.** CI's WebKit job on `f82bb4b8` found two things the pill caused. (1) Since `f1f9e033` the pill is in the page as served, with its word, so on a 320 or 360 px phone the header spilled (363 px) until the script measured it and let the word go; WebKit measured in that gap (`zigi-oneline-z:257`, "past the edge: div.phone-actions:361"). Below 375 px the pill now starts as the check alone in CSS, and the first measure shows the word only if it fits: the header fits as served and after hydration at 320, 360, 375 and 390 px (measured with and without JavaScript). (2) The same three cancelled shell reads came up in `webkit-phone` too, which does not use the Checklist seed. The only network the pill adds to every phone page is Next's background prefetch of `/app/checklist`, so the pill no longer prefetches. That matches the shell's own prefetch rule (`components/nav-prefetch.ts`: every prefetch is a Worker call on the Alpha, and destinations that do not need it skip it). Opening the Checklist from the pill now loads its route on tap, as Settings and Goals do.
