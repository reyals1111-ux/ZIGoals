## Decisions taken without asking
- **J1 — The branch.** The owner named `feature/session-ag-polish`; the session's harness branch (`claude/clever-pasteur-1gmmiv`) is left untouched, as the brief says.
- **J2 — The `frontend-design` skill is not available in this sandbox.** Its rules (a committed aesthetic direction, typographic hierarchy, purposeful motion, no generic defaults) are applied by hand to Parts 1–8, as Session AF did (ADR-028 H3).
- **J3 — The progress arc's root cause.** `path.css` sizes `.path-dot-ring` at `calc(100% + 14px)` with `inset: -7px`; the orbit sheet moved the inset to `-2px` but kept the size, so the arc's box was 10 px too large and anchored at its top-left corner: its centre sat 5 px right of and 5 px below the ring at every width, most visible on a phone's 62 px node (reference 16). The orbit sheet now gives the arc exactly the ring's border box, and the circle runs on the ring's own 2 px track.
- **J4 — Gold displays as pink.** The Checklist's palette leaves gold out. A stored or default gold (custom items, fasts, Wealth and Earn items) is shown as pink, the nebula hue nearest to gold (about 74° away on the hue wheel, against magenta's 109° and teal's 125°), before the no-equal-neighbours pass, so the stored colour is never rewritten. The shared `PATH_COLOURS` table is unchanged, so gold stays for Wealth and Earn elsewhere.
- **J5 — Why the celebration did not always fire, and the rule that replaces it.** It played only when the day went from incomplete to complete while the Checklist was open and its first summary of the day had been incomplete. So it missed a day completed elsewhere (Today's card, a habit's page, ZIGi with the Checklist closed), a first summary that already read complete (a reload right after the last tick, a store loading late), and any tab where `sessionStorage` threw (the guard then read "already celebrated"). The new rule is driven by state, not by a transition: once the Checklist, its stores and the UI preferences have loaded, today is complete and `checklist.celebratedOn` is not today, it plays and stores today's date (once per day per device). The tab-only `zigoals-checklist-celebrated` key is no longer read or written.
