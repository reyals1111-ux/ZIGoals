# ADR-038 — Session AM: "NOVA, ALWAYS ON"

- **Status:** in progress (branch `feature/session-am-nova`, [PR #101](https://github.com/reyals1111-ux/ZIGoals/pull/101); not merged, not deployed)
- **Base:** `main` `c65882f3` (Merge #100, Session AL), live as Alpha #54
- **Scope sources:** the owner's brief for Session AM (11 Nov 2026), `review/session-am-refs:REFERENCES.md` (43 owner mockups and four described screenshots; read-only, never merged), ADR-036 (N1–N12, M2, M4), ADR-037 (O5, Q11, Q14, Q16), `docs/research/session-ak/NOVA.md`, `docs/review/session-ak/NOVA_DESIGN_REVIEW.md`. Art direction: [`docs/design/NOVA_AM_ART_DIRECTION.md`](../design/NOVA_AM_ART_DIRECTION.md).

## Why this session exists
NOVA today is a holographic HUD: eighteen widgets that are always on the stage around a small SVG orb. The owner wants the opposite resting state and a far higher ceiling: a calm, nearly empty stage with one living star that is the agent, and widgets that are *made on request* for the answer the person asked for, in the look of the owner's mockups, good enough that a person keeps NOVA open all day. One task; everything else in the app stays as it is.

## Owner decisions (11 Nov 2026)
- **O1 Outside data.** Only free, keyless services; OFF until the person turns them on; each named in the plan for approval; CSP/egress changes are TIER 3 commits. Without them, the widget explains how to turn it on (never fake data).
- **O2 Sync.** NOVA's history, pinned widgets and layout join the existing end-to-end encrypted sync as an additive domain (TIER 3 sync), behaviour-preserving for existing data. On the public Alpha (no accounts) they stay on the device, and NOVA says so.
- **O3 Dependencies and budgets.** A new dependency or a route-weight budget change only if proposed in the plan with its size and reason; otherwise hand-written WebGL/Canvas/CSS, with heavy visuals loaded after first paint.
- **O4 Rename.** Renaming NOVA is a Premium feature, built now, available to everyone, labelled "Premium · free during the Alpha". Every other feature is for everyone.
- **O5 One plan, one approval.** After it: no questions; decisions are made by the session and recorded here.
- **Standing rules that still hold:** consumer first, chain invisible; private by design (no analytics); non-custodial (no AI signs, holds keys or moves funds); no fake integrations or simulated data shown as real (the Showcase is labelled); unknown is not zero; every figure names its source and as-of; no projection as a guarantee; never a film or comic reference in copy, commits or docs ("personal AI command centre"); the deterministic safety guard, the privacy modes, the page-data switches (ADR-037 Q14) and the Health rule (Q16) hold on every new path; the NOVA motion exception (ADR-037 O5) and no pointer tracking; the readability minimums; the protected list (Checklist orbit path, footer finale, Today hero and slogan, swan intro, Light's dark islands, shell navigation except NOVA's own side bars, the locked ZIGi look); no wallet, contract-signing or key changes.

## Owner approvals and amendments to the plan (11 Nov 2026, verbatim)
**Approved:** 1) MET Norway (api.met.no only) with the conditions in A2; 2) no new dependency, no budget change; 3) the additive `nova` sync section with A6; 4) D2 empty default.

- **A1** Existing NOVA stays reachable: quick actions with Undo, presets as one-tap "arrangements" (no longer the default), Holo/Classic, the Hermes page, ⌘K in NOVA, briefs. Nothing that works today disappears.
- **A2** MET Norway conditions (their terms: browsers should not call the API directly, except low-volume sites identified by Origin/Referer; they log the IP and coordinates). For the Alpha the direct call is accepted with these rules:
  - Make sure requests carry an Origin/Referer that names the app's domain (check our Referrer-Policy); coordinates ≤ 4 decimals (we use ~1 km); cache every answer until `Expires` and revalidate with If-Modified-Since; never fetch while NOVA is hidden; at most one refresh per 10 minutes per place; no HEAD-then-GET.
  - Credit on the widget: "Data from MET Norway" + a CC BY 4.0 licence link; never use the name or logo "Yr".
  - Honest privacy copy at the switch: MET Norway sees your network address and the rounded place. Update docs/PRIVACY.md, the in-app privacy / Your data list of outside services, and landing/privacy.html (the owner redeploys the landing after merge; list it in the report).
  - Record in ADR-038 as carried: a caching proxy Worker (hides the person's IP, required before a wider launch).
- **A3** Standalone app: NOVA opens as its own full-screen app (as Checklist does: no app chrome around it, a clear way back, a keyboard shortcut). If a "keep NOVA open in its own window" path fits the existing PWA without risk, add it (TIER 3 PWA if the manifest or service worker changes); otherwise record why not.
- **A4** Agent: give the model an explicit tool to show/pin/close widgets (not only widgets inferred from lookups). The model can never change permissions, outside-data switches, privacy mode or page-data switches; only the person can. Spoken replies use the existing voice path and its privacy labels, and drive the star's speaking state. Without a model the same requests make the same widgets from on-device answers (EN + NL phrasing families).
- **A5** Proactive (opt-in, off by default): while NOVA is open, gentle cards from the existing briefs (today's plan, due today, a streak at risk, a bill due) may appear on the stage; Calm makes them rarer; never a notification without the person's own switch. Add a setting "keep made widgets until I close them" next to the 12 h expiry.
- **A6** Sync safety: prove with tests that the app with the new `nova` section against the CURRENT private-sync Worker (not redeployed) keeps checklist and all other sync working and simply keeps NOVA local. Keep backup format 4 if the new records fit additively; if a format change is needed, ADR + TIER 3 + the rollback effect #55 → #54. Record the rollback notes in ADR-038.
- **A7** Robustness: WebGL context loss and no-WebGL / low-power / save-data → the SVG orb, still beautiful; the star is lighter on phones; frame governor proven at CPU 4×; nothing animates while hidden.
- **A8** Preview checkpoint: after the widget kit, the centre node and the live stage (parts 2–4) are pushed, add a "Preview now" section to docs/handoff/AM_RESUME.md (what to open, three requests to try) and continue straight on; no stop.

## Decisions (made by the session; D-numbers are referenced from code comments)

### The stage
- **D1 One stage, one list.** The stage is AK's Orbit layout (the star in the centre, two columns, a band below). Widgets that are made on request and widgets the person arranged are the same object in the same grid, drawn by the same frame. Bento, Focus and Minimal stay as they are.
- **D2 Calm by default.** A NOVA with no arrangement of its own shows no widgets: the star, the greeting, the day line, the one-tap requests and the command bar. AL's rule that a brand-new NOVA starts on the Command preset, and AK's eight default widgets for a record without a widget list, both end (owner brief: "With real data, the stage starts calm and nearly empty"). A person who arranged widgets keeps exactly those, as pinned widgets. The Showcase seeds a fully populated stage and says it is the Showcase's. Specs that relied on the defaults arrange the Command preset first; their assertions stay as strict (each change carries `Session AM (D2, deliberate)`).
- **D3 Made, pinned, hidden, closed.** A widget made by a request is *on the stage* until the person closes it, and leaves by itself after 12 hours unless pinned. *Pin* keeps it (it joins the arranged list). *Hide* folds it into the dock as a chip; a tap brings it back. *Close* removes it, with Undo. *Open* goes to its full page with the morph; Back returns to the same stage. One widget per kind: asking again about the same thing re-targets the widget (a new range, another goal) and it is made again in place.
- **D4 The catalogue is closed.** NOVA and a connected model choose *which* widget from a fixed catalogue and with which small, validated parameters (a range, a record's id). The figures always come from the person's own records on the device, through the pages' own stores. A model can never put words, numbers or markup into a widget.
- **D5 Gates on a widget.** A widget made by a request reads under the same switches as NOVA's on-device answers (the page-data switches, Q14; NOVA → Permissions; Health only with NOVA's Health permission and the device's Health gate, Q16). Closed means the *locked* state: which switch, and nothing else. A message the safety guard answers makes no widget.

### The agent
- **D6 One engine, still.** Requests go through ZIGi's engine on NOVA's surface (ADR-036 N1): the safety guard first, then on-device answers, then the person's model. The session adds a deterministic **summon** step beside it: the question (English or Dutch, typed or spoken) and the lookups the answer used are mapped to widgets. With no model the mapping is the whole story; with a model the lookups it ran are mapped the same way.
- **D7 Answer in place.** The command bar answers on the stage: NOVA's words under the star, the widgets beside it. The conversation stays mounted and opens as a panel on request, and by itself when an answer needs a decision (a confirm card, a choice, a failure). Nothing is sent before Enter.
- **D8 Writes.** Unchanged: every change is a confirm card with Undo; money is only a pre-filled card the person signs themselves; nothing is signed, sent or moved by NOVA.
- **D9 Voice.** Speech input and read-aloud keep their rules (off until turned on; the disclosure before first use; Strict only on the device). The star shows listening, thinking and speaking; every state is also text.

### The centre node
- **D10 A hand-written WebGL star system**, one fragment shader on one canvas, no library (art direction §5). The SVG orb stays as the fallback (no WebGL, a lost context, low-end, battery saver, Save-Data, phones) and for the mini window, the chat header and the guides.
- **D11 Rest.** The star's loop has three rates: active (30 fps), resting (≤ 6 fps at half resolution, after 60 s without a key, tap or request, or when the window is not focused) and stopped (hidden, frozen, Motion Off, reduced motion: one still frame per state). Activity is never read from pointer movement. A governor steps quality down when frames are slow.

### Outside data (O1)
- **D12 One service, one host: MET Norway (`https://api.met.no`).** Locationforecast 2.0 and Sunrise 3.0. Free, keyless, global, CC BY 4.0 / NLOD ("Data from MET Norway"), and its terms allow any purpose, paid apps included; a browser identifies itself by its Origin, as the terms ask. Open-Meteo was the better technical fit but its free tier is for non-commercial use only and names apps with subscriptions as commercial, and ZIGoals shows placeholder plans; it is not used. No air-quality widget: no keyless service with terms that fit was found.
- **D13 Off until turned on, and what leaves.** NOVA → Permissions gets "Weather (outside data)". Off: the weather widget explains the switch and what would be sent. On: the browser asks api.met.no for a place rounded to two decimals (about 1 km); the service sees that place and the device's network address, nothing else. No account, no key, no cookie.
- **D14 The place without a geocoder.** The person picks a city from a list that ships with the app (loaded on use), or types coordinates. The browser's location permission stays denied by the Permissions-Policy (unchanged). No geocoding service is called.
- **D15 Cache and manners (A2).** One reading per place is kept on the device until the service's `Expires` time and then revalidated with `If-Modified-Since`; at most one refresh per 10 minutes per place; never fetched while NOVA is hidden, never without a weather widget on the stage or a weather question; a plain GET only (no HEAD first). The widget credits "Data from MET Norway" with a link to the CC BY 4.0 licence and names the reading's time; the name and logo "Yr" are never used.

### Stored formats and sync (O2)
- **D16 One new record, `zigoals:nova-stage:v1`** (additive; loose object, version 1, every field optional, every list bounded): the widgets made on request (`made`), the small parameters of widgets (`params`), the dock (`hidden`), and the history of requests (`asks`: when, the words, the widgets it made, phone or computer, on-device or model; at most 200). A chat that is not kept writes no request. In Export everything, restore, the encrypted backup, erase and the catalog.
- **D17 One new record, `zigoals:nova-world:v1`** (additive, device-only): outside data on or off, the place, the last reading with its own expiry. Exported, restored and erased; never synced.
- **D18 Sync: an additive extras section `nova`,** after the pattern of AK's `checklist` section. It carries `zigoals:nova-stage:v1` and, from `zigoals:nova:v1`, the layout and the arranged (pinned) widgets; every other field of `zigoals:nova:v1` (the side bars' state, the persona, the names, the instructions, the wake phrase, the start page, the opening) stays on each device (`deviceFields`). Consent: the account's (nothing in it is Health data: a request's words are the person's own, as a note's are; widgets carry kinds and ids, never figures). Effective once the owner redeploys `private-sync`; until then, and on the public Alpha, nothing travels and NOVA says "On this device only".
- **D19 A phone's requests on the computer.** A request asked on a phone is kept in `asks` with its widgets; with sync, the computer shows it under Requests ("from your phone") and one tap makes its widgets there. Nothing is projected by itself.

### Shape of the code
- **D20 No new dependency, no budget change.** WebGL, Canvas, SVG and CSS by hand. The star, the widget kit, the summon step, the weather module and the city list are chunks loaded after first paint or on use; `/app/nova` stays inside 458 kB.
- **D21 Rename (O4).** "Make NOVA yours" keeps the name field; it is labelled "Premium · free during the Alpha" and linked to Plans. No gate.
- **D22 Protected surfaces.** Nothing outside NOVA's own page, its side bars, its guides and its entries is redesigned. A "Back to NOVA" return on a page opened from a widget is the browser's Back (the stage is restored as it was).

### Decided while building (11 Nov, after the approval; no questions asked)
- **D23 What was asked for stands in front.** Made widgets are listed before the arranged (pinned) ones, newest first, so a request's widget is in view the moment it is made (a person with a full arrangement would otherwise have to scroll to see it). While the stage is being arranged the pinned widgets lead in their own order, so a place on the orbits means the same before and after a move.
- **D24 The Showcase keeps its Command arrangement.** It opens fully populated on the same arrangement as before (existing HUD specs and the owner's earlier checks depend on it) and its stage record seeds a history of four example requests, one of them from a phone; the new widgets are one tap away (one-tap requests, "Show again"). The Showcase keeps Health off for NOVA, as a real profile does: its weight request makes a *locked* widget that names the switch. The weather widget in the Showcase shows example values labelled "Showcase example · not a real reading"; the Showcase asks no service.
- **D25 A made widget whose switch is off is made locked (D5 in practice).** It appears, says which switch is off and links to Permissions. A widget the person pinned themselves keeps the earlier behaviour of the HUD's panels (Health on screen with "On your screen only · not in NOVA's chat").
- **D26 Weather revalidation (A2 as it can be built in a browser).** MET Norway answers no CORS preflight, so a page cannot add `If-Modified-Since` itself (any header set by the page makes the request non-simple and it then fails). The request is a plain GET with `cache: 'no-cache'`: the browser's own HTTP cache sends the conditional request and reuses its copy on a 304. Everything else in A2 is as written: two decimals, the reading kept until `Expires`, never while hidden, one ask per ten minutes per place (also after an error), no HEAD. The app's own Referrer-Policy is `no-referrer`; the Origin header is sent on every cross-origin request, and each weather request sets `strict-origin-when-cross-origin`, so MET Norway also receives an origin-only Referer. Side effect, said in the privacy text: the last answer also sits in the browser's HTTP cache until it expires.
- **D27 The place list ships with the app.** 315 cities with positions at two decimals (cross-checked against GeoNames), loaded when the place field is first used; typed coordinates are accepted too. Temperatures are Celsius (the app has no temperature preference); days and sun times use the device's time zone.
- **D28 The model's stage tool (A4).** `stage_widget` (show, pin, hide, close; one widget of the catalogue per call; an optional range) and `weather` (the reading the device holds; never a fetch, never a place) exist only where the environment carries NOVA's stage: NOVA's own messages on a computer. They are read-only tools: the stage applies a `stage_widget` call after the reply arrives, at most three shows and three commands per reply. There is no tool, action kind or action write for permissions, outside data, the privacy mode or page-data switches (`lib/ai/tools/nova-stage.test.ts`).
- **D29 A phone has no stage.** On a phone a request is answered in words by the engine (with its inline chart); the weather and the time are answered in words by the device; a stage command says the stage is on a computer. Each request is kept with the widgets it would make, and the computer shows it under Requests with "Show again" (D19).
- **D30 Gentle cards (A5).** Off by default; one card at a time; each at most once a day, three a day, one a day under Calm; never one whose page-data switch is off; never while the tab is hidden, a conversation is open, the person is typing or arranging; a line under the star, never a notification or a sound. What was shown on which local day is an optional field of the stage record.
- **D31 NOVA in its own window (A3).** A top-bar button opens `/app/nova` in a separate window (`window.open` with the name `zigoals-nova`), which the person can keep beside their work; in that window Exit closes it. The installed app already has a NOVA shortcut and "Open ZIGoals on NOVA". **The manifest and the service worker are unchanged**, so there is no TIER 3 (PWA) commit: a `display_override` or a second manifest would change how every installed copy opens, for no gain over a plain window.
- **D32 Readability.** The HUD's labels and notes moved from 13 px to 14 px (the brief's minimum), on old and new widgets alike.

### Sync as built, and its rollback notes (A6)
- **D33 The `nova` section's mechanism.** The extras' existing `deviceFields` is a deny-list (a field a later build adds would start travelling by accident), so the section uses an allow-list beside it: of `zigoals:nova:v1` only `layout` and `widgets` are collected, merged and applied; the eleven other fields are listed as device fields and a test keeps the schema equal to version + sync + device. Records travel as stored (a later build's value is not replaced by this build's default), and two devices' joined lists are cut to the record's bounds (200 requests, 24 widgets) so a merge is never refused for size. On the wire the app asks `with=checklist&with=nova`; today's Worker reads the first `with` only, so everything it knows keeps working.
- **D34 Consent.** The section travels under the account's consent (not the Health consent): it holds kinds, ids, times and the person's own request words, never a figure or a Health record. *Revised by D35 (AM-S5): a request about Health is kept by its widgets' names, not the person's words.*
- **Proof (A6), `scripts/run11/extras-nova-a6.test.mjs`,** against today's Worker frozen from `c65882f3` with its file hashes: (1) the frozen bytes predate NOVA; (2) this app against the deployed Worker, two devices: the five sections' outcomes, device bytes, heads and request sequence equal Session AK's app, NOVA is skipped with a note, every answer is 200 and nothing of NOVA is stored; (3) six syncs with NOVA edits: one heads request each, the Checklist still syncs, "Remove from the cloud" works; (4) the deployed Worker refuses the comma form, `with=nova` alone and `section=nova`; (5) with the new Worker a phone's request and the pinned widgets reach the computer while its side bars and persona stay its own, and a later build's record and field survive; (6) the old app against the new Worker is answered byte for byte as before; (7) the Worker rolled back and forward on the same storage.
- **Rollback, app #55 → #54 after NOVA has synced.** #54 never asks for the `nova` section; its requests are answered byte for byte as before and the sealed NOVA copy stays in the cloud, unread. On the device `zigoals:nova-stage:v1` and `zigoals:nova-world:v1` stay in storage and are ignored; `zigoals:nova:v1` is read by #54's own reader (frozen readers pass; its schema did not change). Widgets pinned on another device show in #54's NOVA; kinds #54 does not know are kept and not shown. Requests asked while on #54 are not recorded. Rolling forward resumes with a normal three-way merge. One caveat: "Remove the extras from the cloud" pressed on #54 deletes five sections and leaves NOVA's sealed copy until #55 returns or the cloud data is deleted. **The backup format is 4 on both sides.**
- **Rollback, the Worker back to `c65882f3` after NOVA has synced.** The five sections keep syncing; NOVA stays on each device with a note, not an error; one heads request per sync, no retries. The sealed NOVA copy stays stored untouched; the account erase still removes it. Rolling forward resumes NOVA, including requests made meanwhile.
- **What the owner redeploys, and in which order.** Only `private-sync` (one file changed, `extras.mjs`) and the app. Either order is safe; recommended: the Worker first, then Alpha #55, so the first sync carries NOVA and the "sync service is not updated yet" note never shows. No migration, no data step; the lifecycle Worker is unchanged. On the public Alpha (no accounts) nothing travels: NOVA says "Your requests, pinned widgets and layout stay on this device only."

### After the two reviews (11 Nov; decided without questions)
- **D35 The security review's findings and what was done** (`docs/review/session-am/AM_SECURITY_REVIEW.md`; 5 Medium, 6 Low, 2 Info, no Critical or High). All thirteen are closed in code and pinned by tests (`lib/nova/stage/am-security.test.ts` and the tests each finding names):
  - AM-S1 A pin never opens a gate: a widget of this session's catalogue reads under its switch whether made or pinned; a model's `pin` on a widget whose switch is closed is dropped. AM-S7 A model's reply may carry one stage command.
  - AM-S2 Notes and Journal widgets read under NOVA's Notes and Journal permissions (two new gates); Today under Today.
  - AM-S3, AM-S10 The weather client: a finished request writes only the reading and only into the record as it is then; 15 s limit; `redirect: 'error'`; a size cap; rounding wherever a place is read; the ten-minute mark is written before the request.
  - AM-S4 The Showcase asks no weather service on a phone either. AM-S6 Only lookups that ran and succeeded become widgets; a phone has no stage commands.
  - AM-S5 **D34 is revised:** a request about Health (its words, or a widget under the Health gate) is kept as the widgets' names ("Health: Weight"), never the person's words; a stage command is kept in the stage's own words ("Close: Weight").
  - AM-S8 Requests carry the conversation they were asked in and leave with it ("Don't keep this chat", a deleted conversation).
  - AM-S9 A restore brings the weather place, never the switch; a Merge keeps this device's own switch only when the place stays the same.
  - AM-S11 "Keep made widgets", the gentle-cards switch and today's shown cards are per device: never collected, never applied from another device's copy. A made widget stamped more than a day ahead is dropped.
  - AM-S12 The made widgets' gates are also read while the conversation is not mounted (a phone). AM-S13 The privacy text names the phone's weather question, what the weather lookup hands a model and that a model can ask for the widget.
- **D36 A request with a widget is answered on the stage; everything else is a conversation.** Words the stage has no widget for, and every message the guard answers, open the conversation at once as before (A1), so a long or a careful answer is read whole. The on-device engine comes first: the stage answers by itself only for stage commands and for widget requests the engine has no answer of its own for; a write, a navigation, a refusal or a "needs your own AI" note never gets a widget.
- **D37 The governor reacts within about a second and a half.** A frame more than four slots late counts as four, so a device that cannot keep up (software rendering, a throttled CPU) reaches a tier it can afford in nine frames, not thirty-six; the first two seconds after the star starts or wakes are not judged; the active pace skips the display's extra frames (30 fps on a 60 or 120 Hz screen). Tiers step the noise detail before the resolution, rest slows the frames and never the sharpness, and the still frame is drawn at full quality.
- **D38 A widget keeps its side of the star** while one or a few widgets are made, hidden, closed or pinned around it (remembered for the visit, not stored); an arrangement or a preset is placed afresh.

- **D39 Software rendering gets the still star (A7).** Where the browser draws WebGL without graphics hardware (it reports a major performance caveat, or the renderer's own name says so: SwiftShader, llvmpipe, softpipe, a basic render driver), the star is one composed frame per state, as with Motion Off: measured in this session's sandbox (no GPU), a running star held the page to 67 ms a frame at CPU 4×. The canvas says so (`data-star-software`). With graphics hardware nothing changes.
- **D40 The design review's findings and what was done** (`docs/review/session-am/NOVA_AM_DESIGN_REVIEW.md`, 40 findings). Blockers: AM-D1 fixed (the phone's command bar fits; Send at x 321 of 390, was 822); AM-D2 and AM-D3 fixed (every made widget's answer is one sentence with its figure from the widget's own selector; the stage never shows the engine's "can't answer" beside a widget). Majors fixed: D5, D6, D7, D8, D9, D10, D11, D12, D13 (the wealth widget is "Tracked wealth": it never called holdings a net worth again), D14, D15, D16, D18, D19, D20, D21, D22, D23, D24, D25. Partly: D4 (the calm stage lost its read-outs, key cap and second row; the top bar keeps its tiles on one row, because every tile must stay reachable by its name, A1), D17 (the conversation is a docked column from 1100 px; the phone's head is one row plus a slim tools row), D26 (the phone is the star, the answer, the chips and the field; pinned widgets sit below, not in a sheet). Minor and polish: D29, D30, D31 (words; the L chart partly), D32, D33, D35, D37, D38 (partly), D39 fixed; D27, D28, D34, D36, D40 partly or left (recorded in the review file's own words). Not judged on real graphics hardware: the star's frame rate and the "made" moment as a recording (owner check).

### Proofs on the production build (11 Nov, this session's sandbox: Chromium without a GPU, so WebGL is software-rendered)
- **Weights (gzip, budgets not raised):** `/app/nova` 434.3 kB of 458; `/app/nova/setup` 445.8 of 458; the shell 433.4 of 447; every route "ok" (`node scripts/check-weight-budget.mjs`).
- **Idle and rest** (empty profile, 1440×900, Full motion, main-thread share over 5–10 s, software GL): active 4.8 %, the window not focused (resting, 6 frames a second) 2.3 %.
- **Hidden** (`tests/grok53/hud/hud-perf.spec.ts`, visibility set hidden): 0 sky frames, 0 running HUD animations, 0.047 s of main-thread work in 3 s (1.6 %).
- **CPU 4×** (Showcase, Command arrangement): with the governor at its still frame the page's own frames are 16.7 ms median, 16.8 ms p95.
- **Motion Off and reduced motion:** one still frame per state, no beam, no count-up, widgets simply there (unit rules in `lib/nova/core/governor.test.ts`, `lib/nova/holo/am-count.test.ts`; browser spec `tests/nova-stage-am.spec.ts`).
