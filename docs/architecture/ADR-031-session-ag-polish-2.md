## Decisions taken without asking
- **K1 — The branch.** The owner named `feature/session-ag-polish-2`; the session's harness branch (`claude/ag-polish-2-checklist-dxe66n`) is left untouched, as the brief says.
- **K2 — The `frontend-design` skill is not available in this sandbox.** Its rules (a committed aesthetic direction, typographic hierarchy, purposeful motion, no generic defaults) are applied by hand, as Sessions AF and AG Polish did (ADR-028 H3, ADR-030 J2).
- **K3 — Why the phone's line was straight, and why names ended in "…".** (1) `lib/path/phone-lanes.ts` kept every node between 34 % and 66 % of the stage's width (`LANES = [.5, .66, .5, .34]`), and `path.tsx` capped the phone line's swing at `min(60 px, 16.5 % of the width)`: neighbours travelled about 60 px apart and every bend was a small one. (2) A phone tab's `max-width` was its canvas-measured width (the body's font at 14 px) plus exactly the tab's chrome (40 px, no slack), measured once before the web font settled and never again (the lanes depended on the width and the names only), with `text-overflow: ellipsis` — so on an iPhone a short name such as "Water" was cut to "Wa…".
- **K4 — The phone's lanes, rebuilt (owner Part 1a, 1d, 1e).** `lib/path/phone-lanes.ts` now spreads the nodes across the whole width: each node travels from the one before it — or from its orbit's planet, for an orbit's first node — by a seeded rhythm (the path's id) of wide sweeps (about half the width) and short ones (about a third), toward the side with more room and now and then the other way, so neighbours never line up. Each tab hangs toward the free side and always shows the whole name on one line; a node moves along until it does; only a name too long even for the leftmost lane wraps (320 px), every line shown. ZIGi keeps its room beside a node where that costs the sweep little (within 7 % of the width); otherwise it takes its slot above the tab (AG Polish's `above`), so the path's width comes first. The phone's pitch grows from about 166 to about 178 px (within the owner's 150–180), which gives ZIGi's cheer room above an edge node's tab. Tablets and computers keep AG's wave and sides exactly.
- **K5 — Tab widths as drawn, and never an ellipsis.** A hidden probe in the stage, with the tab's own font, measures each name on mount, once the web fonts are ready, on each font load and on a resize; the lane width is that plus the tab's padding, border, the check's room (always counted, so a tick moves nothing) and 6 px to spare. On a phone a one-line tab has no width cap and no `text-overflow`; a wrapping tab keeps to its room and shows every line. ZIGi's placer counts an undone tab's check room (22 px) too, so the tick that adds the check never brings the tab onto ZIGi standing past its end.
- **K6 — The phone's flowing line.** The phone's swing is `min(150 px, 40 % of the stage)` (AG Polish: `min(60 px, 16.5 %)`); `flowSegments` takes two phone-only options: `minBow` (18 % of the stage: a stretch whose ends nearly line up still bows out to the side with room) and `obstacles` (each tab at its reserved one-line width, and each banner's words), with a `clearance` of 6 px. Owner amendment 1: at most `MAX_TRIES` (12) seeded shapes per stretch; if none clears, the one with the most room is drawn and the obstacles it still meets are reported; the path then moves such a tab to its node's other side, once per layout and only where the whole name fits there (never a loop, never a blocked render). A computer passes neither option, so its curves are byte-for-byte AG's (unit-tested). Tests: the phone spec samples every pixel of every stretch (no near-vertical run over 40 % of a stretch, inside the stage, under no tab) and the mean sideways travel between neighbours (≥ 25 % of the stage).
- **K7 — The compact banner and the planet beside it (owner Parts 1b, 1c).** Halving a one-row banner (symbol, "Afternoon Orbit", "Complete ✓") is impossible without text under 14 px, so on a phone the Habits banner takes its compact form, stacked: the symbol beside the title (17 px; 16 px under 360 px), the count chip right under the title (14 px) — 157–185 px wide against AG Polish's ~340 px band — and its tint ends 44 px after the words. The planet stands at the banner's right, its sphere centred on the banner's height, 88–100 px (`clamp(88px, 26cqi, 100px)`: at least 1.4 × the 62 px node, about 1.6 × AG Polish's 60 px), 12 px from the stage's edge. A design's wide rings may reach past the edge or toward the banner: past the edge they fade out in the banner's last 16 px (it clips sideways, so nothing scrolls); toward the banner they pass behind its words, which lie above the planet's ring layer. The phone's planet image asks for the 256 px file on a dense screen (`sizes` 100 px). The test "the count chip right after the title" reads, on a phone, "right under the title, aligned with it".
- **K8 — Planets light when they are reached (owner Part 2b), and the banner's short fade (Part 2a).** The day's first planet (the first orbit with nodes) lights as soon as any node of its own orbit is done; every later planet once every node before it is done — read, as the owner's parenthesis says, as every counted node of the orbit before it (all of its nodes where it counts none; an empty orbit has no planet and is passed over). `planetsReached` (components/path/path.tsx) is pure; the path's existing one-time marks (`useJust`, `p:<orbit>`) play the planet's completion at the moment it is reached, Undo that un-reaches it returns it to matte, and a day opened with it reached shows it lit and still. The line counts a reached planet as lit. "Complete ✓", the camera's glide to the next orbit and ZIGi's greeting stay on the orbit's own completion; the finale is unchanged. Moved at equal strength: `checklist-ag-polish` test 4 (lit when reached: the morning's with its first tick, the afternoon's once the morning is done; still on reload) and `path-engine` (three lit stretches after the morning, the third into the afternoon's planet). The banner's tint now ends `--banner-fade` after the words (96 px on a computer, 44 px on a phone) instead of reaching 70 % past them.
- **K9 — The progress bar in segments (owner Part 3).** `components/checklist/progress-segments.tsx` replaces the shared `GlassBar` here only (the shared bar is unchanged): the same `.checklist-progress-bar` element, role, label, values and words. The segments are today's counted items in path order with the colours the path gives them (the same `checklistPath` and `checklistDisplayColours`); each segment's ends are the half-way mixes with its neighbours' colours, so lit neighbours flow into each other like the line. Matte is the unreached node's formula (`oklch(l × .78, c × .28)`) at 40 % over the glass, so an unlit bar never reads as filled. A segment lights in the same render as its node (a 320 ms fade; none under reduced motion and Motion Off) and dims on Undo; the sparkle (16 px, a softer glow) rests at the end of the last lit segment; the shine sweeps the segment just lit (`.checklist-progress-shine`, which `checklist-overlay-af` reads). The track is 10 px (8 px on a phone; AG: 18 and 14). `.checklist-progress-seg` and `.checklist-progress-spark` join the theme islands (the sheets regenerated; `--check` passes).
