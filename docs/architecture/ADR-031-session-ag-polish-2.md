- **Amendments (2026-10-22, after approval):**
  1. Line obstacles: the seeded retry in `flowSegments` is bounded (at most 12 tries per stretch); if none clears, the best try (largest clearance) is taken and the tab moves to the other side or the next lane instead — never a loop, never a blocked render. ## Decisions taken without asking
- **K1 — The branch.** The owner named `feature/session-ag-polish-2`; the session's harness branch (`claude/ag-polish-2-checklist-dxe66n`) is left untouched, as the brief says.
- **K2 — The `frontend-design` skill is not available in this sandbox.** Its rules (a committed aesthetic direction, typographic hierarchy, purposeful motion, no generic defaults) are applied by hand, as Sessions AF and AG Polish did (ADR-028 H3, ADR-030 J2).
- **K3 — Why the phone's line was straight, and why names ended in "…".** (1) `lib/path/phone-lanes.ts` kept every node between 34 % and 66 % of the stage's width (`LANES = [.5, .66, .5, .34]`), and `path.tsx` capped the phone line's swing at `min(60 px, 16.5 % of the width)`: neighbours travelled about 60 px apart and every bend was a small one. (2) A phone tab's `max-width` was its canvas-measured width (the body's font at 14 px) plus exactly the tab's chrome (40 px, no slack), measured once before the web font settled and never again (the lanes depended on the width and the names only), with `text-overflow: ellipsis` — so on an iPhone a short name such as "Water" was cut to "Wa…".
- **K4 — The phone's lanes, rebuilt (owner Part 1a, 1d, 1e).** `lib/path/phone-lanes.ts` now spreads the nodes across the whole width: each node travels from the one before it — or from its orbit's planet, for an orbit's first node — by a seeded rhythm (the path's id) of wide sweeps (about half the width) and short ones (about a third), toward the side with more room and now and then the other way, so neighbours never line up. Each tab hangs toward the free side and always shows the whole name on one line; a node moves along until it does; only a name too long even for the leftmost lane wraps (320 px), every line shown. ZIGi keeps its room beside a node where that costs the sweep little (within 7 % of the width); otherwise it takes its slot above the tab (AG Polish's `above`), so the path's width comes first. The phone's pitch grows from about 166 to about 178 px (within the owner's 150–180), which gives ZIGi's cheer room above an edge node's tab. Tablets and computers keep AG's wave and sides exactly.
- **K5 — Tab widths as drawn, and never an ellipsis.** A hidden probe in the stage, with the tab's own font, measures each name on mount, once the web fonts are ready, on each font load and on a resize; the lane width is that plus the tab's padding, border, the check's room (always counted, so a tick moves nothing) and 6 px to spare. On a phone a one-line tab has no width cap and no `text-overflow`; a wrapping tab keeps to its room and shows every line. ZIGi's placer counts an undone tab's check room (22 px) too, so the tick that adds the check never brings the tab onto ZIGi standing past its end.
- **K6 — The phone's flowing line.** The phone's swing is `min(150 px, 40 % of the stage)` (AG Polish: `min(60 px, 16.5 %)`); `flowSegments` takes two phone-only options: `minBow` (18 % of the stage: a stretch whose ends nearly line up still bows out to the side with room) and `obstacles` (each tab at its reserved one-line width, and each banner's words), with a `clearance` of 6 px. Owner amendment 1: at most `MAX_TRIES` (12) seeded shapes per stretch; if none clears, the one with the most room is drawn and the obstacles it still meets are reported; the path then moves such a tab to its node's other side, once per layout and only where the whole name fits there (never a loop, never a blocked render). A computer passes neither option, so its curves are byte-for-byte AG's (unit-tested). Tests: the phone spec samples every pixel of every stretch (no near-vertical run over 40 % of a stretch, inside the stage, under no tab) and the mean sideways travel between neighbours (≥ 25 % of the stage).
- **K7 — The compact banner and the planet beside it (owner Parts 1b, 1c).** Halving a one-row banner (symbol, "Afternoon Orbit", "Complete ✓") is impossible without text under 14 px, so on a phone the Habits banner takes its compact form, stacked: the symbol beside the title (17 px; 16 px under 360 px), the count chip right under the title (14 px) — 157–185 px wide against AG Polish's ~340 px band — and its tint ends 44 px after the words. The planet stands at the banner's right, its sphere centred on the banner's height, 88–100 px (`clamp(88px, 26cqi, 100px)`: at least 1.4 × the 62 px node, about 1.6 × AG Polish's 60 px), 12 px from the stage's edge. A design's wide rings may reach past the edge or toward the banner: past the edge they fade out in the banner's last 16 px (it clips sideways, so nothing scrolls); toward the banner they pass behind its words, which lie above the planet's ring layer. The phone's planet image asks for the 256 px file on a dense screen (`sizes` 100 px). The test "the count chip right after the title" reads, on a phone, "right under the title, aligned with it".
- **K8 — Planets light when they are reached (owner Part 2b), and the banner's short fade (Part 2a).** The day's first planet (the first orbit with nodes) lights as soon as any node of its own orbit is done; every later planet once every node before it is done — read, as the owner's parenthesis says, as every counted node of the orbit before it (all of its nodes where it counts none; an empty orbit has no planet and is passed over). `planetsReached` (components/path/path.tsx) is pure; the path's existing one-time marks (`useJust`, `p:<orbit>`) play the planet's completion at the moment it is reached, Undo that un-reaches it returns it to matte, and a day opened with it reached shows it lit and still. The line counts a reached planet as lit. "Complete ✓", the camera's glide to the next orbit and ZIGi's greeting stay on the orbit's own completion; the finale is unchanged. Moved at equal strength: `checklist-ag-polish` test 4 (lit when reached: the morning's with its first tick, the afternoon's once the morning is done; still on reload) and `path-engine` (three lit stretches after the morning, the third into the afternoon's planet). The banner's tint now ends `--banner-fade` after the words (96 px on a computer, 44 px on a phone) instead of reaching 70 % past them.
- **K9 — The progress bar in segments (owner Part 3).** `components/checklist/progress-segments.tsx` replaces the shared `GlassBar` here only (the shared bar is unchanged): the same `.checklist-progress-bar` element, role, label, values and words. The segments are today's counted items in path order with the colours the path gives them (the same `checklistPath` and `checklistDisplayColours`); each segment's ends are the half-way mixes with its neighbours' colours, so lit neighbours flow into each other like the line. Matte is the unreached node's formula (`oklch(l × .78, c × .28)`) at 40 % over the glass, so an unlit bar never reads as filled. A segment lights in the same render as its node (a 320 ms fade; none under reduced motion and Motion Off) and dims on Undo; the sparkle (16 px, a softer glow) rests at the end of the last lit segment; the shine sweeps the segment just lit (`.checklist-progress-shine`, which `checklist-overlay-af` reads). The track is 10 px (8 px on a phone; AG: 18 and 14). `.checklist-progress-seg` and `.checklist-progress-spark` join the theme islands (the sheets regenerated; `--check` passes).
- **K10 — The ambient sky (owner Part 4, the one owner-approved loop).** `components/checklist/ambient-sky.tsx`, a fixed layer over the sky and under everything else (z-index 0, before the shell, no pointer events): three twinkle stars reused in turn (one every 2.6–7.2 s, 1.1–1.8 s each) and one shooting star (every 25–45 s, the first after 9–22 s; 14–38° down to the left or right, 28–50 % of the width, 0.9–1.4 s), in whites, ice cyans and violets — cyans and violets by day, where white would not show. Timers and Web Animations on transform and opacity only, no frame loop, the sky's size read once and on a resize. `data-ambient` reads `running`, `paused` (the tab hidden — the Page Visibility API — or the celebration, a sheet or the menu open: timers cleared, animations cancelled) or `off` (reduced motion or Motion Off, followed live; nothing drawn, and the layer hidden in CSS as well; forced colours hide it too). Random positions come from `Math.random`: decoration, never geometry. The theme-parity harness runs with Motion Off, so the sky never enters it.
- **K11 — Quick add (owner Part 5, amendment 3).** The top bar reads close · title · progress · view · flame · "+" · "⋯" (a phone: close · progress · flame · "+" · "⋯"); on a computer the bar spans the shell's own width (1352 px, 24 px sides), so "⋯" ends over the timers' right edge (pointer 11) and "+" stands where "⋯" was, the same 44 px glass button, "Quick add to your Checklist". The sheet (`components/checklist/orbit-quick-add.tsx`, its own lazy chunk) opens in the Checklist's existing sheet (a side panel on a computer, from the foot on a phone): the node it becomes, live (its glyph from the name through the existing map, `orbitGlyph`, as a habit's), Name (1–80 characters, at least one visible), Orbit (Morning, Afternoon, Evening, Anytime; the clock's own by default, the Habits page's `currentSection`), Kind (once a day = a tick; a number with its unit = a count, a whole number from 1; minutes = a duration in minutes, above 0 — the Habits editor's own `readFormNumber` limits, up to 1,000,000,000), Days (every day, or weekdays: at least one) and Colour. Habits have no colour field (their card colour is `visualTone(id)`), so a chosen colour is one of the five habit-card tones (cyan, violet, mint, blue, magenta) and the habit's UUID is drawn until its tone is that one (`lib/checklist/quick-add.ts`, bounded, unit-tested); Automatic draws the id when the sheet opens, so the preview shows the colour it will have (the path's no-equal-neighbours pass may still shift it). Pink is not offered: no habit can wear it without a new field. "Add to my orbit" calls the Habits page's own `create` (category "Checklist", description and notes empty, type build, per day, no end, the orbit as its time of day), stars it with the Checklist's own `addFavourite` in that orbit, closes the sheet (focus back on "+"), brings the new node into view with a one-time bloom (`data-arrive`; none under reduced motion and Motion Off), and shows "Added to Morning Orbit · Edit on Habits" with Undo; the link opens `/app/habits#habit-<id>`. Undo removes the star, then the habit only while it is exactly as created — no entries, `updatedAt` equal to `createdAt`, no rule revisions, timer receipts or Health link — read from the latest stored record inside the store's own update (owner amendment 3); otherwise it says "Removed from your orbit · the habit stays on Habits". (The Habits page's own delete cannot remove a habit created today: an archive takes effect tomorrow at the earliest; this follows ZIGi's create-habit Undo.) The shared toast gains two additive options for this: a link after the message, and an Undo that resolves to `{undone}` to say what it did.
- **K12 — The List view (owner Part 6).** Each section opens with the path's own Habits banner (`OrbitBanner` with an h3 title and no planet: the symbol, "Morning Orbit" in the nebula flow, "n of m" or "Complete ✓", the short fade) and its fold; the orbit sheet's banner rules now apply to the List as well (the phone's compact stacked form stays the path's, where the planet needs the room). Each row is the path's own node at the path's own size (80 px from 1180 px, 72 px from 641 px, 62 px on a phone; it is the 44 px+ button itself) and the path's tab running from the node's right-centre to the right like a step: dark night glass with white words and an edge in the node's colour; once done filled in the node's colour with dark words and a check; the second line (value, what it is, the time) inside it under the name. "+" and ⋯ stay on the right; from 641 px the "+" column is one width on every row (128 px) so the tabs end together. The tab is a theme island like the path's. Moved at greater strength: `checklist-list-ag-polish`'s "34–36 px node inside a 44 px target" becomes "the node is the target (≥ 44 px) and equals the path's node at the same width" (measured by switching views), with new checks of the tab (from the node's right-centre, level with it; white words, then dark words on the node's colour) and the banner's parts.
- **K13 — The celebration is the owner's video (owner Part 7, amendment 2).** The four SHIP files are in `apps/web/public/art/checklist/celebration/` exactly as delivered (MP4 1,438,289 bytes, WebM 1,607,707, first frame 90,112, last frame 71,962; not re-encoded). One constant, `CELEBRATION_CLIP` (`lib/checklist/celebration-clip.ts`): WebM (VP9) then MP4 (H.264 High 4.0, `avc1.640028`), the first frame as poster, the last frame as the still, 7.625 s, our words at 7.3 s — a future clip is a one-line swap. A pure `decideCelebration` (unit-tested) plays it only without reduced motion, Motion Off, Save-Data, a refused autoplay policy or an unplayable source; otherwise the last frame and the words show at once and nothing is fetched. The clip is never part of the first load: `primeCelebrationVideo` (components/checklist/celebration-clip.ts) creates one muted, inline, `preload=auto` element once the day is one item from complete and not yet celebrated today (a detached element loads in full: measured), and the celebration takes it, or makes one when it opens. It plays if ready within 1.5 s (`READY_MS`); otherwise, or if `play()` is refused or a source fails, the still and the words show and the clip is let go (sources removed), so it never starts late. It plays once and rests on its last frame (the still swapped in). On a phone it fills the screen (cover, the subject centred); from 641 px it stands at full height in the middle (9:16), its sides melting (a 12 % mask) into a 36 × 64 canvas copy of the same frames, scaled, blurred and darkened — drawn per frame only while it plays, then the still; no second decode. Our own words (the real count with "lit!" in the nebula gradient, the honest line or the Perfect-orbit line, the glass streak chip with the orange flame and the real streak, the nebula Continue) fade in over its lower part on a soft dark gradient; the baked-in words of the owner's original (reference 14) are never shown. No Undo while it is open: the shared toast gains `holdToast()` (hidden, its time standing still) and the toast comes back for its remaining time after Continue (AG Polish had moved the toast into the celebration).
