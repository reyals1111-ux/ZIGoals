# ADR-017: Session X-Local, "ZIGi comes alive"

Status: **In progress; implemented in Session X-Local on the owner's Mac (branch `feature/session-x-local-zigi`, base `main` `72ad872`), not merged or deployed at the time of writing.** It builds on [ADR-014](ADR-014-zigi-v2.md) (ZIGi v2) and [ADR-015](ADR-015-session-w.md). Session X-Cloud runs in parallel on `feature/session-x-cloud`; lanes and the handoff files are described below. The product record is [ZIGI_ALIVE_X.md](../product/ZIGI_ALIVE_X.md); the real-model evidence is [ZIGI_REAL_MODEL_TEST.md](../verification/x-local/ZIGI_REAL_MODEL_TEST.md).

## Context
- Studio-2 (2026-10-06) delivered real art for eleven of ZIGi's twenty-five states: per state a static 1×/2×/large WebP, a 96×126 animated WebP (≤400 kB) and an APNG fallback, plus a reference companion controller (`controller.mjs`) with its tests and a semantic-event schema. Studio-4 (from 2026-10-08) will deliver the same file names plus reactions R001–R013, so later updates must be a pure file swap.
- Session V's manifest v2 shows one placeholder frame for every state with a small CSS move each; the launcher shell never imports the manifest.
- The owner's goal for this session: ZIGi · your AI feels like a flawless premium feature, the real animated ZIGi is alive in the app with emotions matching what the person and the AI do, and everything is tested so the owner doesn't have to.

## Owner decisions (2026-10-07, plan approval)
- **D1.** Small 96×126 animated WebP only; the large 192×253 set waits for Studio-4. WebP only, no alpha video. Never re-encode or edit the studio's images.
- **D2.** Use the most fitting delivered animations as idle variations; F008 sleepy only after the existing inactivity rule.
- **D3.** Of the two PC model pulls, at least one is a tool-capable model from a family other than Qwen (model-agnostic proof).
- **D4.** Auto-accept: Health logging kinds are eligible only while the Health gate is open, opt-in per kind; weight and fasting stay manual; money, wealth, funding, staking and pre-fill kinds are never eligible.
- **D5.** Calm celebrations: celebrate/proud only for meaningful validated moments; ordinary check-ins get a small success at most; a daily cap on celebrations.
- **D6.** The golden set is never weakened: the 91 existing cases stay unchanged at 100 %; the deterministic CI subset grows to ≥200; the model-scored corpus lives beside it.
- **D7.** The emotion hint marker is stripped before display and before every stored or outbound path.
- **D8.** Home-GPU support (a person's own Ollama on their LAN) is research and a recommendation only; no CSP change in this session.

### 4. Emotions that match what happens (Part 4)
- **One semantic-event layer** (`components/zigi/semantic.ts`): the studio's 17 event names plus ZIGoals' own (`user_closed_panel`, `assistant_reading_records`, `assistant_writing_proposal`, `local_answer`, `ambiguity`, `not_understood`, `careful_topic`, `week_opened`, `reminder_due`, `model_loading`, `model_ready`, `all_habits_done`, `streak_milestone`, `goal_funded`, `challenge_milestone`, `meditation_finished`, `first_sleep_logged`, `card_accepted`, and the six `hint_*`). Only `{type}` crosses (the studio's privacy boundary): no text, records, values, notes, chat, tokens or identifiers; anything else is refused without being echoed. The signal bus in the shell (`bus.ts` `zigiSignals`) carries `{type, validated}`; the controller in the alive chunk turns an allowed signal into one of the machine's events (`EVENT_TO_MACHINE`), so `transition()` and its tests stay as they were.
- **The controller** (`ZigiController`, the port of `controller.mjs`, state in private fields): success events play only when the host validated them (`emitValidated` after the store confirmed the write; a plain signal → `needs_host_validation`); per-state cooldowns, priorities and interruptibility from `components/zigi/actions.json` (the studio's values for the eleven delivered states, ZIGoals' own for the fourteen worn ones); priority holds for one-shots that settle first; loops yield to the host; markers fire once per play (`markerFired`); a replay never resets a cooldown; one proactive nudge (the knock) per session, none after `user_dismissed`, none in the quiet hours (22:00–08:00), none while typing, none on a sensitive screen; the reaction rate limit (≥8 s between non-conversational reactions; listening, thinking, speaking, reading, writing, loading, offline, sleepy, greeting, goodbye and peek are the conversation's flow and never wait); sensitive screens refuse celebrate, proud, attention, reminder and peek.
- **Calm celebrations (D5, S17):** celebrate or proud only for `all_habits_done`, `streak_milestone` (proud), `goal_milestone_reached`, `goal_funded`, `challenge_milestone`, `meditation_finished`, `first_sleep_logged`; `habit_completed`, `health_log_recorded` and `card_accepted` are a small success (F012); at most 3 celebrations a day, then a small success (`celebration_cap`); a new day starts over.
- **The app's validated facts** (each signalled after its store confirmed the write): the Habits check-in (`lib/habits-v2/zigi-facts.ts`: a streak milestone the engine counted, the day's last scheduled habit done once a day through `zigoals:celebrations:v1`, or a plain completion), a goal milestone reached or done (once per milestone), a goal funded, a finished challenge (once), a finished meditation session and a manual entry, the first night logged for its wake day (else a small success), water, a meal, a weight. Accepted cards: a streak milestone (proud) or a small success (Session V celebrated every card; changed under D5).
- **The AI's emotion hint (S18):** one trailing `⟦zigi: <mood>⟧` (or `[[zigi: <mood>]]`) per reply from `insight|curious|encouraging|empathetic|surprised|confused` (`lib/ai/emotion-hint.ts`). Read and stripped at `finish`, before the turn is stored or shown; stripped again defensively in the history sent back to a model (`messagesFor`, which "Continue in my AI" uses), in "Copy as Markdown" and in the streaming draft. A hint is ignored when the reply has proposals (presenting) or careful mode is on (empathetic); the controller never lets a hint celebrate or knock. The context pack holds no chat text; push labels hold habit names; "Actions by ZIGi" holds planner titles.
- **Outside the chat:** the alive chunk runs the controller and the machine on every app page, the launcher shows the state, and the typing guard comes from keydown in any text field. The knock asks the controller (`nudgeAllowed`) before it counts a knock, signals `reminder_due` when it shows, and `user_dismissed` when closed with × or "Not today".
- **Meet ZIGi:** every state carries its plain-words meaning and when it shows.
- Tests: `lib/ai/zigi-semantic.test.ts` (the studio's nine tests ported, the mapping onto the manifest, calm celebrations, the cap, the rate limit, sensitive screens, the hints, typing and dismissal), `lib/ai/emotion-hint.test.ts` (extracting, stripping, every path), `lib/habits-v2/zigi-facts.test.ts`, `zigi-events.test.ts` (the two new transitions), and `tests/zigi-emotions.spec.ts` on real pages.

### 5a. Create anything by chat (Part 5a)
- **Six new card kinds**, each through the page's own mutator, with Edit for plain fields and an exact Undo: `stack-habit` (the habit editor's "stack after"; either habit may be one the same reply creates, "new1", and the write waits for its card), `edit-habit` (title, type, measurement, target, schedule, time of day, description, category; from today through `editHabit`, exactly as the editor's save; Undo restores the previous habit object), `edit-goal` (name, target, target date (or `null` to remove it), notes, category: the Goals detail form's own fields; refused for a locked, closed or simulation goal, and for a project's target), `log-mood` (the evening wrap-up's 1–5 answer with a note, under Health's `moods` group; the first mood ever is removed again by Undo, group and version included), `add-link` (My links: https only, the icon suggested from the host, a twin address refused), `add-widget` (Today's widgets through `saveWidget`: a goal or habit by handle, every other catalogue kind by metric; the kinds that need a record chosen on Today refuse; a duplicate binding is refused by the catalogue's own rule).
- **The four goal types.** `create-goal` and `plan-goal` accept `REWARD` and `PROJECT`. A project is the wizard's shape (target "1", 0 decimals, at least one milestone, no amount); a reward goal is an asset quantity like `QUANTITY`. The schema insists per type (`goalRules`), so a project without milestones or a value goal without a target is refused before any card.
- **Corrections.** A block may carry `"revise": true`; the parser strips it (never a card field) and flags the reply; the chat marks the previous reply's still-pending cards "Replaced by the next reply" (added, undone or dismissed cards keep their state). The protocol tells the AI to use it when the person corrects a proposal, and that "plan my week" is several cards.
- **Today's settings are a proposal store.** `Stores.settings` (`zigoals:settings:v1`) joins the runner through the same `usePrivateStore` Today and Settings use. Nothing else about the settings record is reachable from a card.
- **Accepted means correct (owner addition 4).** `tests/zigi-accept-correct.spec.ts`: every writing kind through the real panel against a MOCK server, the stored record checked field by field (units, dates, in-zone clock times), then Undo compared store by store with the pre-accept snapshot. Three zones: UTC, Europe/Brussels and Asia/Tokyo (already the next day at the test's moment). What Undo may leave behind, by the app's own paths: `updatedAt` stamps, the water operation ledger, an empty Session W group (`sleep`, `meditation`, `moods`, `links`) after the group's own remove, and a raised module version; the records themselves are byte-identical. The two pre-fill kinds are proven in `your-ai.spec.ts` (the form opens filled in, nothing saved).

### 5b. Auto-accept ([TIER 3] storage: ai options, Part 5b)
- **The record.** `zigoals:ai-options:v1` gains `autoAccept: {kinds: {<kind>: true}, dailyCap, days: {<day>: n}}` (loose, optional; absent means off; older builds ignore it; rollback = revert, the field stays inert). Only booleans and integers: never a value, a name or a note.
- **Eligibility (owner addition 5, `lib/ai/actions/auto-accept.ts`).** Opt-in per kind. Never: `log-weight`, `start-fast`, `stop-fast`, `prefill-holding`, `update-account-balance` (no switch exists for them). The twelve Health logging kinds (`log-food` incl. photo meals, `create-food`, `create-recipe`, `plan-meal`, `grocery-item`, `log-water`, `counter`, `log-sleep`, `log-meditation`, `log-mood`, `log-steps`, `log-measurement`) are eligible only while the Health gate is open at the moment the cards arrive: the chat passes the page's own consent (`context.consent.health`, fail-closed), never a stored flag; Settings greys their switches with the reason while the gate is closed. Every other writing kind is eligible without Health. A daily cap (default 20, 1–100) counts every automatic addition, cards of one reply included.
- **Behaviour.** Once the records are ready, the cards whose kind passes are added by ZIGi in order through the same `runner.apply` as a tap (so the write path, the undo and Activity's "Actions by ZIGi" are the ones a tap uses; the Activity line says "Added by ZIGi (auto-accept)"), each counted toward the cap; they show "Added by ZIGi (auto-accept)" and a toast "Added by ZIGi: …" with one Undo for ten seconds; the rest of the reply stays proposals; a cap hit says so under the cards. A pre-fill is never automatic. The controller receives one validated `card_accepted` (a small success, never a celebration).
- **Tests.** `lib/ai/actions/auto-accept.test.ts` (the never list, the Health list, the cap, pruning, bounds, the schema), a `privacy-cross-path.test.ts` block (with the gate closed no Health kind is ever automatic however it is switched on; the record carries no value), `tests/zigi-auto-accept.spec.ts` (water automatic with Health shared and the weight waiting, Undo exact, Activity's line; Health not shared: proposal only and Settings greyed with the reason; a habit kind without Health; the cap; the switch in Settings is what the chat obeys).

### 5c. Robustness on every wire (Part 5c)
- **Almost-JSON is repaired, never widened.** `repairJson` in `lib/ai/actions/parse.ts`: strict `JSON.parse` first; then one string-aware walk that drops comments, turns single quotes into double, quotes bare keys, maps Python's `True`/`False`/`None`, drops a trailing comma, and finally tries the text as a bare list or with a missing closing brace. Quoted numbers in numeric fields (`"300"`, `"2,5"`) become numbers, `None` in a numeric field becomes absence, kinds are case- and space-insensitive, a day written `Today` or `2026/10/8` is the same day. Every repaired block is still held to the whitelist schema, so a repair cannot create a card the schema refuses. An empty block stays "not valid JSON".
- **One automatic repair round.** In log or plan mode, when a reply held proposal blocks and every one was refused, the chat asks the same model once more in the same request flow (the first answer travels as the assistant's turn, the ask as a user turn; neither is stored) and shows the second answer under the note "_ZIGi asked your AI once more for valid cards (one automatic retry)._"; if the retry fails or returns nothing, the first answer stands. Never when any card survived, never after a stop, at most once per message (`lib/ai/actions/repair.ts`).
- **A stall watchdog.** `lib/ai/sse.ts` races every read against a 60 s timer (`STALL_MS`); a quiet stream is cancelled and the read ends with a `stalled` AiError (the rejection settles before the cancel, so a cancel's "done" can never win). The chat keeps what arrived as the reply with "Interrupted: Your AI sent nothing for 60 seconds…", shows the honest steps (local models that stop mid-reply are often swapping) and offers **Ask ZIGi to continue**, which sends "Continue from where you stopped." as the person's next message. Stop and regenerate are unchanged and tested.
- **The budget is question-aware** (`fitToBudget`): the latest question is never dropped, even past the budget; earlier turns go oldest first; a reply never leads. Tested in `lib/ai/context/budget.test.ts`.

### 6b. The corpus and the golden set (Part 6b, first half)
- **The golden set grows from 91 to 272 cases** (the 91 unchanged, byte for byte: D6/S19); every case deterministic, 100 % in CI. The new cases: more lookups (typos, capitals, other phrasings, every Session W area), ambiguity and the unknown (never a zero: a counter that exists reads its true count; a thing never recorded gets no answer), other languages (range words understood, whole questions handed to the AI), Health closed for the new areas, logging/advice/planning/settings/money never answered on the device, every card kind through the parser with aliases, fence variants, arrays and wrapped objects, refused shapes (a future day, a wrong unit, a bad handle, two amounts, an empty recipe, an unclosed fence), injection inside notes, recipe names, imported lines and link labels, money shapes (withdraw, allocate, swap, sign, set-price) refused, careful mode in German and Dutch and the everyday phrases that must not match.
- **Engine gaps the new cases found, fixed in the code that decides** (not the cases): a question about a range still ahead ("next month", "tomorrow", nl/fr/de) was answered with today's range because the refused range was dropped and the default stood in; it is now refused in words. "How much debt do I have", "list my accounts", "show my debts" and a bare "net worth?" were not recognised as lookups. "How is my Japan goal doing?" is a progress question when a goal is named ("How is my fasting going?" stays an open question for the AI, T's own rule). ## Rejected options
- **Renaming the studio's files to lowercase** to keep the brand test's character class: Studio-4 ships the same names and a swap must stay a copy; the test's reason (percent-encoding) does not apply to letters.
- **Keeping the placeholder frames as the base frame:** every fallback chain now ends in the idle art, and nothing outside the lane referenced them (repo-wide grep, Part 0).
- **`<picture>` with a `type` source for the APNG fallback:** a per-page probe is one decision, testable, and never downloads both files.
- **Shipping the manifest in the launcher shell** so the launcher could pick its own files: the shell stays the size it was; the frames arrive as data on the bus from a lazy chunk.
- **Guessing reaction semantics** (a default R001–R013 → event table): a reaction with no name never plays; names come from the delivery's `reactions.json` or the owner (Part 2, S11).
- **Committing the Studio-2 clips as the importer's fixture:** 6.9 MB of binaries in `scripts/` for a parser test; the synthetic fixture is 70 kB and decodes as real images.
- **A synthetic user turn for the automatic repair round.** Rejected: the chat would show words the person never typed. The retry travels inside the same request and only the labelled second answer is stored (S31).
- **Byte identity of whole modules after Undo.** Rejected: the forms' own save paths append ledgers (`goalHistory`, `planRevisions`, `lifecycle`) and leave empty groups; matching that would mean changing those paths, outside ZIGi's lane. The records themselves are byte-identical and the traces are named (S26).
- **A stored "Health gate" flag for auto-accept.** Rejected: a flag can go stale after the gate closes; the chat passes the page's consent of the moment (S27).
- **Mapping a tool's name sent as a card kind (`water`, `list_goals`) to a lookup.** Rejected: a lookup is never a card; the block stays refused and the model's miss is recorded (S34).
