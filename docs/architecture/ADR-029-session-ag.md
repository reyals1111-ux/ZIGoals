## Decisions taken without asking
- **I1 — The engine stays; the orbit look is redrawn.** Ticking, Undo, the stepper and panel, the keyboard, landing on the next planet, "Jump to next", ZIGi's placement and the measured geometry (`data-path-point`, `data-settled`) already live in `components/path/path.tsx` and are pinned by tests. Only the `variant="orbit"` drawing changes (the Checklist alone imports it), so Earn's Start-here path and the Academy (`variant="default"`) cannot move. The new pieces — the node, the glyph table, the flowing line's geometry and SVG, the per-item colours — live in `components/orbit-path/` so S1 can align Earn's path with them later (AF12).
- **I2 — Class names kept.** The redrawn node keeps `.path-dot`, `.path-label`, `data-path-point` and the aria names, so the layout audit, theme parity and the a11y tools read the new design without changes to their selectors.
- **I3 — Per-item display colours are presentation only.** A starred habit takes its habit card's tone (`visualTone`), anything else its stored or page colour, else a stable hash into the palette; a fix-up pass then makes every neighbour on the path differ. The stored `colour` is never rewritten (non-negotiable 2).
- **I4 — The Checklist's game-layer XP stops at the source** (`lib/game/xp.ts` emits no Checklist events); the First orbit, Seven orbits and Perfect orbit badges still read `checklistOrbitDays`. XP is derived from stored days, so no stored data changes; a person's XP total and level can drop slightly (owner-approved). Labelled `TIER 3 (game-layer data)`; rollback: revert.
- **I5 — No reference pixels.** Every shape (nodes, lines, glyphs, cards, the heatmap) is SVG or CSS; the only image is the app's existing destination planet art.
- **I6 — Parts 1–3 in one commit.** The nodes, the line and the orbit labels share the engine's one orbit sheet (`components/path/path-orbit.css`, rewritten) and its render; splitting them would leave commits that do not build a coherent page. The chests left the path in the same commit (the new line runs where they stood); their XP and every other XP word leave with Part 6.
- **I7 — How the line attaches.** A node takes the line in on its name's side and lets it out on the other, both at the ring's side point heading straight down (tangent to the ring); the name tab sits in the free lower quadrant on the in side. Between two nodes on the same side the line bows outward past both rims; across sides it swings through the middle. Amplitude, turning height and direction are seeded per stretch from the two ids (FNV-1a, mulberry32), never `Math.random`. The geometry is pure (`lib/path/flow-geometry.ts`, unit-tested: tangency, determinism, the column's width at 320 px).
- **I8 — Theme parity mechanics.** The node's glass and halo are `:root` tokens named so the theme builder keeps them (`--orbit-hue-disc-*`, `--orbit-hue-rim`); the tab carries its node's colour token itself (a custom property resolves where it is declared, and the island restores Nebula's tokens only inside it); the ring is a colour token with `!important` (the sheets carry the shared dot's `border: 0` as a stronger colour reset, and copy only a border's colour). `.orbit-node`, `.orbit-tab` and `.orbit-flow` join the islands.
- **I9 — The finale's ZIGi stands still.** At the day's end ZIGi stands beside the finale on the side it stood beside the last planet, placed by the sheet (no glide across the finale, which had turned it away from its planet for a few frames), 18 px clear of the finale's name.
- **I10 — Tests moved, not weakened (each listed in STATUS).** Planet sizes → node sizes; fourteen sparks and a comet → the ring's bloom, the tab's fill and check, and a light along the stretch to the next node (recorded as they appear); "a name beside, no box" → "a tab attached to its node, on its lower side, toward its side, one line / two on a phone, the full name in its title"; the next name's glide → it no longer moves (greater strength); "a long name stays whole" → two lines at most with a clean ellipsis and the full name in its title (the owner's AF15 wording); chest placement → no chest on the path; the line "centre to centre" → "side point to side point, tangent"; ZIGi's size bounds halved (owner Part 6), its clearance read from the node's 6 px halo.
