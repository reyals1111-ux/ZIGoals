## Summary

Scope and method. Read on origin/main (4f92495): FEATURE_GAP_MATRIX, MARKET_LANDSCAPE §6, BETA_IDEAS_BACKLOG (top 20), MONETIZATION_OPTIONS, OWNER_UI_REQUESTS_AB, the Session AE STATUS entry, and the code behind the three candidate features (life-review engine, share-image, weekly-review, reminders, reminders-plan, fasting, planner, game levels/freeze, celebrations, Journey). Then 22 apps were scanned on 2026-10-18 (18:40–19:15 UTC) with official pages first; where a site blocks automated reads (WHOOP, Delta, Monarch, Revolut, Finch help, Habitica wiki: 403; Duolingo/Rabby: JS-only shells) the claim is marked "reported" from an official page surfaced by search or a reputable secondary source, never invented. No prices were estimated; unknown is unknown.

What people pay for that ZIGoals lacks (features, not pricing). Across the 22 apps the paid tiers cluster in six things: (1) time-based planning — Todoist Pro's Calendar layout + Task duration + Deadlines, TickTick Premium's Timeline/calendar views and durations, Structured Pro's Recurring tasks, Calendar import and Replan; (2) recaps — Strava's Year in Sport and most of Month in Sport are now subscriber-only, Duolingo's Year in Review share card drives new users, Oura's Year in Review needs 60 days of data; (3) a living companion/record — Finch's bird (energy → adventure → growth, core free forever, Plus is cosmetics) and Forest's forest where even dead trees stay as a record; (4) wearable-derived scores (Oura, WHOOP) — out of reach by platform and partly by principle; (5) AI assistants (Oura Advisor, Headspace Ebb, Delta AI, Todoist Assist); (6) social/household (Monarch partner included, Strava Social Competition, Habitica parties). Items 1–3 are web-buildable from data already in the app; 4–6 are platform, principle or sync-gated.

The three features, confirmed with evidence. My Universe (confirm; strongest differentiation): no competitor renders four pillars as one living system; Finch and Forest prove the loop. Scores V4 D5 C4 Data5 R3. Rules to copy: growth never regresses (Finch, Journey's "Nothing is ever taken away"), a daily energy threshold rather than per-item rewards (Finch), misses leave a trace not a deletion (Forest), one celebration per milestone (celebrations.ts exists). Day Timeline (confirm with a scope cut): Structured's product and TickTick/Todoist paid tiers prove demand; but habits carry only a 4-value timeOfDay enum, meals have slots not clocks, and only reminders/bedtime/fasting-end carry HH:MM. Drag-to-move needs a new time field (Tier 3 storage) and a keyboard/tap alternative (Structured ships no drag on Web). Scores V4 D3 C4 Data3 R3. Stories (confirm; cheapest): the life-review engine already computes week/month highlights, wins, streaks, goals moved, health trends, money change and counts; share-image already draws 1080×1350 PNGs on device; only a 'year' period, the tap-through UI and per-card share are new. Scores V4 D4 C2 Data5 R2. Share cards must respect S3/LC§3 (no returns or prices).

Two alternates for plan review: Guided Journeys (Fabulous/Headspace-style multi-day programs assembled from existing habit and goal templates, Duolingo-path unlocking; V4 D3 C3 Data3 R3, LC§4 wording) and Focus Sessions that grow the Universe (Forest-style timed focus on a habit or goal, withered-but-kept record; V3 D2 C2 Data4 R1). Five optional quick wins: Replan at day end, a streak-at-risk evening card that offers an earned freeze, a sleep-regularity figure, share-per-section in the Life review, and a "jump to now" button on long Today/Checklist pages.

Caveat: there is no "Checklist" on origin/main (git grep returns nothing in apps/web); the Checklist-relevant mechanics are written for whatever Session AF defines it as.

### Pattern across the 22 (features, not prices)
1. **Time-based planning is the most common paid line** in planners: Todoist Pro (Calendar layout, Task duration, Deadlines), TickTick Premium (calendar views, Timeline, duration), Structured Pro (Recurring, Calendar import, Replan). Gap matrix already lists "calendar view, time blocking ✗".

### (1) My Universe — evidence and rules to copy
- Finch: energy from completed goals; a threshold event (adventure) once the day's energy is full; growth from the count of adventures; discoveries brought back; **core free forever, Plus = cosmetics** (reported from help center). → Universe: a daily "light" threshold per pillar rather than a reward per check-in; growth = cumulative counts (streak weeks, goals reached, health days, contribution days); a "discovery" can be a cross-pillar insight card (engine exists, `lib/insights/cross-pillar.ts`).

## 4. Checklist-relevant findings — concrete mechanics

**Caveat:** no Checklist exists on `origin/main`; these are written for a per-day ordered list on Today.

- **Duolingo path (verified):** one linear sequence; each node is one unit of work; completed nodes turn gold; units carry plain-language outcome headers ("get directions", not "City 3"); practice is interleaved as nodes rather than a separate tab; a guidebook per unit; a floating arrow returns you to your current spot. → Checklist: one ordered list with a single highlighted "current" item and a "you are here" marker; section headers phrased as outcomes ("Morning: wake, water, walk"); a "what counts" note per section; a return-to-now control when scrolled away.
- **Todoist lists (verified):** Smart Quick Add (natural language) on the free plan; sections and board/list layouts; Karma rewards completing *and* planning (due dates, labels, reminders); daily goal (default 5) and weekly goal (default 25); streaks that a vacation mode pauses without breaking; eight levels; week-by-week graphs are Pro; Pro adds Task duration and Deadlines. → Checklist: a daily count goal the person sets; a pause that does not break a streak (H1 exists); a duration on items so the Timeline can draw blocks; "planning counts" (adding an item to tomorrow earns the same credit as completing one, if XP is used).
- **Structured timeline (verified):** time on the left, items with start+duration, an all-day row for untimed items, an inbox for undated ones that can be checked off in place or dragged into a free slot; subtasks, notes, icon and colour per item; drag with a drop-time preview; drop on a day keeps the time; drag to top = all-day; Replan for leftovers (past-only, four actions, badge after three moves); drag absent on Web/Android. → Timeline: an "untimed" row above the clock for anytime habits; the Checklist is the inbox-equivalent; every move must also work by tap/keyboard; leftovers get a Replan, never a silent carry-over.
- **Finch pet (reported, official help via search + reviews):** goals fill daily energy; at full energy a threshold event (adventure) fires once; growth is a cumulative count of events through named stages; the pet returns with a discovery; nothing is lost on a missed day; core loop free forever, premium is cosmetics. → My Universe: per-pillar daily "light" that fills from the Checklist and fires one event at full; planet stages by cumulative counts; a "discovery" = one cross-pillar insight; misses leave a trace, never a loss; keep the whole loop free.
- **Forest (verified):** every session becomes an object; failed sessions remain as a record; the view is "a living record"; tags label sessions; Plant Together shares one outcome. → Universe/Checklist: a completed item leaves a permanent object; an abandoned timer leaves a withered one; the Journey view is the archive, not a leaderboard.
- **Strava Month in Sport (verified):** refresh on a fixed day with the previous recap gone; a Trophy Case picks ≤4 achievements by a priority order; a share icon per card; only the person sees it. → Stories: a fixed weekly/monthly refresh with a "save it before it rolls" note; a priority order for which facts become cards; per-card share.
