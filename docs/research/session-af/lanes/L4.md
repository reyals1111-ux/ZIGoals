## 3. Registry additions (exact)
### 3.1 `apps/web/lib/ai/actions/schema.ts`
New action kinds (all `z.strictObject`, handles/day rules as today):
- `checklist-check` `{item: text(100), done: z.boolean().default(true), day: daySchema}`
- `checklist-add` `{text: text(120), favorite: z.boolean().default(false), day: daySchema}`
- `checklist-favorite` `{item: text(100), favorite: z.boolean()}`
- `set-brief` `{morning?: boolean, evening?: boolean, eveningTime?: clock, earning?: boolean}` (a device write to `zigoals:zigi:v1.brief`)
- `prefill-staking` `{network: z.enum(['zig-test-2','zigchain-1']), action: z.enum(['delegate','undelegate','claim']), validator: text(120).optional(), amount: decimalString.optional()}` — **added to `PREFILL_KINDS`** (so it is not a `WRITING_KIND`) and to `AUTO_ACCEPT_NEVER`.
New composite: `plan-bundle` `{title: text(80), goal?: {...goalFields, milestones ≤6}, habits: [habitFields] ≤4, reminders: [{for:'habit', habit: name|ref, time: clock}] ≤4, checklist: [text(120)] ≤6, remember: [{text ≤500, category: REMEMBER_CATEGORIES}] ≤2, earn?: {path: z.enum(['learn','native-staking']), network: z.enum([...]).default('zig-test-2')}}` → `expandComposite` emits, in order: `create-goal`, `create-habit` (refs new1…), `create-reminder`, `checklist-add`, `remember`, then `open-page {page:'staking'}` (learn) or `prefill-staking` (native-staking, no amount). Every part carries `bundle: {id, title, index, total}`; the parser keeps the first 10 and reports the cut ("Only the first 10 steps of this plan are shown").
`KIND_ALIASES` += `tick:'checklist-check', 'mark-done':'checklist-check', 'check-off':'checklist-check', 'add-to-checklist':'checklist-add', 'checklist':'checklist-add', 'favourite-item':'checklist-favorite', 'plan_bundle':'plan-bundle', 'set-up':'plan-bundle', 'program':'plan-bundle', 'staking-review':'prefill-staking', 'start-staking':'prefill-staking', 'brief-settings':'set-brief'`. No alias for a bare `stake`, `unstake`, `claim`, `sign` (an unknown kind stays refused, as `unknown-kind` pins).
### 3.2 Planner: `apps/web/lib/ai/actions/plan-af.ts` (lazy chunk, `installAfPlanner/loadAfPlanner`, the `plan-aa.ts` pattern)
- `checklist-*` → `device` write through the registry (see 3.3); refused with "No checklist in this build yet; this is a habit? - `set-brief` → `device: {key: ZIGI_KEY, patch: {brief: {...}}}` (runner-effects gains `brief` beside `LOOK_KEYS`, merged like `knock`).
- `prefill-staking` → `{target:'form', card:{title:'Pre-fill the staking review', lines:['<action> · <network> · <validator?> · <amount?>', 'Opens the Staking page's review filled in; nothing is signed or sent until you do it there, in your own wallet']}, staking: StakingPrefill}`; on the public Alpha the card adds "On this Alpha the review shows practice money only (testnet)".

### 3.5 Device paths

## 5. The briefing assembler and when it fires
`dailyBrief(env, extras, when)` reuses: open habits (`habitDay`/`habitStats`), goals needing a step (`summaries(env)`: `nextContributionDate` ≤ 7 days, or an open milestone with a device date from `milestoneDates`), running timers (`habit.timer.state==='running'`, `runningNights(sleep)`, `runningSession(fasting)`, the meditation run record), today's meal plans (`HealthData.mealPlans` for `healthDay`, gate), water/steps (gate), notes of category `schedule`/`preferences` (only while `usingNotes`), `extras.checklist` (`{due, done, items ≤5}`), `extras.earning` (`{lines:[…], asOf}` or `unavailable`), plan bundles in progress (`zigi_plans`). Order: morning = checklist dots · habits due · meals planned · timers · goals needing a step · earning line · one note; evening = done/open counts · water, steps (gate) · meals logged · timers finished · steps taken on goals (contributions today) · checklist done/total · "one for tomorrow" (yesterday's wrap-up intention or the first open habit) · earning line. Calm tone (`toneProblem` test on every line); a section with nothing is left out; both empty → no card.
Fires: morning card on Today when `localClock(now)` ∈ [05:00, 12:00) and `brief.morning !== false`; evening card from `wrapUpTime(settings)` (default 18:00; the person's own time when the wrap-up is on) until the day is dismissed (`dismissFor(day,'recap')`); both via the existing probe pattern (made again only when records/gates change). Knock: a new `KnockKind 'recap'` candidate at the evening time under the existing rules (off by default, quiet hours, cap). Read aloud: a "Read aloud" button on each card and in the chat block, `useReadAloud` (never automatic, never in quiet hours; the mute chip applies).

## 6. Plans (agent mode): one reviewable bundle
"Set me up for a 10k", "plan my week", "start me on staking" → the model answers with one `plan-bundle` block (the `/plan` `PLAN_NOTE` and `ACTION_PROTOCOL` gain the bundle shape and three examples). The parser expands it; `proposal-list.tsx` renders a `BundleHeader` ("Your 10k plan · 6 steps", "Add all" = existing `applyAll` over `batchable` plans, form cards stay per-item, "Skip the rest"), each step the existing card with per-item Add/Edit/Dismiss and Undo. Accepting any step records the bundle in `zigoals:zigi-plans:v1` (`recordBundle`, `markItem`). Money stays pre-fill only: a bundle's Earn step is `open-page` or `prefill-staking`, never a contribution amount unless the person named one (then `prefill-contribution`, a form). A bundle may carry ≤2 `remember` cards (seed 2 delta).

## 7. Page awareness
`pageView(document, pathname)` collects `[data-module]`, `[data-card]`, `[data-layout-item]`, section `id`s and their visible headings (`aria-labelledby`/first `h2`), flags `inView` by IntersectionObserver, and the focused card (the one whose menu opened "Explain this card"). `use-ai-context.ts` refreshes it on route change and a 500 ms-debounced MutationObserver, and puts it on `ToolSources.pageView`. In the question context it is one removable chip "This page" (`QUESTION_HEADING` section "## On screen"): route + card ids/labels, ≤1,500 chars, no values. "Explain this card": `AskZigiItem` gains a generic branch (any card with an id) → `askZigi('Explain the <label> card', {tool:'page_view', args:{card:id}, label})`; the device answers from `CARD_HELP` when the id is known (LOCAL_LABEL), else the model with the chip. Privacy: on Settings or a sensitive screen `pageView` is null ("Paused on this private screen"); Health cards only through the gate.

## 8. Earn coach
Education on device (`earnGlossaryAnswer`, EN/NL), live options through `earn_options` (the Earn lane's `registerEarnSource`), the "Start here" walk as an `open-page {page:'staking'}` card plus the glossary, and the review hand-off `prefill-staking`. ZIGi never states an APR of its own: the tool text is the only source and the scorer's `mustNot` fails any percentage the tool text did not carry. Decoy warning: the glossary's "ZIG" entry says the real coin is the chain's native `azig` and that a factory token also called "ZIG" exists; match by denom, never by name (source: the Earn lane's registry; the sentence is labelled).

## Risks

- Cross-lane contracts: the Checklist record (zigoals:checklist:v1 and registerDeviceWrite), the wallet lane's Staking review (takeStakingPrefill) and the Earn lane's registerEarnSource must land before the integration commits; until then checklist commands fall back to habit check-ins, prefill-staking opens the Staking page with the stash unread, and earn_options answers 'unavailable' — all three states are tested so the fallback is honest, but the owner's full experience needs all lanes merged
- The plan-bundle shape is large; small or local models may emit malformed bundles; mitigated by the parser's per-part validation (a bad step is refused alone), aliases, the repair round for plan intents, and corpus-af cases that score the kind multiset
- Quick-command false positives in ordinary chat ('mark' or 'tick' used otherwise); the stop rule (whole-message match, no question mark, no second clause, no quantity, exactly one item) and golden negatives bound it; a wrong device card is still a card the person must add
- Model-stated rates: a model may write a percentage the tool text never carried; the tool text labels every figure and corpus-af scores mustNot '%' on glossary cases; the UI shows no number ZIGi computed
- Dutch recognition quality on the device and the Prompt API's missing 'nl' support: NL commands are device regexes, whole-Dutch lookups still go to the model (recorded)
- System prompt growth (new kinds and bundle examples) raises per-turn input tokens; stays under the 10,000-token default budget and Haiku's 100,000-token rate card, to be measured in the round
- Read-aloud and recap timing depend on the device clock and quiet hours; a recap at 18:00 while the wrap-up card is on may feel doubled — the recap links to the wrap-up form instead of repeating it
- The WebKit set gains five zigi-* specs (slower CI, the AE navigation-stall intermittent); every seed waits for networkidle as ed1d90f4 did
- zigi-guard scans by regex: a method name built at run time could pass; the test also forbids every wallet/cosmjs import under the ZIGi trees, which is the real boundary
- What's new release id is shared by every lane of Session AF; two lanes bumping it differently conflict — one integration commit owns the bump
- Memory delta: adding a new source enum value would make the memory record unreadable in older builds (strict z.enum inside the loose note) — kept at 'zigi' on purpose
- Earning summary on the public Alpha is practice money only (G16): a person expecting fiat sees 'unavailable' with the reason
