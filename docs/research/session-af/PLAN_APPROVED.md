Everything is additive, non-custodial, private by design and honest about numbers. The result is one giant PR on `feature/session-af-wealth-unlocked`, out of Draft with CI green by Sun 25 Oct 11:00 Brussels, Gate C from Sat 24 Oct 08:00.

Plan mode forbids writes, so two setup steps the owner listed first happen in the first minute of Phase B instead (section 1). Reference captures with Playwright are also deferred to Part 0 for the same reason; the research helpers described every public step in text instead.

### 11.8 Privacy changes (PRIVACY.md, a bold-led paragraph after **Linked wallets**, plus the Session AF row in THREAT_MODEL.md)

> **Earn, staking and wallets (Session AF, ADR-028).** When you link a wallet address or sign a staking transaction, your browser talks directly to public blockchain services; ZIGoals' own server never receives your address. Which service sees what: ZIGChain balances, stakes, rewards and votes go to `api.zigchain.com` or `testnet-api.zigchain.com` (and, when one is slow, to the public mirrors run by Keplr, Polkachu, NodeStake or Numia) — the operator can see your IP address and the public address you asked about. Cosmos Hub, Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, Optimism, Polygon, BNB Chain and Solana balances go to PublicNode (`*.publicnode.com`, which says it keeps no data beyond 24 hours); Bitcoin addresses go to `mempool.space` (which keeps web-server logs for 10 days). Prices come from ZIGoals' market coordinator as before and carry no address. The Earn hub's own numbers (rates, pools, proposals) are fetched by ZIGoals' server from the chain and DefiLlama without any personal data. When you sign, Keplr sends the signed transaction to the chain itself; ZIGoals only shows you what it contains first. Your linked addresses, goal-vault links, checklist, Earn progress and ZIGi memory stay on this device (in Export, never synced, never restored from a backup). Opening an on-ramp or an ecosystem app is a normal link in a new tab; ZIGoals sends no referrer, and the provider's own privacy policy applies from there.
