## Session 1 — wrap-up
### What works
Honest, calm copy ("nothing is saved until you finish", "Browser storage is not a backup", "Nothing is ever taken away"); every step skippable; final "When you finish" receipt; live "You'll see:" page summary; personalised starter habits/targets (Run A sleep+money vs Run B chess/water/reading differ visibly); "Tick your first habit ↓" nudge; empty Journey universe; 3 themes; mobile layout with More sheet; no payment/permission/notification prompt anywhere in onboarding; no pricing in wizard.
### Missing vs competitors (Habitica / Finch / Fabulous / Habitify / Streaks)
No name/avatar or pet/companion choice (Finch/Habitica), no "why are you here/commitment" quiz or science-based plan (Fabulous), no reminder-time step or notification permission ask (Habitify/Streaks all set a time in onboarding), no first-habit-ticked celebration moment (Finch/Streaks), no progress/level preview, no premium/pricing explanation (only Help: NOVA "Premium, free during the Alpha"), no NOVA mention (NB-022), no resume-draft after reload (NB-011), wizard not forced for new users (card dismissible, wizard only at /app/welcome).
### Copy / UX notes
Hero "Your financial orbit… Tomorrow's Wealth" shown to users who skipped Wealth; three competing first CTAs on empty Today; "Showcase… separate storage in this tab" is jargon; "Skip setup" vs "Continue without choosing" vs "Skip the tour" vs "Not now" — four different skip labels; Dark ≈ Nebula theme; light-theme toggles low contrast.
