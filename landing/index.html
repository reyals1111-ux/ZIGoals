08 PERSONAL BY DESIGN

Your plans
should remain
yours.

Know where your records live, what leaves your device, and the one thing only you keep.

AVAILABLE IN THE ALPHA

On your device.

Your Goals, Habits, Health and portfolio records stay in the browser on the device you use. No account is needed. What’s stored there isn’t encrypted and isn’t a backup, so lock your device.

AVAILABLE IN THE ALPHA

A copy you control.

Export your records or make an encrypted backup file with its own recovery secret. Including Health is your choice.

NOT ACTIVE YET · COMING WITH THE FRIENDS ALPHA

End-to-end encrypted sync.

After you sign in with a code sent to your email, your records are encrypted on your device before they leave it. The server stores encrypted copies and sees your account, which part of the app each record belongs to, record identifiers, sizes and times, not what you wrote. Health syncs only if you choose it.

NOT ACTIVE YET · COMING WITH THE FRIENDS ALPHA

A recovery secret only you hold.

Sync shows you a long recovery secret once. Keep it in a password manager. If you lose it and every device, nobody can recover the cloud copy, including us.

09 ON YOUR HOME SCREEN

Install it
like an app.

ZIGoals runs in your browser. Installed, it opens from its own icon, and on an iPhone it keeps its own data, separate from Safari.

  1. iPhone

    Install first, then use the icon.

    In Safari, open alpha.zigoals.app/app, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. The Home Screen app keeps its own data, so start there.

  2. Mac

    Add it to the Dock.

    In Safari, open alpha.zigoals.app/app and choose File, then Add to Dock.

  3. Chrome, Edge or Android

    Install from the browser.

    Open alpha.zigoals.app/app and choose Install in the address bar or the browser menu.

12 A LITTLE CLARITY

Good questions.
Clear answers.

Start with the picture as it is today.

What is ZIGoals?+

ZIGoals connects Goals, Habits, Health, Wealth, Markets and Today in one personal system. Its public Alpha combines private tracking, a local financial simulation, and read-only market and position context.

How do I get an invite to the friends Alpha?+

Write to contact@zigoals.app; the Request an invite button opens that email for you. We reply by email, and invites go to a small group of friends first. The public Alpha stays open to everyone in the meantime.

What can I do in the Alpha today?+

Create and track Goals, build Habit routines, keep a Health journal, track your Wealth, follow Markets, and customize Today. You can also load a separate fictional Showcase from Settings. No wallet or account is needed for Local Demo.

How do the different parts connect?+

Goals describe your destinations. Habits support consistent action. Health keeps your own wellbeing records in view. Wealth and Positions put tracked resources in the context of your Goals. Today brings the relevant next steps together.

Does ZIGoals move my money?+

No financial execution is enabled in the public Alpha. The local simulation uses fictional balances. Goal allocations and tracked positions do not move funds, and financial signing and broadcasting remain disabled.

Is ZIGoals live on mainnet?+

Mainnet financial execution is disabled. The Goal Manager contract is not deployed. The app can offer mainnet read-only / watch-only position context; that is observation, not a deployed financial service.

Where are my records stored?+

In your browser, on the device you use. That storage is not encrypted and is not a backup, so lock your device and keep a copy of what matters. Encrypted backup files are available today; end-to-end encrypted sync is built but not active yet.

What does end-to-end encrypted sync mean?+

Your records are encrypted on your device before they leave it, and only your devices can unlock them. The server stores encrypted copies and sees your account, which part of the app each record belongs to, record identifiers, sizes and times, not what you wrote. That holds as long as the app code we serve has not been tampered with, and anyone holding one of your remembered devices can open your records too. Health syncs only if you choose it. You keep a recovery secret: if you lose it and every device, nobody can recover the cloud copy, including us. Sync isn’t active yet; it comes with the friends Alpha.

Does ZIGoals provide investment advice?+

No. It is a planning and tracking tool, not an investment adviser or financial manager. Scenarios are illustrative; they do not guarantee returns. Funding plans assume no future investment return.

Does ZIGoals provide medical advice?+

No. Health is a personal journal for records and targets you choose. It does not diagnose, treat, or recommend medical care.

How do I install ZIGoals on my phone or computer?+

On an iPhone, open it in Safari and tap Share, then Add to Home Screen; install first, because the Home Screen app keeps its own data. On a Mac, use Safari’s File, then Add to Dock. In Chrome, Edge or on Android, choose Install in the address bar or menu. Until sync opens, records stay on the device where you entered them. See the steps.

Is ZIGoals affiliated with ZIGChain?+

No. ZIGoals is an independent project. Ecosystem research and references do not imply official affiliation, a partnership, endorsement, or a security audit.

Where can I follow development or see the source?+

Follow ZIGoals on X, the creator’s build updates, or the public GitHub project for current status and source code.
