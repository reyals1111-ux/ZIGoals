# Session AM: NOVA references (owner, 2026-11-11)

Reference material for the NOVA Dashboard redesign. Inspiration only: copy no image, layout or label 1:1.
The ZIGoals design language (cosmic/nebula, pillar accents, readability rules) always wins over these images.

## `grok/`: 43 owner-made mockups (Grok)

AI-generated by the owner for this session. They show the **look of holographic data widgets**: thin neon strokes,
glass panels with cut corners, glowing rings, bars and lines, small caps labels, dark backgrounds, cyan + magenta + violet.
**The words inside them are placeholders** ("system alert", "CPU", "network", city names, numbers): ignore them.
NOVA shows the person's own data with honest sources and as-of times. Seven owner mockups with a fictional-AI brand name
were left out on purpose; never use that name or any film/comic reference in the app.

## Owner screenshots 1–4 (described in words; the images are not licensed for a public repo)

1. **Glass panel wall.** Three tall translucent glass slabs float side by side above a dark reflective desk. Each slab has a
   thin cyan or magenta outline and small corner ticks. Left: a magenta bar histogram shaped like a soft wave, a glowing cyan
   crystal cube with small ring gauges and toggle chips. Centre: a large magenta mountain-shaped line chart over a fine grid,
   small chips above it, a faceted violet gem. Right: a cyan crystal object, a small ring gauge and a cluster of tall thin
   neon bar columns. On the desk below, low-poly crystal sculptures on small glowing pedestals reflect cyan and pink light.
   *Take from it:* panels as floating glass with depth and reflection, data drawn as light, 3D crystal objects as markers.
2. **Command screen.** A dark plum screen with one large concentric radial gauge in the centre (pink → violet → orange
   arcs, segmented rings, a soft glow) as the hero. Left of it, a column of small ring icons each with a horizontal progress
   bar. Right, a dotted world map with glowing pulsing nodes joined by thin lines. Two small circular dials top right.
   Along the bottom, a strip of five thin-bordered mini panels (small line charts, thin progress lines, round toggles).
   *Take from it:* one hero in the centre, satellites around it, a strip of mini cards, information density without clutter.
3. **Nebula vortex.** Seen from inside a nebula: violet and peach/salmon gas clouds spiral into a bright white-violet core;
   stars stream toward the viewer, giving depth and slow forward motion.
   *Take from it:* NOVA's centre node as a living spiral with a bright core and particles.
4. **Planet at a galactic core.** A purple/pink/peach dust band crosses the frame diagonally; a planet sits in front of
   the bright core, rim-lit from behind; one thin horizontal blue light streak crosses the whole image; deep blue-violet
   starfield.
   *Take from it:* the centre as a star system / planet / black-hole orbit with rim light and a thin light streak.
